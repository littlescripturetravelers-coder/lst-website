Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026

Little Scripture Travelers (“we,” “us,” or “our”) makes a faith-based bedtime story service for families — available on our website and in our iPhone and Android apps. We care about your family’s privacy, and we keep what we collect small and simple. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your first name, email address, and a password (stored securely in encrypted/hashed form) when you create an account.
  • Content you create — the family stories and notes you choose to save in the app.
  • Notification token — if you turn on notifications, a device token so we can send your morning story reminder.
  • Basic technical data — standard information needed to operate the app reliably (such as sign-in status and app diagnostics).

We do not collect payment card details. Subscriptions are handled by our payment provider (see below), and card information goes directly to them — never to us.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and subscription.
  • To deliver the daily scripture story, lullabies, and songs inside the app.
  • To send your morning story notification, if you’ve enabled notifications.
  • To send the daily story email, if you are a subscriber and have opted in.
  • To keep the service secure and to improve it.

Service providers we use

We share the minimum information necessary with trusted providers who help us run the service:

  • Supabase — secure hosting for your account and saved content.
  • Payhip — processes subscription payments and free trials (handles card details directly).
  • Mailchimp — sends the daily story email to subscribers.
  • Apple / Google — deliver push notifications to your device.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Your choices and data deletion

You are in control of your information:

  • Delete your account any time — in the app, go to Account → Delete My Account. This permanently removes your account and your saved stories.
  • You can also email us at littlescripturetravelers@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information.
  • You can turn notifications on or off at any time in your device settings.
  • Subscribers can unsubscribe from the daily email using the link at the bottom of any email.

Children’s privacy

Little Scripture Travelers is designed to be set up and managed by a parent or guardian and enjoyed together with young children. The app is directed to parents, not to children for independent use, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Your information is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and stored with reputable providers. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard safeguards.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Email us any time at littlescripturetravelers@gmail.com.
