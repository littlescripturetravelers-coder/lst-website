Tonight's Lullaby  ★  Week 1 · Night 5

You Made Me Just Right

from tonight's story You Know Me! · with Wren the Wonderer
Common meter (8-6-8-6) — it also sings to any hymn tune you already know.
Verse 1

You know me when I sit to rest,
You know me when I stand;
You know each word before I speak,
And hold me in Your hand.

Chorus

Heavenly Father, You made me,
You know me through and through;
From wiggly toes to thumping heart,
I'm wonderful to You.

Verse 2

If I climb up the highest hill,
Or sail the farthest sea,
Wherever I may go tonight,
You're right there watching me.

Chorus

(repeat chorus)

Verse 3

You made my fingers, made my smile,
You made my giggle too;
Each part was made with tender care
By loving hands, by You.

Chorus

(repeat chorus)

Verse 4

So now I close my sleepy eyes,
I'm precious, loved, and known;
You made me just exactly right,
And I am all Your own.

Chorus

(repeat chorus)
