Tonight's Lullaby  ★  Week 1 · Night 4

A Lamp for My Little Feet

from tonight's story A Lamp for My Feet · with Wren the Wonderer
Common meter (8-6-8-6) — it also sings to any hymn tune you already know.
Verse 1

The night is dark, the path is long,
But I am not afraid,
For Heavenly Father's loving words
Are like a lamp He made.

Chorus

A lamp for my feet, a light for my way,
Shine on the path I go;
I'll keep Your words safe in my heart
So I will always know.

Verse 2

The song-writer, he loved them so,
He sang them soft and sweet:
"Your words are like a shining lamp
That glows before my feet."

Chorus

(repeat chorus)

Verse 3

He kept them like a treasure hid
Deep in his heart to stay,
And when he didn't know what's right
They showed him the safe way.

Chorus

(repeat chorus)

Verse 4

So close your eyes, my little one,
And hold your lamp up high;
The stories that you heard tonight
Will light you by and by.

Chorus

(repeat chorus)
