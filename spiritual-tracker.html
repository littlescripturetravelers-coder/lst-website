Nightly Spiritual Growth Tracker

28 nights. One habit at a time. Track your family's bedtime scripture routine and watch it become second nature.

✦

Habits aren't built in a single night. They're built in twenty-eight of them — ordinary, quiet, repeated moments that slowly become the rhythm your child can't imagine going to sleep without. This tracker gives you a simple way to celebrate every night you showed up.

“In coming days, it will not be possible to survive spiritually without the guiding, directing, comforting, and constant influence of the Holy Ghost. My beloved brothers and sisters, I plead with you to increase your spiritual capacity to receive revelation.”

— President Russell M. Nelson

This tracker is your tool for answering that plea — one bedtime at a time.

How to Use This Tracker

Step 1

Come Back Each Night

Each night after your Lantern bedtime routine, come back to this page — or print it out and keep it by the bed.

Step 2

Check What You Did

Check the boxes for what you completed tonight: Story, Lullaby, Parent Testimony, Prayer Together.

Step 3

Watch the Streak Grow

After 28 nights, celebrate — you've built something that lasts. Your child is carrying this forever.
