# Child-checked story words

One file per night, named by the night's slug. When the nightly build finds a file here for the night it is building, the `p1` (three paragraphs, first story page) and `p2` (two paragraphs, second story page) replace the PDF's story paragraphs everywhere: the story book, and the story card of both editions (Christian reads God). `old_p1`/`old_p2` are the PDF's words, kept so the printed book can be matched. See HANDOFF §40 (the Child's Story Rule).

- `score` (Levi, 29 Oct 2026): the story's rating on the app repo's docs/STORY-PROMPT.md rubric. A file with a score of 9 or 10 is not rated again by the publisher's guard rail, so the night goes out exactly as written. All 35 November nights (Morning by Morning) are here; the printed book and the app's Books pages carry the same words.

- December (Levi, 29 Oct 2026): all 28 nights of Book 12 (We Have Waited for Him) are here, written the picture-book way and rated 9-10 by a reviewer that did not write them. `p2_christian`, when present (nights 10, 15, 21), is the Christian edition's second story page, used in place of `p2` because that night's p2 is Latter-day Saint teaching.

- The published nights, June 1 - Oct 28 (Levi, 29 Oct 2026: "replace the scripture stories that are in the Morning lanterns that have been published with the new ones"; asked and chosen: swap the words where the night tells the same scripture): 104 of those nights took the story words of the rewritten Old Testament books (`from` names the book's night). Their folders were changed in place (story book and both editions' story card), and these files keep the guard rail from rewriting them. `p1_christian`/`p2_christian`, when present, are the Christian edition's words where the book's words are Latter-day Saint teaching. `old` is the words the night carried before.
