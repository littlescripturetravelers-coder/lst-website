Tonight's Lullaby  ★  Week 2 · Night 8

Hold His Hand and Take a Step

from tonight's story Trust Him to Lead the Way · with Beau the Brave
Common meter (8-6-8-6) — it also sings to any hymn tune you already know.
Verse 1

The path winds up a great big hill,
I cannot see the top;
But Heav'nly Father holds my hand,
So I don't have to stop.

Chorus

I'll trust Him with all of my heart,
He knows the way to go;
I'll hold His hand and take a step,
Then one more, soft and slow.

Verse 2

Wise Solomon wrote long ago,
"Don't lean on what you know;
Just ask, and He will show the way,
The best way you can go."

Chorus

(repeat chorus)

Verse 3

When I don't know which way to go,
I whisper, "Please lead me";
He lights up one more little step,
That's all I need to see.

Chorus

(repeat chorus)

Verse 4

So close your eyes, my little one,
The way is safe and true;
He knows the road when you can't see,
And He is leading you.

Chorus

(repeat chorus)
