Tonight's Lullaby  ★  Week 1 · Night 6

You Are a Gift

from tonight's story Children Are a Gift · with Willow the Warm
Common meter (8-6-8-6) — it also sings to any hymn tune you already know.
Verse 1

Who builds a house that's strong and warm?
Not hammer, brick, or nail;
Heavenly Father's gentle hands
That never, ever fail.

Chorus

A gift, a gift, that's what you are,
A present from above;
He gave you to our family
To fill our home with love.

Verse 2

The song-writer sang long ago
About a table spread,
With little ones like growing plants
Around it, loved and fed.

Chorus

(repeat chorus)

Verse 3

So reach your arms out big and wide,
And squeeze them in real tight;
One family hug, warm and safe,
Goes with you through the night.

Chorus

(repeat chorus)

Verse 4

Now close your eyes, my little one,
Say "thank You" for each face;
Heavenly Father built our home,
A warm and loving place.

Chorus

(repeat chorus)
