I Will Love Them Freely
The prophet Hosea’s people kept wandering away from Heavenly Father. Wandering away means not following Him anymore.
But Heavenly Father made a kind promise. He said, “I will heal their wandering away. I will love them freely.”
Freely means it is a gift. You do not have to earn it. You never have to pay it back.
He said, “I will be like the dew to Israel.” Dew is tiny drops of water on morning grass. In that dry land, dew helped plants live. He said His people would grow like a lily, a pretty flower. Their roots would grow deep, like tall trees.
Heavenly Father loves you freely too, just as you are. Tonight you can whisper, “Thank You, Heavenly Father, for loving me.”

Week 4 · Night 5 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Compassion & Tenderness
“Warm hugs, warm hearts!”
Willow fits tonight because she gives mercy, being kind even after someone makes a
mistake. In Willow’s world today: her big brother snapped at her over a spilled cup, then
went quiet and pink-eared. Willow brought him a towel and sat beside him until he was
ready to talk. Just like Heavenly Father promised to love His people freely, Willow learned
that mercy does not wait to be earned.

Week 4 · Night 5 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Press your palms together, then open them slowly
like a lily blooming in the night. Now hold them still
and quiet. Can you show me a flower opening
without any noise?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The people had wandered away, and they
felt far off. Heavenly Father loved them
anyway.
NAME   That warm, loved-anyway feeling is called
forgiven. Your mind might think, ‘I am still
loved.’ Your body might feel light, like a
big breath out.
FIND   When did someone love you after you did
something wrong?
FORK   He could make them earn it, or love them
freely. Heavenly Father chose to love them
freely.
CALM   Open both hands like petals and whisper,
‘Heavenly Father, thank You for loving me.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father loves you freely, like fresh dew on the morning grass!

Week 4 · Night 5 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What did Heavenly Father promise to
heal?”
“What flower did He say His people would
grow like?”
“What did Willow bring her brother?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When were you loved freely, before you had
made anything right? Share that memory
with your child.
2. How do you offer mercy before asking for an
apology? Tell about a time you chose that.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor forgive
without keeping score? Describe that quality
to your child.
