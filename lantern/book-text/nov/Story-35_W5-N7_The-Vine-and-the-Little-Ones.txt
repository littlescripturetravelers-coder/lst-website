The Vine and the Little Ones
Jonah was not happy that Heavenly Father saved Nineveh. They had been enemies of his people. Jonah wanted them punished.
Jonah sat outside the city to see what would happen. Heavenly Father made a leafy vine grow over Jonah for shade, like an umbrella.
The next morning, Heavenly Father sent a little worm. The worm chewed the vine, and it dried up. The hot sun beat down, and Jonah was upset.
Heavenly Father said, “You cared about this vine. You did not make it grow. Should I not care about Nineveh?” Nineveh had more than 120,000 people. They still had much to learn, like little children.
Heavenly Father loves everyone, even people who are hard to love. Tonight, think of someone hard to be kind to. Ask Him to help you love them.

Week 5 · Night 7 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Compassion & Tenderness
“Warm hugs, warm hearts!”
Willow fits tonight because a warm heart shows mercy, which means being kind when
someone was unkind. In Willow’s world today: a boy knocked over her tower on purpose.
Later he tripped and scraped his knee. Willow looked at him a long moment, then went
and got the bandage box. Just like Heavenly Father showed mercy to Nineveh, Willow
learned that kindness is bigger than being right.

Week 5 · Night 7 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Hold both arms over your head like a cool green
vine making shade. Now open them wide and
gather everyone you love into a big hug. Can you
show me how big Heavenly Father’s kindness is?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Jonah wanted Nineveh punished, and he
felt angry when Heavenly Father kept it
safe.
NAME   That that’s-not-fair feeling is called angry.
Your mind might think, ‘They should not
get away with it!’ Your body might feel hot
cheeks and tight fists.
FIND   When did it feel unfair that someone else
was forgiven?
FORK   Jonah could stay angry, or let mercy in.
Heavenly Father chose mercy for the
whole city.
CALM   Unclench both hands on your blanket and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, help my heart
be kind.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father loves every person in the whole world, and that means you!

Week 5 · Night 7 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What grew up over Jonah to give him
shade?”
“What happened to the vine the next
morning?”
“What did Willow get for the boy with the
scraped knee?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When has Heavenly Father’s mercy toward
someone else been hard for you to accept?
Share that honestly with your child.
2. How do you teach mercy when your child has
truly been wronged? Tell about a moment
they chose kindness anyway.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor forgive
someone who did not deserve it? Describe
that quality in them for your child.
