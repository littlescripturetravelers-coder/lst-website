The Fish That Was Prepared
The wind and waves were very big.
Jonah knew it was happening because
he had run from Heavenly Father. So
he told the sailors the truth: “Put me in
the sea, and it will be calm.”
The sailors did not want to. They
rowed hard to get back to land, but
the wind was too strong.
At last they put Jonah into the sea, and the
sea grew still. The sailors made promises to
Heavenly Father.
Heavenly Father had already prepared a
great fish. It swallowed Jonah, and he stayed
safe inside for three days and three nights.
Jesus later said this was a sign about Him.
Heavenly Father makes safe places for the
people He loves. Feel your soft, safe bed
tonight. You are one of His people too.

Week 5 · Night 4 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Compassion & Tenderness
“Warm hugs, warm hearts!”
Willow fits tonight because a tender heart knows dark places can still be safe. In Willow’s
world today: the lights went out at nap time, and her little brother started to cry. She
crawled over, found his hand, and held on. They sat together until the lamp blinked back
on. Just like Heavenly Father kept Jonah safe inside the great fish, Willow learned that
love can keep us safe in the dark.

Week 5 · Night 4 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Pull your blanket right up over your shoulders,
like the great fish’s big, quiet room. Breathe in
slow, breathe out slow, all safe inside. Can you
show me your calmest, quietest breathing?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Jonah went down into the deep, dark
sea, and he was afraid.
NAME   That dark, all-alone feeling is called
afraid. Your mind might think, ‘Who will
help me?’ Your body might feel shivery,
with a fast heartbeat.
FIND   When has the dark felt too big for you?
FORK   Jonah could give up, or trust Heavenly
Father. Jonah chose to trust Him and
pray.
CALM   Pull the blanket up to your chin and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, You are here
with me.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father prepares safe places for the people He loves, and that means you!

Week 5 · Night 4 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What did Jonah tell the sailors to do?”
“Who prepared the great fish?”
“What did Willow do when the lights went
out?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did a dark, uncertain season turn out
to be a place where you were held? Share
that with your child.
2. How do you sit with your child’s fear instead
of hurrying it away? Tell about a moment you
simply stayed.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor stay steady
through a long, dark season? Describe that
quality in them for your child.
