The King Who Looked Up
King Nebuchadnezzar dreamed of a great, tall tree full of fruit. Daniel told him what the dream meant. He asked the king to be kind to the poor.
But the king forgot. He said, “I built this great city by my own power!” He was proud. Proud means forgetting that good things come from Heavenly Father.
For a long time, the king could not think clearly. He lived outside with the animals and ate grass like a cow.
At last the king looked up to heaven, and his good mind came back. He praised Heavenly Father and was king again. This time he was humble. Humble means remembering that every good thing comes from Heavenly Father.
Tonight you can look up and say thank you for three good things.

Week 3 · Night 4 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Compassion & Tenderness
“Warm hugs, warm hearts!”
Willow fits tonight because Heavenly Father gave the proud king another chance, and
gentle mercy is Willow’s gift. In Willow’s world today: her big brother says his tower is
the best one anybody ever built. Then it topples, and he goes quiet. Willow does not say,
“I told you so.” She sits down and hands him blocks. Just like Heavenly Father welcoming
the king back, Willow learned that kindness helps a heart after a fall.

Week 3 · Night 4 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lie back and look up at the ceiling the way the
king lifted his eyes to heaven. Now let both hands
rest open and soft on your tummy. Can you show
me your looking-up face?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The king felt big and important, and he
forgot who gave him everything.
NAME   That puffed-up, look-at-me feeling is
called pride. Your mind might think, ‘I did
it all by myself.’ Your body might feel
puffed up, with your chin held high.
FIND   When did you want everyone to see how
big you are?
FORK   The king could keep saying, “Look at
me,” or look up and give thanks. At last
he looked up.
CALM   Turn your palms up and whisper,
‘Heavenly Father, thank You for
everything I have.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father helps us when we look up to Him with thankful hearts!

Parent Prompt
Question for the Child

Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did a humbling season turn out to be a
mercy? Tell about it.
2. How do you receive your child after a fall
without lecturing? Tell about a moment you
managed it.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor carry success
humbly? Describe that quality in them.
