Week 1 · Night 5
Mourning Turned Into Joy
Heavenly Father’s people were very sad. Many had been taken far from home.
So Heavenly Father gave Jeremiah a happy promise to share. One day the people would come home. They would sing together on a high hill in Jerusalem.
He said they would be like a watered garden. A dry garden droops, but a watered garden is green and full of life.
Girls would dance, and young men and old men would be glad together. Heavenly Father said, “I will turn their mourning into joy.” Their big, heavy sadness would turn into great big happiness.
Heavenly Father can help a sad heart feel glad again. If you felt sad today, tell Him about it tonight. He loves to comfort you.
=== page 3 ===
Week 1 · Night 5 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Compassion & Tenderness
“Warm hugs, warm hearts!”
Willow fits tonight because she stays close until sad feelings soften. In Willow’s world
today: her brother drops his ice cream, and his whole face crumples. Willow does not
laugh. She sits beside him on the curb and hands him her own spoon, and soon they are
both giggling. Just like Heavenly Father turning mourning into joy, Willow learned that
comfort helps a sad thing become a glad one.

Week 1 · Night 5 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Let your face go sad and droop your shoulders
way down low. Now lift your shoulders up, let a
smile grow slowly, and do one little dance. Can
you show me sad turning into glad?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The people were far from home and had
been sad for a long time.
NAME   That sad-for-a-long-time feeling is
called grief. Your mind might think, ‘I
miss how things were.’ Your body might
feel tired, with tears close to your eyes.
FIND   When did you feel sad for more than a
little while?
FORK   They could hide their sadness, or let
Heavenly Father comfort them. Heavenly
Father promised to turn it into joy.
CALM   Rest a hand on your tummy and whisper,
‘Heavenly Father, please help my sad
turn glad.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father can turn our sad times into happy, singing times!

Week 1 · Night 5 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What would Heavenly Father turn their
mourning into?”
“Their souls would be like a watered what?”
“What did Willow give her brother?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did Heavenly Father turn a grief of
yours into something you could be glad
about? Share it.
2. How do you sit with your child’s sadness
before fixing it? Tell about a time you simply
stayed.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor carry real joy
through real sorrow? Describe that quality in
them.
