Slow to Anger, Great in Kindness
Joel told the people what Heavenly Father said. It was a hard, hungry time, and many had turned away.
Heavenly Father said, “Turn to me with all your heart.” He wanted all of them, not just a little bit.
Back then, people tore their clothes to show they were very sorry. But Heavenly Father said, “Tear your heart, and not your clothes.” He wanted them sorry inside, ready to change.
Joel told them why it was safe to come back. Heavenly Father is kind and forgiving. He is slow to get angry. His kindness is very, very big.
When you do something wrong, you can turn back to Him too. You can say sorry and try again. He is happy when you come back.

Week 4 · Night 6 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Courage & Faith
“Big heart, brave start!”
Beau fits tonight because telling the truth takes real courage. In Beau’s world today: Beau
broke the little clay bird off the shelf and hid the pieces under a cushion. His tummy hurt
all afternoon. At bath time he brought the pieces out in both hands and told Mom
everything. Just like the people Joel asked to turn back with all their heart, Beau learned
that turning back is safe when love is waiting.

Week 4 · Night 6 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Roll over and face away from me. Now turn all
the way back around and look at me. Can you
show me your bravest turn-around?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The people had wandered away and felt
sad. Heavenly Father asked them to turn
back.
NAME   That heavy, need-to-tell feeling is called
sorry. Your mind might think, ‘I wish I had
not done that.’ Your body might feel a
tight tummy.
FIND   When did your tummy feel tight because
of something you hid?
FORK   They could just look sorry outside, or be
sorry inside. Heavenly Father asked for
their whole heart.
CALM   Put one hand on your tummy and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, I want to
come back to You.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father is slow to anger and full of kindness, so turning back is safe!

Week 4 · Night 6 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What did Heavenly Father ask His people
to turn back with?”
“Is Heavenly Father quick or slow to get
angry?”
“What did Beau hide under the cushion?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did you turn back and find kindness
instead of the anger you expected? Share
that story with your child.
2. How does your face look when your child
tells you the truth? Tell about a time your
gentleness helped.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor answer with
patience instead of temper? Describe that
quality to your child.
