Week 1 · Night 7
The Words Written Again
Jeremiah spoke Heavenly Father’s
words out loud. His friend Baruch
wrote every word on a scroll. A scroll
is a long paper rolled up like a tube.
Baruch read the words in the temple,
Heavenly Father’s holy house. Later
the king heard them, but he did not
like them. He cut the scroll into pieces
and put it in his fire.
The words were a warning: change your ways,
or Babylon will come. The king did not want to
change.
Then Heavenly Father told Jeremiah, “Take
thee again another roll.” A roll is another
scroll. So Baruch wrote all the words a second
time, and even more words were added.
Heavenly Father’s words could not be lost.
When something you made gets broken, you
can feel brave and begin again too.

=== page 3 ===
Week 1 · Night 7 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Courage & Faith
“Big heart, brave start!”
Beau fits tonight because starting over takes courage, and that is his kind of brave. In
Beau’s world today: his tall block tower gets bumped over right before show-and-tell. His
chin wobbles for a second. Then he kneels down and starts stacking again from the very
bottom. Just like Baruch writing every word a second time, Beau learned that something
worth making is worth making again.

Week 1 · Night 7 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Pretend your finger is a pen and write something
in the air. Now wipe it away with your hand and
write it again, just as carefully. Can you show me
how to begin again?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The king burned the scroll, and all of
Baruch’s careful writing was gone.
NAME   That all-my-work-is-gone feeling is
called disappointed. Your mind might
think, ‘It’s ruined, so I give up.’ Your body
might feel hot cheeks and a wobbly chin.
FIND   When did something you worked hard
on get ruined?
FORK   Baruch could quit, or pick up his pen
again. Baruch wrote every word once
more.
CALM   Open your two hands and whisper,
‘Heavenly Father, please help me begin
again.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father’s words can never be lost, and we can always begin again!

Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“Who wrote Jeremiah’s words on a scroll?”
“What did Baruch do after the scroll was
burned?”
“What happened to Beau’s block tower?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did you have to rebuild something
from nothing? Share what helped you pick it
back up.
2. How do you respond when your child’s work
is ruined? Tell about a time you helped them
restart.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor rebuild after
losing nearly everything? Describe that
quality in them.
