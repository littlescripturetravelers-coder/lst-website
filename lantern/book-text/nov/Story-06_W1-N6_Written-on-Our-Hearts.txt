Written on Our Hearts
Heavenly Father made a promise
through Jeremiah. “I will make a new
covenant,” He said. A covenant is a
special two-way promise between
Heavenly Father and His people.
Long before, His law was written on
flat stones for Moses. A law is a rule
that helps us live well. This new
covenant would be different.
“I will put my law in their inward parts, and
write it in their hearts,” He said. Inside them,
it would go everywhere they went.
“They shall all know me, from the least of
them unto the greatest.” Least means the
littlest. Many years later, Jesus came and
brought this new covenant.
That means you, little one. Tonight, rest your
hand on your heart and feel the good He
wants to write there.

Week 1 · Night 6 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wisdom & Reﬂection
“Pause and ponder — what a wonder!”
Wren fits tonight because she keeps quiet truths tucked inside her, right where this
promise goes. In Wren’s world today: nobody is watching when she knocks a puzzle piece
under the couch. She could walk away. Instead, she lies flat on her tummy, fishes it out,
and puts it back. Just like Heavenly Father writing His law on hearts, Wren learned that
the best way to do right is from the inside.

=== page 4 ===
Week 1 · Night 6 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Put your hand flat on your chest and feel your heart
tapping. Now trace one slow line over it, like
Heavenly Father writing something good inside.
Can you show me where your heart is?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The people had broken Heavenly Father’s
first covenant, and they needed a new
start.
NAME   That oops, I-wish-I-had-done-better
feeling is called sorry. Your mind might
think, ‘I wish I had chosen right.’ Your body
might feel a quick drop in your tummy.
FIND   When did you wish you had made a better
choice?
FORK   They could do good only when someone
watched, or keep His law in their hearts.
Heavenly Father promised to write it there.
CALM   Rest your hand over your heart and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, please write
good things in me.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father writes His goodness inside your heart, so it goes wherever you go!

Week 1 · Night 6 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“Where did Heavenly Father promise to
write His law?”
“Who brought the new covenant many
years later?”
“What did Wren do with the lost puzzle
piece?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did something you were taught move
from your head down into your heart? Share
when it changed.
2. How do you help your child obey from the
inside out? Tell about a time they chose right
unwatched.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor carry their faith
quietly on the inside? Describe that quality in
them.
