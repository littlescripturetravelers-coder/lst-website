Sure as the Morning
Heavenly Father’s people had wandered far from Him, and they missed Him. In Hosea’s book, they said, “Come, let’s go back to the Lord.”
They said, “He will heal us. He will wrap up our hurts.” A mom puts a bandage on a scraped knee. Heavenly Father helps our hurts get better like that.
They said He would come to them as sure as the morning. Every day, the sun comes up. Morning never forgets to come.
They said He would come like the rain, too. Farmers waited for rain, because plants need water. Heavenly Father’s help comes just when it is needed.
The sun has gone down tonight, but morning will surely come. Heavenly Father’s love is that sure. If you ever feel far from Him, you can come back with a prayer.

Week 4 · Night 3 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Curiosity & Discovery
“Look and see — what could it be?”
Scout fits tonight because he loves to go looking and finding. In Scout’s world today:
Scout woke before anyone else and pressed his nose to the cold window to watch the sky
turn pink. He watched the whole thing. At breakfast he told everyone the sun came back
again. Just like the people who learned Heavenly Father comes as sure as the morning,
Scout learned that morning always keeps its promise.

Week 4 · Night 3 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Curl up tight and small like the dark middle of the
night. Now slowly open, like the sun coming up,
arms stretching wide. Can you show me how
morning comes?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The people were far from Heavenly
Father and missed Him. They wanted to
come home.
NAME   That missing, want-to-go-home feeling
is called longing. Your mind might think,
‘I want to be close again.’ Your body
might feel an ache in your chest.
FIND   When did you miss someone and want
to run to them?
FORK   They could stay far away, or come back
home. They said, “Come, let us return.”
CALM   Breathe in like fresh morning air and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, I want to stay
close to You.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father comes to us as sure as the morning and as gentle as the rain!

Parent Prompt

Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did Heavenly Father meet you as
steadily as sunrise after a long dark time?
Share that with your child.
2. How do you welcome your child back after a
hard day? Tell about a time you did.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor keep faithful
habits day after day? Describe that
steadiness to your child.
