Ten Days and a Cup of Water
Daniel and his three friends were taken
far from home to Babylon, a big city
with a powerful king. The king sent
them rich food from his own table.
But Heavenly Father had taught His
people which foods to eat, and Daniel
wanted to obey. “Please,” he asked
kindly, “give us vegetables and water
for ten days. Then look at us and see.”
For ten days they ate vegetables and drank
water. When the days were over, their faces
looked healthier than all the other young
men’s.
Heavenly Father gave the four friends
knowledge and wisdom. The king found them
ten times better than all his wise men.
Small, quiet choices make a heart strong.
Think of one good choice you made today, and
feel how strong and good it made you inside.

Week 3 · Night 1 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wisdom & Reﬂection
“Pause and ponder — what a wonder!”
Wren fits tonight because Daniel chose quietly and wisely, and quiet wisdom is Wren’s
gift. In Wren’s world today: at snack time everyone reaches for the cookies first. Wren
eats her apple slices first, slowly, without telling anybody, and she feels good and strong
inside. Just like Daniel asking for vegetables and water for ten days, Wren learned that
small, quiet choices make a heart strong.

Week 3 · Night 1 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Sit up tall like Daniel standing before the king.
Take a slow pretend sip of cool water and feel
strong all the way down to your toes. Can you
show me how tall and strong you can be?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Daniel was far from home in a new,
strange place, and he wanted to obey.
NAME   That wobbly, far-from-home feeling is
called unsettled. Your mind might think,
‘Everything here is different.’ Your body
might feel shaky inside, like your tummy
can’t sit still.
FIND   When did you feel unsure in a new place?
FORK   Daniel could eat the king’s food to fit in,
or ask kindly to obey. Daniel asked kindly
and obeyed.
CALM   Press both feet flat and whisper,
‘Heavenly Father, help me choose what is
right.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father makes quiet, faithful hearts strong and wise!

Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What did Daniel ask to eat and drink?”
“How many days did the four friends wait?”
“What did Wren eat first at snack time?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When has a small, quiet choice made you
stronger than you expected? Tell about it.
2. How do you help your child choose well
without shaming them? Tell about a moment
you got it right.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor hold quietly to
their standards far from home? Describe that
quality in them.
