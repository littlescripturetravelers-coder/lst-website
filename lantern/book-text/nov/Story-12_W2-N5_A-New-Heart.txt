Week 2 · Night 5
A New Heart
Heavenly Father’s people were far from home. They had stopped listening. Their hearts had grown hard, like a stone. Squeeze a stone all day, and it never gets soft.
Heavenly Father did not give up on them. He made a promise through the prophet Ezekiel.
He said, “I will give you a new heart. I will take away the heart of stone. I will give you a soft, living heart.”
A soft heart is warm and alive, like your heart beating right now. A soft heart can listen, say sorry, and love again. Heavenly Father also promised to put His Spirit inside them, to help them do good.
When you feel grumpy inside, He can help. Tonight, put your hand on your heart. Say, “Heavenly Father, please give me a soft, kind heart.”

Week 2 · Night 5 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Compassion & Tenderness
“Warm hugs, warm hearts!”
Willow fits tonight because a soft, warm heart is what she cares about most. In Willow’s
world today: Willow stayed mad at her brother all afternoon and would not look at him.
Her chest felt tight and cold. At bath time something loosened, and she handed him the
good boat without being asked. Just like Heavenly Father’s promise of a new heart,
Willow learned that He can turn a hard feeling soft.

Week 2 · Night 5 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Squeeze both fists tight and hard, like two little
stones. Now open them slowly and let your fingers
go soft and floppy. Can you show me your stone
hands turning into soft hands?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The people’s hearts had grown hard and
cold, and they stopped listening.
NAME   That tight, grumpy, closed-up feeling is
called stubborn. Your mind might think, ‘I
won’t, and you can’t make me!’ Your body
might feel tight fists and a hard chest.
FIND   When did your heart feel hard and not
want to say sorry?
FORK   They could keep hearts of stone, or let
Heavenly Father make them new. He
promised to help them.
CALM   Press one hand on your chest, breathe out
long, and whisper, ‘Heavenly Father,
make my heart soft.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father can change a heart of stone into a soft, loving heart!

Week 2 · Night 5 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What new thing did Heavenly Father
promise to give?”
“What kind of heart did He promise to take
away?”
“What did Willow hand her brother at bath
time?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did your own heart grow hard, and
what softened it? Share that with your child.
2. How do you invite softness instead of
demanding an apology? Tell about a time you
waited for a child’s heart to turn.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor forgive when it
was very hard? Describe that quality in them.
