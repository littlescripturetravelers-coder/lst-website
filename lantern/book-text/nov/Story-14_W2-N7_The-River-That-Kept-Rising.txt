Week 2 · Night 7
The River That Kept Rising
In a vision, a picture from Heavenly
Father, Ezekiel saw the temple. A
temple is Heavenly Father’s holy house.
A little stream came trickling out from
under the temple door, flowing east.
A man with a measuring line walked
Ezekiel into the water. First it came up
to his ankles. Then his knees. Then his
waist!
Soon it was a deep river, too deep to walk
across. Trees grew along both sides. Their fruit
was good to eat, and their leaves could help
people get well.
That land was hot and dry, and plants need
water. So the river showed how Heavenly
Father’s goodness brings life everywhere it
goes. Later, Jesus offered “living water,” His gift
that keeps us alive inside.
Tonight you can feel calm, like floating on a
gentle river, safe in His care.

=== page 3 ===
Week 2 · Night 7 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Curiosity & Discovery
“Look and see — what could it be?”
Scout fits tonight because a seeker always wants to take one more step and see what
happens. In Scout’s world today: Scout waded into the lake in his boots. Ankles, then
knees, then he stopped and looked back at Dad, grinning. Dad held out his hand, and they
went deeper together. Just like Ezekiel walking deeper into the river, Scout learned that
Heavenly Father’s good gifts grow bigger the farther we walk with Him.

Week 2 · Night 7 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lie down and pat your ankles, then your knees,
then your tummy, as the water rises higher and
higher. Now float still and easy on top. Can you
show me how deep the water got?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The water kept rising higher, and Ezekiel
wondered what would come next.
NAME   That eager, what-will-happen feeling is
called curious. Your mind might think, ‘I
want to see more!’ Your body might feel
bouncy feet and wide eyes.
FIND   When did you want to keep going just to
see what happened?
FORK   Ezekiel could stop at the edge, or keep
walking in. Ezekiel chose to keep walking
in.
CALM   Wiggle your toes, then hold them still,
and whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, lead me
close to You.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father’s goodness grows deeper the farther we walk with Him!

=== page 5 ===
Week 2 · Night 7 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“Where did the little stream of water come
from?”
“How deep did the water get at the end?”
“Who held out a hand to Scout in the lake?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. Where has your faith grown deeper than
where you began? Share that part of your
journey with your child.
2. How do you invite a child one step farther
without pushing? Tell about a time you held
out your hand instead.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor keep growing
in faith their whole life? Describe that quality
in them.
