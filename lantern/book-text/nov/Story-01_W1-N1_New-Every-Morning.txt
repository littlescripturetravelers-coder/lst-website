Week 1 · Night 1
New Every Morning
Long ago, Heavenly Father’s people lived in a city called Jerusalem. An army from a land called Babylon came and broke the city. Many people had to leave their homes.
The prophet Jeremiah was very sad. A prophet shares Heavenly Father’s words. Jeremiah wrote sad songs about his city. His heart felt heavy, like a big stone.
Then Jeremiah remembered that Heavenly Father is always kind. He wrote, “The Lord’s kindness never runs out. It is new every morning.”
Why did Jeremiah talk about the morning? Because the sun comes up every day. It comes up after happy days and sad days. Heavenly Father’s kindness comes back new like that, every morning.
If today felt hard, remember what Jeremiah remembered. Tomorrow the sun will come up. Heavenly Father’s kindness will be there, waiting for you.

Week 1 · Night 1 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wisdom & Reﬂection
“Pause and ponder — what a wonder!”
Wren fits tonight because she sits still long enough to notice a quiet truth. In Wren’s
world today: she spills her juice at breakfast, and the awful feeling stays all morning. At
bedtime, Mom says tomorrow will be a brand-new day. Just like the writer of
Lamentations remembering that Heavenly Father’s mercies are new every morning, Wren
learned that His kindness never runs out.

Week 1 · Night 1 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Squeeze your eyes shut tight, like the darkest part
of the night. Now open them slowly, slowly, the
way the sun comes up. Can you show me how a
new morning opens?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Jerusalem’s people lost their homes, and
their hearts felt heavy and sad.
NAME   That heavy, everything-is-gone feeling is
called sorrow. Your mind might think,
‘Nothing will be good again.’ Your body
might feel a heavy weight in your chest.
FIND   When did your heart feel heavy because
something you loved was gone?
FORK   The writer could stay in the heavy dark,
or remember Heavenly Father’s
kindness. The writer chose to remember.
CALM   Take one slow breath and whisper,
‘Heavenly Father, thank You for a new
morning.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father’s kindness comes back brand-new every single morning!

Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When has a new morning given back
something a hard night took from you? Share
that with someone.
2. How do you help your child begin again after
a hard day? Tell about a time you offered a
fresh start.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor greet each
morning with quiet hope? Describe that
quality in them.
