Week 4 · Night 2
Cords of Love
Heavenly Father told how He led His
people. “I drew them with cords of a
man,” He said, “with bands of love.”
Cords and bands are ropes. Back then,
people used ropes to lead their
animals every day. But Heavenly
Father did not pull hard. His cords
were love, like arms around you,
guiding you home.
A yoke is a heavy wooden bar that farm
animals wore to pull a load. Farmers saw
them all the time. Heavenly Father said He
was like someone lifting that heavy yoke off.
Then He set food before them so they could
eat. Kindness, and more kindness.
Heavenly Father draws you with love, too.
Tonight, feel how His kindness makes your
heart want to come near.

Week 4 · Night 2 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wisdom & Reﬂection
“Pause and ponder — what a wonder!”
Wren fits tonight because she notices how love leads without pushing. In Wren’s world
today: a new boy at preschool would not come to circle time. Wren did not tug his sleeve.
She sat down nearby, slid the good blue cushion over to him, and waited. He came. Just
like Heavenly Father drew His people with bands of love, Wren learned that kindness
invites better than pulling.

Week 4 · Night 2 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Wrap your arms around yourself in a soft hug,
gentle and never tight. Now let your shoulders
drop, heavy and loose. Can you show me your
softest hug?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Heavenly Father’s people were tired, and
He gently lifted their heavy load away.
NAME   That light, load-lifted-off feeling is called
relief. Your mind might think, ‘Ahh, that is
better.’ Your body might feel your
shoulders drop and loosen.
FIND   When has someone carried something
heavy for you?
FORK   He could pull them with hard ropes, or
draw them with love. Heavenly Father
chose love.
CALM   Roll your shoulders down slowly and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, thank You for
being gentle.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father leads us with soft cords of love, never a hard tug!

Week 4 · Night 2 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What did Heavenly Father use to draw His
people close?”
“What heavy thing did He lift off?”
“What did Wren slide over to the new
boy?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did someone lift a load off you at just
the right moment? Share that story with your
child tonight.
2. Where could you invite instead of tug at
home? Tell about a time gentleness worked
better than pressure.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor lead your
family with gentleness instead of force?
Describe that to your child.
