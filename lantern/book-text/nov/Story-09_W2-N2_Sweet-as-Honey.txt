Sweet as Honey
Heavenly Father showed Ezekiel a vision. A hand reached out, holding a long rolled-up paper called a scroll. It was full of writing.
Then He said something surprising: “Eat this scroll. Then go and speak to my people.” His people had stopped listening to Him.
Ezekiel trusted Him. He opened his mouth and ate the scroll. It tasted as sweet as honey!
Why eat a scroll? Heavenly Father wanted His words to go inside Ezekiel. Then Ezekiel would know them by heart when he taught. His words are good, so they tasted sweet.
When someone reads you the scriptures, His words go inside you too. Tonight, ask a grown-up to read you one sweet verse. Keep it in your heart.

Week 2 · Night 2 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wisdom & Reﬂection
“Pause and ponder — what a wonder!”
Wren fits tonight because she keeps good words in her heart and thinks about them. In
Wren’s world today: Wren asked Dad for the very same story three nights in a row. She
said the words along with him, slower each time. In the morning, over her cereal, she was
still thinking about it. Just like Ezekiel eating the sweet scroll, Wren learned that Heavenly
Father’s words stay sweet long after the book is closed.

Week 2 · Night 2 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Rub your tummy and smack your lips, like
something sweet just went all the way down. Now
lay both hands over your heart, where good words
stay. Can you show me your sweetest happy face?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Ezekiel ate the scroll, and it tasted sweet
as honey. He was surprised and glad.
NAME   That happy, surprised, yummy feeling is
called delight. Your mind might think, ‘Oh,
this is so good!’ Your body might feel a
big smile and a warm tummy.
FIND   When did something turn out much
better than you thought?
FORK   Ezekiel could keep his mouth shut, or
trust Heavenly Father and eat. Ezekiel
chose to trust.
CALM   Take one slow breath and whisper,
‘Heavenly Father, thank You for Your
sweet words.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father’s words are sweet, and they stay in our hearts!

Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What did Heavenly Father ask Ezekiel to
eat?”
“What did the scroll taste like?”
“How many nights did Wren want the same

Parent Experience
Testimony
1. Which scripture has tasted sweet to you
when you needed it most? Share that verse
with your child.
2. How do you help good words settle in
instead of rushing past? Tell about a time
your child asked to hear something again.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor love reading
the scriptures out loud? Describe that quality
in them.
