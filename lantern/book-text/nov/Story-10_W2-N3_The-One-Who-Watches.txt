Week 2 · Night 3
The One Who Watches
Long ago, cities had tall stone walls. A watchman stood high on the wall and looked far away. If he saw trouble coming, he blew a loud trumpet to warn everyone.
Heavenly Father told Ezekiel, “I have made you a watchman for my people.” Ezekiel would warn the people to keep them safe.
Many people had wandered away from Heavenly Father. They were choosing wrong things.
Heavenly Father wanted every one of them to come home. So Ezekiel did not stay quiet. He called out kindly: “Turn, turn, and live!” Turning around and coming back to Him is called repenting.
If you choose something wrong, you can turn around too. Say sorry and try again. Heavenly Father is always glad when His children come back.

Week 2 · Night 3 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Courage & Faith
“Big heart, brave start!”
Beau fits tonight because calling out when it matters takes a brave voice. In Beau’s world
today: Beau saw his little cousin chasing a ball toward the street. His throat went tight,
but he shouted “Stop!” loud and clear. His cousin froze at the curb and came running back.
Just like Ezekiel the watchman calling out to his people, Beau learned that a brave voice
can help someone get safely home.

Week 2 · Night 3 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Sit up tall like a watchman on the wall and shade
your eyes with one hand, looking far, far away. Now
wave both arms: come home, come home! Can you
show me your watching eyes?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Heavenly Father’s people had wandered
away, and He wanted them to come home.
NAME   That missing-someone, please-come-back
feeling is called longing. Your mind might
think, ‘I wish you were here.’ Your body
might feel an ache in your chest.
FIND   When did you miss someone and keep
watching for them?
FORK   Ezekiel could stay quiet on the wall, or call
out to help. Ezekiel chose to call out.
CALM   Put your hand on your heart and whisper,
‘Heavenly Father, help me turn back to
You.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father always wants us to turn around and come home to Him!

Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When has someone’s warning protected
you? Share that story with your child.
2. How do you correct a child without shaming
them? Tell about a time you called your child
back gently.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor watch over the
whole family? Describe that quality in them.
