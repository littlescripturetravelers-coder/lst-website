The Ship Going the Other Way
Jonah was a prophet, a man Heavenly
Father sent to tell His words. Heavenly
Father said, “Go to Nineveh.” Nineveh
was a big city of the Assyrians, who
had been enemies of Jonah’s people.
Jonah did not want to go. He went to
Joppa, a town by the sea, and got on a
ship going far the other way, to
Tarshish.
Out at sea a great wind blew, and the waves
grew tall. The sailors, the men who work on
a ship, threw their heavy things over the side
to make it lighter.
Jonah was fast asleep down inside the ship.
The captain woke him and said, “Get up and
pray!”
Even far out at sea, Heavenly Father knew
right where Jonah was. Snuggle in and feel
it: He knows where you are tonight too.

Week 5 · Night 3 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Courage & Faith
“Big heart, brave start!”
Beau fits tonight because it takes courage to walk toward a hard job instead of running
from it. In Beau’s world today: Beau knocked over the cracker box, and crumbs went
everywhere. His feet wanted to run down the hallway, but he found the little broom
instead. Mom came in and found him sweeping. Just like Jonah could not sail away from
Heavenly Father, Beau learned that turning around is braver than running.

Week 5 · Night 3 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Curl up small on your side, like Jonah asleep in
the bottom of the ship. Now rock gently side to
side, the way a boat rocks on big water. Can you
show me how a sleepy sailor rocks?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Jonah was asked to do a hard thing, and
he wanted to run away.
NAME   That heavy, don’t-make-me feeling is
called dread. Your mind might think, ‘I
don’t want to!’ Your body might feel
slow, heavy feet and a tight tummy.
FIND   When did you not want to do something
hard?
FORK   Jonah could go to Nineveh, or sail the
other way. This time, Jonah chose the
ship.
CALM   Take one slow breath in and out and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, help me turn
around.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
No ship can ever sail away from Heavenly Father’s love!

=== page 5 ===
Week 5 · Night 3 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“Where did Heavenly Father ask Jonah to
go?”
“What was Jonah doing while the big wind
blew?”
“What did Beau find instead of running
away?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did you go the other way from
something Heavenly Father asked of you?
Share with your child how you turned around.
2. How do you invite your child back when they
run from a hard task? Tell about a time they
turned around.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor answer a call
they would rather have avoided? Describe
that quality in them for your child.
