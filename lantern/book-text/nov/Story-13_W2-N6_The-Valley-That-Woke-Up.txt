Week 2 · Night 6
The Valley That Woke Up
Heavenly Father showed Ezekiel a vision,
a picture from Heavenly Father. He saw a
wide valley full of very dry bones.
Heavenly Father led Ezekiel all around
them. Everything was very still and quiet.
“Can these bones live again?” Heavenly
Father asked. Ezekiel answered,
“Heavenly Father, You know.” Heavenly
Father told him, “Speak my words to
them.”
Ezekiel spoke, and there was a noise and a
shaking. The bones came together, and people
formed. Then breath came into them. They
stood up alive, as many as a great army!
This vision taught Ezekiel’s sad people that
Heavenly Father could bring them home again.
It also reminds us that, because of Jesus,
everyone will live again one day.
Heavenly Father can make new things grow.
Tonight, breathe in slowly and feel the gift of
your own breath.

=== page 3 ===
Week 2 · Night 6 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Courage & Faith
“Big heart, brave start!”
Beau fits tonight because doing the right thing when it looks impossible takes real faith. In
Beau’s world today: Beau planted one seed in a paper cup, and nothing happened for
days. He kept watering it, even when his sister said it was done for. On Friday a green curl
pushed up through the dirt. Just like Ezekiel speaking Heavenly Father’s words in the still
valley, Beau learned that Heavenly Father brings life where we cannot see it yet.

Week 2 · Night 6 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lie down very still and quiet, like the still valley
before morning. Now take a big breath in and
stretch both arms up tall and alive. Can you show
me the whole valley waking up?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The valley was dry and still, and it
looked like nothing could ever live there.
NAME   That nothing-can-change feeling is
called hopeless. Your mind might think,
‘It’s too late.’ Your body might feel heavy
and droopy, like a wilted flower.
FIND   When did something feel too broken to
ever be fixed?
FORK   Ezekiel could stay quiet, or speak
Heavenly Father’s words anyway.
Ezekiel chose to speak them.
CALM   Breathe in slowly through your nose and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, thank You for
my breath.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father can bring life back, and because of Jesus we will all live again!

Week 2 · Night 6 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What did Heavenly Father ask Ezekiel in
the valley?”
“What happened when the breath came
in?”
“What finally grew in Beau’s paper cup?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. What in your life came back after you had
given up on it? Share that with your child.
2. How do you keep hoping out loud for your
family? Tell about a time you spoke hope
before you felt it.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor keep faith
through a long, hard season? Describe that
quality in them.
