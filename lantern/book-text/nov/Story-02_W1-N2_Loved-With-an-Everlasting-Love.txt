Week 1 · Night 2
Loved With an Everlasting Love
Heavenly Father’s people had been
taken far from their homes to faraway
lands. Far from home, it was easy to feel
forgotten and alone.
Then Heavenly Father sent a message
through Jeremiah, His prophet. A
prophet shares Heavenly Father’s words.
“I have loved thee with an everlasting
love,” He said. Everlasting means it
never, ever stops.
“With lovingkindness have I drawn thee,” He
said. Lovingkindness is love and kindness
together. Drawn means pulled close, gently,
the way you pull a blanket up to your chin.
“Again I will build thee,” He promised. Their
land had been broken, but they would be built
up again, and the people would dance once
more.
That same love is holding you right now in your
warm bed. It started before you were born, and
it will never stop.

=== page 3 ===
Week 1 · Night 2 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Compassion & Tenderness
“Warm hugs, warm hearts!”
Willow fits tonight because she is gentle, and this story shows Heavenly Father being
gentle with people who felt forgotten. In Willow’s world today: a new boy sits alone at
the edge of the preschool rug. Willow does not tug his arm. She scoots over slowly and
shares half her crayons until he scoots closer. Just like Heavenly Father drawing His
people close with lovingkindness, Willow learned that real love invites and never pushes.

Week 1 · Night 2 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Stretch your arms out wide, wider, wider. Now
wrap them all the way around yourself in a hug
that does not let go. Can you show me a hug that
never ends?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The people were far from home and felt
like Heavenly Father had forgotten them.
NAME   That far-away, left-out feeling is called
lonely. Your mind might think, ‘Nobody
wants me.’ Your body might feel small
and quiet, with a lump in your throat.
FIND   When did you feel left out, like nobody
remembered you?
FORK   They could believe they were forgotten,
or believe His promise. Heavenly Father
showed them they were loved.
CALM   Hug your own shoulders and whisper,
‘Heavenly Father, thank You for loving
me always.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father’s love for you started before you were born, and it will never stop!

Week 1 · Night 2 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What word means a love that never, ever
stops?”
“What did Heavenly Father promise to build
again?”
“What did Willow share with the new boy?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did you feel drawn back by love
instead of pushed by guilt? Share that
memory with someone.
2. How do you invite your child back after they
pull away? Tell about a time you chose
gentleness.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor love people
steadily for a very long time? Describe that
quality in them.
