Week 4 · Night 1
He Taught Me How to Walk
Hosea told how Heavenly Father loves His people, like a parent loves a child.
He said, “When Israel was a child, I loved him. I called my son out of Egypt.” He had led His people away from a mean king there. Matthew later said these words were about Jesus too.
His people wandered, but He kept helping them. He called His people Ephraim. He said, “I taught Ephraim to walk, holding them by their arms.”
Think of a baby learning to walk. A parent holds the baby’s arms, so the baby won’t fall. Heavenly Father held His people up like that.
Someone held your hands when you learned to walk. Tonight you can say, “Thank You, Heavenly Father, for holding me up.”
=== page 3 ===
Week 4 · Night 1 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Compassion & Tenderness
“Warm hugs, warm hearts!”
Willow fits tonight because her love shows up in slow, gentle holding. In Willow’s world
today: her baby cousin wobbled across the rug and plopped down hard. Willow did not
laugh. She knelt, gave him both her hands, and walked slowly backward while he tried
again. Just like Heavenly Father held Ephraim by the arms, Willow learned that love helps
little steps feel safe.

=== page 4 ===
Week 4 · Night 1 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Reach up and hold my two hands. Now squeeze
softly, the way a baby holds on while learning to
walk. Can you show me how gently you can hold
on?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Heavenly Father held His people up like a
baby learning to walk, and they felt safe.
NAME   That safe, held-up, cozy feeling is called
loved. Your mind might think, ‘Someone
has me.’ Your body might feel warm and
soft in your chest.
FIND   When did someone hold your hand when
you felt wobbly?
FORK   He could let them wobble alone, or hold
them up. Heavenly Father chose to hold
their arms.
CALM   Open your hands on the blanket and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, please hold
me while I sleep.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father held His children’s hands, and He holds you close, too!

Week 4 · Night 1 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What did Heavenly Father teach Ephraim
to do?”
“How did He hold them up?”
“Who did Willow help walk across the
rug?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did Heavenly Father steady you
through something hard, though you only
noticed later? Share that memory with your
child.
2. How do you slow down to match your child’s
small steps? Tell about a time you did.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor quietly hold
someone up without needing thanks? Tell
your child about them.
