Week 3 · Night 5
The Hand Upon the Wall
King Belshazzar had a big party. The fingers of a hand wrote words on the wall! The king was afraid.
No wise man could read them. Then the queen remembered Daniel. “Send for Daniel,” she said. “He can tell you what it means.”
The king promised Daniel gifts for reading it. He would get a red robe, a gold necklace, and an important job.
Daniel said, “Keep your gifts. I will read the writing for you anyway.” Then he told what the words meant. It was not happy news, but Daniel told the truth.
Daniel did not tell the truth to get a prize. He told the truth because it was right. You can tell the truth too, even when it is hard. Tonight, feel how light your heart is when you are honest.
=== page 3 ===
Week 3 · Night 5 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wisdom & Reﬂection
“Pause and ponder — what a wonder!”
Wren fits tonight because Daniel told the plain truth without wanting a prize, and quiet
truth is Wren’s way. In Wren’s world today: Wren knocks over the crayon cup, and her
friend gets blamed. Wren’s tummy goes tight. She walks over, tells the truth, “It was me,”
and helps pick up every crayon. Just like Daniel telling the truth without wanting the king’s
gifts, Wren learned that telling the truth is right, even with no reward.

Week 3 · Night 5 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Put your hands behind your back like Daniel
saying, “Keep your gifts.” Now take one big, brave
breath. Can you show me your telling-the-truth
face?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   A hand wrote on the wall, and the king
felt scared and mixed up.
NAME   That shaky, something’s-wrong feeling is
called uneasy. Your mind might think,
‘What is happening?’ Your body might
feel a tight tummy and wobbly knees.
FIND   When did your tummy feel tight because
something wasn’t right?
FORK   Daniel could take the prize and change
the truth, or tell it plainly. Daniel told the
truth.
CALM   Rest a hand on your tummy and whisper,
‘Heavenly Father, help me be honest.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father loves a heart that tells the truth, even with no prize!

Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did you tell a hard truth with nothing
at all to gain from it? Tell about it.
2. How do you make honesty safe for your
child? Tell about a moment they told you
something hard.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor refuse a
reward to stay honest? Describe that quality
in them.
