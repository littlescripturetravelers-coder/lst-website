Week 3 · Night 2
The Secret in the Night
King Nebuchadnezzar had a dream, but he could not remember it. He told his wise men, “Tell me my dream!” No one could.
Daniel asked the king for a little time. He asked his three friends to pray with him.
That night, Heavenly Father showed Daniel the king’s dream. Daniel thanked Him. Heavenly Father knows everything, even things hidden in the dark.
In the dream, a little stone was cut out without any hands. It grew into a mountain that filled the whole earth! Daniel said it was like Heavenly Father’s kingdom, which will never end.
When something is hard, you can pray like Daniel. Your family can pray with you too. Heavenly Father loves to help.

Week 3 · Night 2 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Curiosity & Discovery
“Look and see — what could it be?”
Scout fits tonight because Daniel went looking for an answer only Heavenly Father could
give. In Scout’s world today: Scout cannot find his blue sock anywhere. He stops hunting,
sits on the rug, and asks Heavenly Father to help him remember. Then he thinks of the
laundry basket, and there it is! Just like Daniel praying with his friends before the answer
came, Scout learned that asking Heavenly Father first is the best kind of looking.

Week 3 · Night 2 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Close your eyes and fold your hands the way
Daniel and his friends did. Now open your eyes
slowly, like morning light coming into a dark
room. Can you show me your quiet asking hands?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The king forgot his dream and felt upset.
Daniel and his friends needed an answer.
NAME   That stuck, need-to-know feeling is
called puzzled. Your mind might think, ‘I
can’t figure it out.’ Your body might feel a
scrunchy forehead and tight shoulders.
FIND   When did a question keep spinning in
your head?
FORK   Daniel could worry all alone, or pray with
his friends. Daniel chose to pray with his
friends.
CALM   Fold your hands on your chest and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, please help
me understand.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father knows every answer, and He loves to help when we pray!

Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did an answer come only after you
finally stopped and prayed? Tell about it.
2. How do you teach your child to ask Heavenly
Father before solving everything alone? Tell
about a time you prayed together.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor turn to prayer
before big decisions? Describe that quality in
them.
