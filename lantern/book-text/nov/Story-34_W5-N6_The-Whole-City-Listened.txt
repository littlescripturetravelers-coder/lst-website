Week 5 · Night 6
The Whole City Listened
Heavenly Father spoke to Jonah a second time. “Go to Nineveh,” He said. This time, Jonah went!
Nineveh was so big, it took three days to cross. Jonah called out, “In forty days, Nineveh will be broken down!”
The people believed Heavenly Father. They stopped doing wrong things. They stopped eating for a while. They wore scratchy sackcloth to show they were sorry.
Even the king got down from his throne. He took off his fancy robe. He sat in ashes, a way people showed they were sorry. Heavenly Father saw them change, so He kept their city safe.
Heavenly Father gave Jonah and Nineveh a second chance. He gives you second chances too. Tonight, thank Him for letting you try again.

Week 5 · Night 6 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Courage & Faith
“Big heart, brave start!”
Beau fits tonight because obeying the second time takes courage too. In Beau’s world
today: Beau was asked to put his boots away, and he did not. Later he thought about it,
walked back down the hall all by himself, and set them side by side in the closet. Nobody
had to ask again. Just like Jonah went when Heavenly Father asked a second time, Beau
learned that it is never too late to turn back.

Week 5 · Night 6 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Sit up tall and proud, like a king on a high throne.
Now climb slowly, slowly down and sit low on
your blanket instead. Can you show me how a
heart comes down off its throne?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The people of Nineveh had done wrong
things, and they felt very sorry.
NAME   That I-wish-I-hadn’t feeling is called
sorry. Your mind might think, ‘I want to
fix it.’ Your body might feel droopy
shoulders and a heavy heart.
FIND   When did you feel sorry and want to
make it right?
FORK   They could keep doing wrong, or turn
back to Heavenly Father. The whole city
chose to turn back.
CALM   Turn your body toward the door and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, help me turn
back to You.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father always gives another chance to a heart that turns back to Him!

Week 5 · Night 6 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What did Jonah do when he was asked a
second time?”
“What did the king do when he heard the
message?”
“What did Beau go back to put away?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did a second chance change the
direction of your life? Share that turning point
with your child.
2. How do you offer your child another try
without shame? Tell about a moment they
turned back on their own.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor change course
later in life than anyone expected? Describe
that quality in them.
