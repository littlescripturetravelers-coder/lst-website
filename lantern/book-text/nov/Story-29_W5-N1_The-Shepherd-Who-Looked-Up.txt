The Shepherd Who Looked Up
Amos was a herdman. A herdman takes care of animals, like sheep. Amos also picked figs from trees. He was not a prophet.
One day, Amos was following his sheep. Heavenly Father picked Amos. He said, “Go and tell my people my words.” So Amos went!
The people had stopped looking for Heavenly Father. Some of them were unkind to poor people.
Heavenly Father told them, “Look for me, and you will live.” Amos said to look for the One who made the stars. He made a little group called the seven stars. He made a big group called Orion.
The same stars shine over your house tonight. Heavenly Father made every one. When you pray, you are looking for Him. Tonight you can say, “Thank You for the stars.”

Week 5 · Night 1 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Curiosity & Discovery
“Look and see — what could it be?”
Scout fits tonight because seekers keep looking until they find. In Scout’s world today:
Scout lost his little blue marble under the couch. He looked and looked, cheek flat on the
rug, until his fingers closed around it, and he sat up glowing. Just like Amos told the
people to seek Heavenly Father, Scout learned that the ones who keep looking are the
ones who find.

Week 5 · Night 1 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lie back and open both hands on your blanket like
you are catching starlight. Now curl your fingers
closed, holding tight to what you found. Can you
show me your seeking hands?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Amos was just a herdman, yet Heavenly
Father picked him. Being picked feels
special.
NAME   That picked-for-something-big feeling is
called being wanted. Your mind might
think, ‘Me? Really?’ Your body might feel
tall and tingly in your chest.
FIND   When has someone picked you for an
important job?
FORK   Amos could say, “I am only a herdman,”
or he could go. Amos chose to go.
CALM   Open one hand toward the window and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, I will look for
You.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father made the stars, and He is always there when you look for Him!

Week 5 · Night 1 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What did Amos take care of before he was
a prophet?”
“Who did Heavenly Father tell the people
to seek?”
“What did Scout find under the couch?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did Heavenly Father ask something of
you while you felt too ordinary for it? Share
that story with your child.
2. How do you help your child keep looking
when something feels lost or hard? Tell
about a time they kept trying.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor keep seeking
Heavenly Father through plain, quiet days?
Describe that quality in them for your child.
