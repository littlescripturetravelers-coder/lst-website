Week 2 · Night 1
The Sky Opened Wide
Ezekiel was a prophet. A prophet is
someone Heavenly Father talks to, so he
can teach the people. The army of
Babylon had taken Ezekiel’s people far
from home.
One day, by a river in that faraway land,
the sky opened up. Heavenly Father
showed Ezekiel a vision. A vision is a
picture from Heavenly Father that a
prophet sees.
Ezekiel saw a shining throne, a king’s chair, and
Someone glowing on it, with bright light all
around.
Around the light was a rainbow, just like the
one you see in the clouds after it rains.
Everyone had seen a rainbow, so Ezekiel used it
to tell people how bright and beautiful it was.
He bowed down low.
Heavenly Father’s light is beautiful and kind.
Tonight you can lie still and feel glad that He is
so wonderful.

Week 2 · Night 1 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Curiosity & Discovery
“Look and see — what could it be?”
Scout fits tonight because a seeker’s whole heart opens wide when he sees something
wonderful. In Scout’s world today: the rain stopped and a rainbow bent over the yard.
Scout pressed his nose to the cold window and stood so still he forgot to blink. Then he
ran and tugged Mom’s hand to come and see. Just like Ezekiel bowing low by the river,
Scout learned that wonder is a quiet way of loving Heavenly Father.

Week 2 · Night 1 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lie back and open your arms wide, wide, like the
sky opening up. Now let warm, soft light settle all
over you. Can you show me how big the sky
opened for Ezekiel?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Ezekiel saw bright light and a rainbow, so
beautiful he bowed down low.
NAME   That big, quiet, wonderful feeling is called
awe. Your mind might think, ‘Wow, that is
so beautiful!’ Your body might feel very
still, with wide-open eyes.
FIND   When did you see something so beautiful
you went very still?
FORK   Ezekiel could hurry past it, or stop and
look with wonder. Ezekiel chose to bow
with wonder.
CALM   Open both hands on your blanket and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, You are
wonderful.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father’s light is bright, beautiful, and kind!

Week 2 · Night 1 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“Where was Ezekiel when the sky opened
up?”
“What was all around the bright light?”
“What did Scout see out the window after
the rain?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When has something in nature stopped you
and felt holy? Share that moment with your
child.
2. How do you make room for wonder on a busy
family evening? Tell about a time you
stopped to look together.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor have a gift for
noticing beauty? Describe that quality in
them.
