Week 2 · Night 4
The Shepherd Finds His Sheep
A shepherd takes care of the sheep. He finds them food and keeps them safe.
The leaders of Israel should have cared for the people, like good shepherds. But they did not. The people were like lost sheep, scattered on a cloudy, dark day.
Heavenly Father told the prophet Ezekiel, “I myself will look for my sheep. I will find them.” His people were His sheep.
He promised to bring the lost ones home to green grass. He would wrap up the hurt ones, like a bandage on a scraped knee. He would make the weak ones strong. Many years later, Jesus said, “I am the good shepherd.”
You are one of Heavenly Father’s little lambs. He knows your name. Snuggle in and feel safe tonight. The Good Shepherd takes care of you.

Week 2 · Night 4 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Compassion & Tenderness
“Warm hugs, warm hearts!”
Willow fits tonight because going after someone who feels left out is pure tenderness. In
Willow’s world today: a boy sat by himself at the edge of the playground with his hood
pulled up. Willow left the swings and sat down beside him. After a while she gave him
half of her crackers. Just like the Shepherd going to find His lost sheep, Willow learned
that Heavenly Father wants nobody left alone.

Week 2 · Night 4 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Curl up small and round like a little lost lamb.
Now feel gentle hands lift you and carry you all
the way home to bed. Can you show me how a
lamb rests when it is safe?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The sheep were lost and scattered on a
dark day, and they felt alone.
NAME   That all-alone, far-away feeling is called
lonely. Your mind might think, ‘Nobody is
with me.’ Your body might feel small,
with a lump in your throat.
FIND   When have you felt left out or all alone?
FORK   The Shepherd could leave His sheep
scattered, or go find them. He promised
to find every one.
CALM   Hug your knees close, then let them go,
and whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, thank
You for finding me.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father looks for every lost sheep, and He never gives up!

Week 2 · Night 4 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“Who said He would search for the lost
sheep?”
“What did the Shepherd promise to do for
the hurt sheep?”
“What did Willow give the boy on the
playground?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When were you the one who was found?
Share that time with your child.
2. How do you notice the child who has drifted
to the edge? Tell about a time you went after
one of your own.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor reach out to
people others forgot? Describe that quality in
them.
