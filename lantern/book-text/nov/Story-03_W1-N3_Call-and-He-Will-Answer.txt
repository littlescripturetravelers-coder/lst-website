Call and He Will Answer
The king did not like Jeremiah’s
messages, so his guards kept Jeremiah
shut in a courtyard. A courtyard is an
open yard with walls all around.
Jeremiah could not go out.
But Heavenly Father’s words can go
anywhere, even over walls. A
message came to Jeremiah right there:
“Call unto me, and I will answer thee.”
To call means to talk to Heavenly Father, like
we do when we pray. He promised He would
answer.
“I will shew thee great and mighty things,
which thou knowest not,” He said. Shew is an
old way to say show. He would show Jeremiah
wonderful things he did not know yet.
You can pray from right where you are, even
snuggled in your bed. Fold your arms tonight
and feel how close He is.

Week 1 · Night 3 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Curiosity & Discovery
“Look and see — what could it be?”
Scout fits tonight because asking is how he finds things out, and this story is Heavenly
Father saying, “Call unto me.” In Scout’s world today: he wonders why the moon seems to
follow the car. He does not just guess and give up. He asks Poppa Allen at dinner, and the
answer makes Scout’s mouth fall open. Just like Jeremiah being promised great things if
he would call, Scout learned that asking opens the door to wonder.

Week 1 · Night 3 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Cup your hands around your mouth like a little
trumpet and call one quiet word into the dark.
Now be very still and listen with your whole self.
Can you show me how a listening person sits?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Jeremiah was shut inside walls and could
not go out. He felt stuck.
NAME   That stuck, can’t-get-out feeling is called
trapped. Your mind might think, ‘I can’t
do anything.’ Your body might feel tight
in your arms and legs.
FIND   When did you feel stuck and wish you
could get out?
FORK   Jeremiah could feel stuck and alone, or
pray. Jeremiah prayed, and Heavenly
Father answered.
CALM   Cup your hands near your heart and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, I am calling to
You.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
When you call to Heavenly Father, He hears you and answers!

Week 1 · Night 3 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“Where was Jeremiah shut in?”
“What did Heavenly Father promise when
Jeremiah called?”
“Who did Scout ask about the moon?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did an answer come only after you
finally asked out loud? Share how that
answer reached you.
2. How do you show your child that questions
are welcome? Tell about a time you loved
their question.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor pray as though
they truly expected an answer? Describe that
quality in them.
