Week 1 · Night 4
A Field for Tomorrow
An army from Babylon was all around
Jerusalem, and Jeremiah was kept in the
guards’ courtyard. It was a very hard
time, and soon the army would take the
land.
Heavenly Father told Jeremiah, “Your
cousin Hanameel will come and ask you
to buy his field.” A field is land where
food grows. Soon Hanameel came, just
as Heavenly Father said.
Jeremiah weighed out seventeen pieces of
silver to pay. He signed the paper, sealed it
shut, and had people watch so everyone knew
it was true.
His helper Baruch put the paper in a clay jar so
it would last many days. Why? Heavenly Father
promised that someday people would own
houses and fields here again.
Buying the field was a way to show hope.
When tomorrow feels hard, you can do one
hopeful thing today, like planting a seed.

=== page 3 ===
Week 1 · Night 4 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Courage & Faith
“Big heart, brave start!”
Beau fits tonight because doing the brave thing before you can see why is his kind of
courage. In Beau’s world today: he plants one tiny seed in a paper cup, and nothing
happens for days. He waters it every morning anyway, even when the dirt still looks
empty. Just like Jeremiah buying a field in a hard year, Beau learned that faith means
doing good things before you see them grow.

=== page 4 ===
Week 1 · Night 4 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Hold out one hand and count seventeen silver
pieces into it, one at a time. Now fold your fingers
over them and hold them tight. Can you show me
how to hold a promise?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   It was a very hard time, and buying a field
seemed to make no sense.
NAME   That nothing-good-is-coming feeling is
called discouraged. Your mind might think,
‘Why even try?’ Your body might feel slow
and droopy, like your feet are heavy.
FIND   When did something feel so hard you did
not want to try?
FORK   Jeremiah could give up on tomorrow, or
trust Heavenly Father’s promise. Jeremiah
bought the field.
CALM   Press your palms together and whisper,
‘Heavenly Father, I trust You with
tomorrow.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father’s promises are true, so we can do hopeful things even on hard days!

Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“Who asked Jeremiah to buy a field?”
“Where did Jeremiah put the paper to keep
it safe?”
“What did Beau plant in a paper cup?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did you act on a promise you could not
yet see fulfilled? Share what that choice cost
you.
2. How do you show hope to your child in a
hard season? Tell about a time they saw you
choose it.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor work for a
future they never got to see? Describe that
quality in them.
