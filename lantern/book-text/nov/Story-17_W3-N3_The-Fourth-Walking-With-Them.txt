The Fourth Walking With Them
King Nebuchadnezzar made a giant
golden statue and told everyone to bow
down to it. Shadrach, Meshach, and
Abed-nego would not. They prayed to
and served only Heavenly Father.
“Heavenly Father is able to save us,”
they said, “and even if He does not, we
still will not bow.” So the king had them
put into a furnace. A furnace is a huge,
very hot oven.
Then the king jumped up. “Did we not put
in three men?” he asked. “I see four men
walking in the fire, and they are not hurt!”
The king said Heavenly Father had sent His
angel to save them. Not one hair was
burned. They did not even smell like smoke.
Heavenly Father was with them in the hard
place. Snuggle into your blanket and feel
that He is near you tonight too.

Week 3 · Night 3 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Courage & Faith
“Big heart, brave start!”
Beau fits tonight because the three friends stood brave together, and bravery is Beau’s
whole heart. In Beau’s world today: the lights go out in a summer storm, and his little
sister starts to cry. Beau holds her hand in the dark and tells her, “We are not alone,” until
the lamps hum back on. Just like the friends finding someone walking beside them in the
fire, Beau learned that Heavenly Father is with us in the dark.

Week 3 · Night 3 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Get up on your knees in bed, tall and brave like the
three friends. Hold up four fingers and wiggle the
fourth one, because someone came to be with
them. Can you show me your four walking fingers?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The three friends would not bow to the
statue. They felt brave, standing together.
NAME   That strong, standing-tall feeling is called
courage. Your mind might think, ‘I will do
what is right.’ Your body might feel a
straight back and steady, planted feet.
FIND   When did you do the right thing while
others watched?
FORK   They could bow down to stay safe, or
stand true. The three friends chose to
stand true.
CALM   Plant your feet flat and whisper,
‘Heavenly Father, please stay right beside
me.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father is with you, even in the hardest places!

Week 3 · Night 3 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What would the three friends not do?”
“How many men did the king see walking?”
“What did Beau do when the lights went
out?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did you stand alone for something true
and find you were not alone? Tell about it.
2. How do you build courage in your child
without shaming their fear? Tell about a
moment.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor hold their
ground at real cost? Describe that quality in
them.
