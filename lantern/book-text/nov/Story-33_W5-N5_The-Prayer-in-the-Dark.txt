The Prayer in the Dark
Jonah was inside the great fish. It was dark in there. So Jonah talked to Heavenly Father in prayer.
Jonah prayed, “I called to You in trouble, and You heard me.” He remembered the deep sea. Seaweed had wrapped around his head.
Jonah had run away from Heavenly Father. Now he was turning back to Him. “When I felt weak,” Jonah said, “I remembered the Lord.”
Jonah said thank you to Heavenly Father. He promised to do what he had said. Then Heavenly Father spoke to the fish. The fish put Jonah out onto dry land.
No place is too dark or too far for Heavenly Father to hear you. Tonight you can fold your arms and talk to Him, just like Jonah.

Week 5 · Night 5 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wisdom & Reﬂection
“Pause and ponder — what a wonder!”
Wren fits tonight because a quiet heart can hear what matters most. In Wren’s world
today: Wren had a loud, tangled morning, so she took her blanket behind the big chair and
sat down. She stayed there a long, still minute. Then she came out, found Mom, and said,
“I’m sorry.” Just like Jonah prayed in the quiet dark, Wren learned that still places help us
remember what matters.

Week 5 · Night 5 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Close your eyes and fold your hands, small and
quiet like Jonah in the dark. Keep your hands very,
very still. Can you show me your quietest praying
hands?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Jonah was far down in the dark, and he
felt far from home.
NAME   That far-away, need-help feeling is
called lost. Your mind might think, ‘How
do I get back?’ Your body might feel
small and droopy all over.
FIND   When have you felt far away and
wanted someone near?
FORK   Jonah could stay quiet in the dark, or
pray. Jonah prayed, and Heavenly Father
heard him.
CALM   Fold your hands beneath your chin and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, thank You for
hearing me.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
No place is too dark or too deep for your prayers to reach Heavenly Father!

Parent Prompt
Question for the Child

“Where did the fish put Jonah?”

Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did a prayer from your lowest place
reach Heavenly Father? Share what you
remember about that moment.
2. How do you make room for stillness in your
child’s noisy day? Tell about a moment quiet
changed their heart.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor pray through a
long, hard season? Describe that quality in
them for your child.
