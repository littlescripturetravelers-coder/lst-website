Break Up the Hard Ground
Most people in Hosea’s land were
farmers, so Heavenly Father used a
farming word. “Break up your fallow
ground,” He said.
Fallow ground is dirt that sat too long
without being planted. It gets hard
and crusty, and little seeds cannot
grow there at all. A farmer has to dig
it up and make it soft again first.
“Sow to yourselves in righteousness, reap in
mercy,” He said. Sow means plant, and reap
means gather. Righteousness means doing
right. Plant good things, and kindness grows
back.
“It is time to seek the Lord,” He said, “till He
come and rain righteousness upon you.” The
farmer digs, and Heavenly Father sends the
rain.
Hearts can get hard, too. Tonight, feel your
heart go soft, ready for good things to grow.

Week 4 · Night 4 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Courage & Faith
“Big heart, brave start!”
Beau fits tonight because starting over takes steady courage. In Beau’s world today:
Beau’s block tower fell over three times, and his chest went tight and hot. He set the
blocks down, took a breath, and started the bottom row again, wider this time. Just like
the farmer who breaks up the hard ground before planting, Beau learned that the hard,
quiet work comes first.

Week 4 · Night 4 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Make two fists, hard as packed dirt. Now open
them slowly, fingers soft and loose like dug-up soil
ready for seeds. Can you show me your hands
going from hard to soft?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The ground was hard and crusty, and
nothing good could grow until it was dug.
NAME   That tight, stuck, grumpy feeling is called
frustrated. Your mind might think, ‘This is
too hard!’ Your body might feel hot and
tight in your chest.
FIND   When did something feel so hard you
wanted to stop trying?
FORK   They could leave the ground hard, or dig
it soft. Heavenly Father asked them to
break it up.
CALM   Unclench your fists on the blanket and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, please make
my heart soft.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
When our hearts get soft, Heavenly Father sends His goodness like rain!

Week 4 · Night 4 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What is fallow ground?”
“What did Heavenly Father promise to send
down like rain?”
“What did Beau start over, wider this time?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. Where has your own heart gone hard and
needed softening? Share honestly with your
child what helped.
2. How do you praise effort, not just results?
Tell about a time your child quietly tried
again.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor do patient
work that no one clapped for? Describe that
to your child.
