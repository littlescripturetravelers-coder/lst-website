Week 3 · Night 7
The Ancient of Days
Daniel had a special dream. He saw thrones, big royal chairs, set in place. Then the Ancient of Days sat down. Ancient of Days is a name that means very, very old.
His clothes were white as snow. His hair was like clean, white wool from a sheep. A stream of fire flowed out before him.
Thousands and thousands stood before him, more than anyone could count.
Then Daniel saw One like the Son of Man coming with the clouds of heaven. He was given a kingdom that will never end. Jesus called Himself the Son of Man, so Daniel was seeing Jesus, our King!
Daniel wrote his dream down so we could know it too. Jesus is King forever, and He loves you. Tonight, picture soft white clouds and whisper, “Thank You for Jesus.”

Week 3 · Night 7 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Curiosity & Discovery
“Look and see — what could it be?”
Scout fits tonight because Daniel saw something wonderful, and joyful discovery is
Scout’s delight. In Scout’s world today: Scout finds a rainbow on the hallway wall, made
by sunshine through the window glass. He runs and takes Mom by the hand so she can
see it too. They stand there quietly and look. Just like Daniel seeing the bright clouds and
sharing his dream, Scout learned that wonder grows bigger when you share it.

Week 3 · Night 7 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Open both arms as wide as clouds and turn your
palms up toward the light. Now let them float
down slow, slow, slow onto your blanket. Can
you show me how gently clouds come down?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Daniel saw something so bright and
beautiful that his heart felt still.
NAME   That big, quiet, wow feeling is called
awe. Your mind might think, ‘That is so
beautiful!’ Your body might feel very still,
with wide eyes and an open mouth.
FIND   When did something beautiful make you
stop and stare?
FORK   Daniel could forget the dream, or
remember it and share it. Daniel wrote it
down for us.
CALM   Open your palms and whisper, ‘Heavenly
Father, thank You for Jesus.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus is a King forever, and His kingdom will never end!

Testimony
1. When did one glimpse of glory reset your

2. How do you make room for awe in your
