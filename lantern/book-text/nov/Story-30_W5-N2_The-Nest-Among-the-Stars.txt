Week 5 · Night 2
The Nest Among the Stars
Obadiah was a prophet. He told about the people of Edom. They were unkind to their own family, the people of Israel.
Edom lived high up in the mountains. They made their homes in rocky cliffs. They felt safe and proud. They said, “Who can bring me down?”
Proud means thinking you are better than others and need no one.
Heavenly Father said, “You may fly high like an eagle. You may make your nest among the stars. But I will bring you down.” Eagles build nests way up high. Edom thought they were too high for anyone. Heavenly Father wanted them to come down and be humble.
Humble means knowing we need Heavenly Father and each other. Tonight you can say, “Heavenly Father, I need You. Please help me be kind.”

Week 5 · Night 2 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wisdom & Reﬂection
“Pause and ponder — what a wonder!”
Wren fits tonight because a quiet, thinking heart can notice pride before it grows. In
Wren’s world today: Wren built the tallest block tower and told her cousin he could not
touch it. Then she saw his sad face and felt something turn inside her. She scooted the
whole tower to the middle. Just like Obadiah warned the proud people, Wren learned that
being close is better than being high.

Week 5 · Night 2 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Stretch both arms up high, like a nest among the
stars. Now bring them down slowly and wrap
them around yourself in a warm hug. Can you
show me how good close feels?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Edom lived up high and felt proud, like
no one could ever reach them.
NAME   That I’m-better-than-you feeling is
called pride. Your mind might think,
‘Mine is the best.’ Your body might feel
puffed up, with your chin lifted high.
FIND   When have you wanted something all to
yourself instead of sharing?
FORK   They could stay proud up high, or come
down and be humble. Heavenly Father
warned them to come down.
CALM   Lower both hands into your lap and
whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, help my heart
be humble.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father loves a humble heart that stays close to others!

Week 5 · Night 2 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“Where did the people of Edom build their
homes?”
“Where do eagles build their nests?”
“What did Wren do with her tall tower?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did pride quietly lift you away from
someone you loved, and what brought you
back? Share that with your child.
2. How do you help your child come down
gently instead of shaming them? Tell about a
moment you watched them soften.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor stay humble
when they could have looked down on
others? Describe that quality in them.
