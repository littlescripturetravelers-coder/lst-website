Week 4 · Night 7
The Years Given Back
Joel’s land had a hard time. Locusts,
bugs like big grasshoppers, came in
huge crowds and ate the plants, and
the people were hungry. Then
Heavenly Father made a happy
promise. “Fear not, O land,” He said.
“Be glad and rejoice.”
He promised soft green grass on the
hills and sweet fruit on the trees again.
“I will restore to you the years that the
locust hath eaten,” He said. Restore means
give back. There would be plenty of wheat,
and they would eat and be full.
He promised more: “I will pour out my spirit
upon all flesh.” All flesh means all people.
Long after, Jesus’ friend Peter said this came
true when the Holy Ghost came.
Tonight, feel happy and safe. Heavenly
Father gives good things back.

=== page 3 ===
Week 4 · Night 7 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Curiosity & Discovery
“Look and see — what could it be?”
Scout fits tonight because he is the first to spot new green after a long gray time. In
Scout’s world today: Scout’s little garden pot sat brown and bare all winter, and he
almost stopped checking it. One cold morning he found one curled green shoot and
shouted so loud that Margo woke up. Just like the green Heavenly Father promised after
the hungry time, Scout learned that Heavenly Father gives good things back.

Week 4 · Night 7 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lie flat and still like bare winter ground. Now
wiggle your fingers up out of the blanket like new
green shoots pushing through. Can you show me
the grass growing back?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The people were hungry and sad. Then
Heavenly Father promised that green
would come back.
NAME   That bright, it-is-getting-better feeling is
called hope. Your mind might think, ‘Good
things are coming!’ Your body might feel
light and bouncy.
FIND   What is something you waited a long, long
time for?
FORK   They could stay afraid, or be glad.
Heavenly Father told them to be glad and
rejoice.
CALM   Stretch your fingers wide like new leaves
and whisper, ‘Heavenly Father, thank You
for giving back.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father gives back what was lost and fills our hearts with good things!

Week 4 · Night 7 · Prompt · Experience
Parent Prompt
Question for the Child
“What ate the plants in Joel’s land?”
“What did Heavenly Father promise to give
back?”
“What did Scout find in his garden pot?”
Parent Experience
Testimony
1. What season felt lost to you, and how has
Heavenly Father given something back?
Share that with your child.
2. How do you help your child keep hoping
through a long wait? Tell about a time it paid
off.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor rebuild after
losing much? Describe that hopefulness to
your child.
