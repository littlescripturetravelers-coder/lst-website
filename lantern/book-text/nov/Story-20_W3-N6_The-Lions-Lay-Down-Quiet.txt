Week 3 · Night 6
The Lions Lay Down Quiet
Some men tricked King Darius into making a new rule. For thirty days, no one could pray to anyone but the king.
But Daniel loved Heavenly Father. He opened his window toward Jerusalem, his faraway home. He knelt and prayed three times a day.
The men told the king. The sad king could not change his rule. Daniel was put down with the lions. The king hoped Heavenly Father would save him.
The king could not sleep. In the morning he hurried back. “Daniel!” he called. Daniel answered, “Heavenly Father sent His angel and shut the lions’ mouths.” Daniel was not hurt!
Daniel kept praying, even when it was hard. Tonight you can kneel and pray, just like Daniel. Heavenly Father watches over you too.

Week 3 · Night 6 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Courage & Faith
“Big heart, brave start!”
Beau fits tonight because Daniel kept praying when it was hard, and steady faith is Beau’s
strength. In Beau’s world today: some new kids at the park say praying is silly. Beau still
bows his head over his crackers, calm and kind, then asks if anybody wants to share. Just
like Daniel kneeling to pray three times a day, Beau learned that he never has to hide his
love for Heavenly Father.

Week 3 · Night 6 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Kneel on your bed and bow your head three times,
slow and gentle, like Daniel. Now curl up like a big,
sleepy lion and let your whole body go soft. Can
you show me your sleepiest lion?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   A new rule said not to pray, but Daniel
kept praying and trusted Heavenly Father.
NAME   That steady, trusting feeling is called
faith. Your mind might think, ‘Heavenly
Father will help me.’ Your body might feel
calm and still, like a deep, slow breath.
FIND   When did you keep doing right, even
when it was hard?
FORK   Daniel could stop praying to stay safe, or
keep praying and trust. Daniel chose to
keep praying.
CALM   Bow your head and whisper, ‘Heavenly
Father, thank You for staying near me.’
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father hears our prayers and watches over us all night long!

Parent Prompt
Question for the Child

Parent Experience
Testimony
1. When did faithfulness cost you something,
and you chose it anyway? Tell about it.
2. How do you keep family prayer steady in
busy seasons? Tell about a moment it
mattered.
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor pray with
unshakable regularity? Describe that quality
in them.
