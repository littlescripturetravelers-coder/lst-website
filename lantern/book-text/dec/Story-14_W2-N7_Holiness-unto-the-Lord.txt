Week 2 · Night 7
Holiness unto the Lord
Zechariah told of a wonderful day when the Lord would be King over all the earth. On that day, even the little bells on horses would carry words of love and honor for Him: 'Holiness unto the Lord.'
The people might have expected only the temple to be holy. But Zechariah's picture reached right out into the busy streets and ordinary homes.
Even the pots used for everyday cooking would be set apart for the Lord. Bells, bowls, and cooking pots could all remind people to honor Him.
Our own ordinary things can be used for good too. We can carry a bag for someone, stir soup for a neighbor, or share what we have. Look around your room tonight. Is there something you can use to show love for Jesus tomorrow?

Week 2 · Night 7 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wren stood on a little stool beside Mom and stirred a pot of soup. The warm smell filled the kitchen. They were making dinner for a sick neighbor. 'Can cooking be something we do for Jesus?' Wren asked. Mom smiled. 'When we do it with love, it can.' Wren kept stirring carefully. A simple kitchen pot could help her serve the Lord, just as Zechariah had taught.

Week 2 · Night 7 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Pretend to ring a tiny bell, ding, ding, ding. Now stir a little pot slowly. Can you show me how you do everyday things with love?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Zechariah told how even bells and pots will be holy, and the people felt wonder and joy.
NAME That soft, wide-eyed feeling is called wonder. Your mind might think, ‘Everyday things can be for Jesus!’ Your body might feel tingly and still, with big shining eyes.
FIND When have you done an everyday job with love?
FORK We could think everyday things do not matter, or do them for the Lord. Zechariah taught that everyday things can be holy.
CALM Hold your hands like a tiny bell, very still. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help my whole day be for You.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Everyday things can be holy when we do them for the Lord!
