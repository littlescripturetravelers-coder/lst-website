Heavenly Father’s Lamb
Long ago, Abraham trusted that God would provide a lamb. Later, the Lord asked His people to choose a perfect lamb for a sacred offering. Those lambs helped point their hearts toward Jesus, the perfect Son of Heavenly Father.
On the night Jesus was born, shepherds watched their sheep in the fields near Bethlehem. The night was quiet until a bright light suddenly shone around them.
An angel appeared, and the shepherds were afraid. But the angel brought wonderful news: do not fear! The Savior had been born. They could go and find Him.
Years later, John the Baptist pointed to Jesus and called Him 'the Lamb of God.' Jesus came to save us through His loving sacrifice. He is Heavenly Father's perfect Lamb. Tonight, rest as snug as a little lamb and remember how much the Savior loves you.

Week 4 · Night 4 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Beau visited the neighbor, and a big fluffy dog bounded toward him. Beau took a quick step back. The neighbor smiled. 'Don't worry. He's friendly.' Beau reached out one careful hand, and the dog gave it a gentle lick. Beau laughed. He thought of the shepherds who were frightened until an angel brought peaceful news about Jesus. Good words can help a worried heart feel calm.

Week 4 · Night 4 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Cup your hands like you are holding a tiny lamb. Now look up, like the shepherds seeing the bright light. Can you show me your brave, wide-open eyes?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT The shepherds felt afraid, but the angel’s good news helped them not be afraid.
NAME That strong, standing-tall feeling is called courage. Your mind might think, ‘I do not need to be afraid.’ Your body might feel steady, with strong shoulders.
FIND When have you felt brave after someone said, “Do not be afraid”?
FORK The shepherds could stay afraid, or listen to the angel. They listened and heard about Jesus.
CALM Hug your knees like a little lamb. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for Jesus, Your perfect Lamb.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus is Heavenly Father’s perfect Lamb, sent to save me!
