Week 1 · Night 1
Under the Fig Tree
Imagine a quiet afternoon under a wide, leafy fig tree. Grapes grow nearby, and a family rests in the cool shade. No one is hurrying. No one is afraid.
Long ago, the prophet Micah shared this peaceful picture of a day to come. Every family would have a place to rest, with food to eat and room to feel at home.
Micah promised that no one would make them afraid. The people would walk with the Lord and follow His ways.
What a beautiful promise! The Lord wants His children to know His peace. As you curl up in bed tonight, imagine the soft leaves of that fig tree rustling overhead. You can pray to Heavenly Father and feel His love close to you, right where you are.

Week 1 · Night 1 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wren carried her apple slices to the big tree in her backyard. She sat down where the branches made a cool, shady roof. A little breeze brushed her cheeks, and the leaves whispered above her. Wren smiled and took a bite. She felt peaceful in her own little resting place. Micah's promise helped her remember that Heavenly Father wants His children to feel safe and at peace.

Week 1 · Night 1 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Stretch your arms up wide like a big fig tree. Now bring them down softly and hug yourself like cool shade. Can you show me your safe, cozy face?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Micah told of families resting under their own fig trees, feeling safe and happy.
NAME That cozy, nothing-to-fear feeling is called feeling safe. Your mind might think, ‘I am home, and all is well.’ Your body might feel soft, warm, and still.
FIND When have you felt cozy and safe at home?
FORK The people could live in worry, or walk in the name of the Lord. Micah chose to walk with the Lord.
CALM Snuggle under your blanket like cool shade. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for my safe home.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
The Lord wants me to feel safe and at peace!
