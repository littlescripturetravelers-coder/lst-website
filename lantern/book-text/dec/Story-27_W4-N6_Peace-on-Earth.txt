Peace on Earth
Long before the first Christmas, Isaiah promised that a special child would be born. One of His beautiful names would be the Prince of Peace. Isaiah was telling the people about Jesus.
Then came the night of His birth. An angel told shepherds the joyful news: the Savior had been born in Bethlehem. Before they could even finish wondering, something wonderful happened in the sky.
Suddenly, many angels praised Heavenly Father together. They praised God and spoke of peace on earth. The heavens seemed full of joy!
Jesus came to show us the way of love and peace. When we follow Him, we can choose gentle words, forgive, and help others feel welcome. Tonight, take one slow breath and rest your hands. The Prince of Peace can bring peace to your heart, even when the world feels busy.

Week 4 · Night 6 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow drew a big red heart on a Christmas card. She wrote a thank-you for the mail carrier and waited by the door. When he came, she handed him the card. 'Thank you!' she said brightly. The mail carrier's face lit up with a smile. Willow felt warm and glad inside. The angels had spoken of peace on earth, and Willow had shared a little peace through kindness.

Week 4 · Night 6 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lift your arms up high, then float them down slowly, soft as a feather. Rest them gently on your blanket. Can you show me your peaceful arms?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT The angels joyfully praised Heavenly Father, because Jesus, the Prince of Peace, was born.
NAME That calm, quiet, all-is-well feeling is called peace. Your mind might think, ‘Everything is okay.’ Your body might feel soft and still, with slow breaths.
FIND When have you felt calm and peaceful inside?
FORK We can choose grumpy words, or kind words. Jesus, the Prince of Peace, taught us to choose kindness.
CALM Breathe in slowly, then breathe out softly. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for peace.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus is the Prince of Peace, and He brings peace to my heart!
