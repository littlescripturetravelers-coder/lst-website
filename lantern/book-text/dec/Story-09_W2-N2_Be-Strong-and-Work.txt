Week 2 · Night 2
Be Strong, and Work
The people worked hard to rebuild the Lord's temple. Some of the older people remembered the first temple, with all its beauty. When they looked at the new building, it seemed so small. Was their work even enough?
The Lord knew their hearts. Through the prophet Haggai, He gave them courage: 'Be strong ... and work: for I am with you.'
The temple was not finished yet. There were still stones to lift and work to do. But the builders were not alone. The Lord promised His Spirit would stay with them, and He promised peace.
Maybe you have a big job that looks hard at first. You can begin with one little part, then another. Heavenly Father can help you be brave and keep going. Tonight, stretch your arms strong, then rest. He is with you.

Week 2 · Night 2 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Beau spread his puzzle pieces across the table. There were so many! He tried two pieces, but they did not fit. 'I can't do it,' he sighed. Then Beau took a breath and found one blue corner. Click! Another piece fit beside it. Soon he was smiling and trying again. Big things can happen one small step at a time. With the Lord's help, Beau could keep working.

Week 2 · Night 2 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Sit up tall and make strong arms. Now hug yourself, like the Lord saying, “I am with you.” Can you show me your brave, happy face?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT The new temple looked small, but the Lord said, “I am with you,” and the people felt strong.
NAME That strong, keep-going feeling is called courage. Your mind might think, ‘I can do it.’ Your body might feel tall, with strong arms and a steady heart.
FIND When have you kept working even when a job felt too big?
FORK The people could stop because the house looked small, or keep working. They were strong and kept building.
CALM Hug your pillow and breathe slowly. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for being with me.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Be strong and keep going, for the Lord is with you!
