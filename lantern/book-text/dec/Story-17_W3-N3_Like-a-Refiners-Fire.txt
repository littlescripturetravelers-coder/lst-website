Like a Refiner’s Fire
Have you ever seen silver shine? Long ago, a worker would heat silver until it melted. He watched it carefully as dark bits rose to the top. Little by little, he took them away. Soon the silver was clean and bright.
Malachi taught that the Lord would be like a careful worker with silver. Jesus does not give up on us when we need help growing better.
Malachi also spoke of strong soap that could wash wool clean. Both pictures helped people understand the Savior's love. He can help us change when we make wrong choices.
We do not need to stay the same forever. As we repent, say sorry, and keep trying, Jesus helps our hearts become cleaner and brighter. Tonight, imagine a little silver spoon catching the lamplight. Think of the gentle ways Jesus is helping you grow.

Week 3 · Night 3 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Grandma handed Wren an old silver spoon. It looked dark and dull. Together they rubbed it with a soft cloth. Rub, rub, rub! At last a bright patch began to shine. Wren leaned close and saw her smile reflected in the silver. 'Look, Grandma!' she cried. The shining spoon reminded her of Malachi's words. Jesus can help us change, grow, and become brighter on the inside.

Week 3 · Night 3 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Rub your hands together like you are washing with soap. Scrub, scrub, scrub! Now hold them up, clean and shining. Can you show me your sparkly clean hands?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Malachi taught that Jesus makes people clean and shining, like silver, and that gave them hope.
NAME That fresh, clean, start-again feeling is called feeling forgiven. Your mind might think, ‘I can try again.’ Your body might feel light, like after a warm bath.
FIND When have you felt fresh and new after saying sorry?
FORK The people could keep their wrong ways, or come back to the Lord. Malachi taught them the Lord said, “Return unto me.”
CALM Rub your hands slowly, then rest them still. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me be clean and shining.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus helps me be clean and shining, like silver!
