Week 3 · Night 7
Elijah Will Come
At the end of his book, Malachi shared a promise from the Lord. Before a great day to come, Elijah the prophet would return. He would help turn the hearts of children toward their fathers and mothers, and the hearts of families toward one another.
It was a promise about love stretching across generations: from grandparents to parents, from parents to children, and even to family members who lived long ago.
Many years later, in 1836, Elijah appeared to Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery in the Kirtland Temple. He restored sacred priesthood keys so families could be sealed together in the Lord's temples.
Because of Jesus Christ and His restored priesthood, families can be joined for eternity. Tonight, picture the faces of your parents, grandparents, and people you love. Heavenly Father's plan gives families a wonderful reason to hope.

Week 3 · Night 7 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wren sat beside an old family photo album. On one page was a little girl with a bright smile. 'Who is she?' Wren asked. 'Your great-grandma when she was little,' Mom said. Wren leaned close. 'She looks like me!' Suddenly, someone from long ago felt close to her heart. Wren wanted to hear more family stories. She was beginning to feel the love Malachi promised would join generations together.

Week 3 · Night 7 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Put both hands on your heart. Now turn slowly to one side, then the other, like turning your heart toward your family. Can you show me your turning heart?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Elijah came as Malachi promised, so families could be together forever and feel full of love.
NAME That close, heart-to-heart, together feeling is called belonging. Your mind might think, ‘We belong together.’ Your body might feel warm and snug, all over.
FIND When have you felt that you belong with your family?
FORK Joseph Smith could keep this to himself, or teach it. Joseph taught it, so families everywhere could be together forever.
CALM Hug your pillow like a family hug. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You that families can be forever.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Families can be together forever, because Elijah came!
