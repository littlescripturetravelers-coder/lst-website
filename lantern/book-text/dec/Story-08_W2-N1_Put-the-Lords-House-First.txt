Week 2 · Night 1
Put the Lord’s House First
After many years away, the people of Jerusalem had finally come home. They were busy fixing roofs and building houses. But the Lord's temple still stood unfinished.
The prophet Haggai asked the people to stop and think. They had made time for their own homes. Could they make time for the Lord's house too? It was time to put Him first.
The Lord told them to go to the mountains, bring wood, and build His house. This time the people listened. They gathered what they needed and got to work. Then the Lord gave them a wonderful promise: 'I am with you.'
You can make room for Heavenly Father in your day too. Before toys, games, or busy plans, you can pause to pray. Even a small choice to put the Lord first can bring peace to your heart.

Week 2 · Night 1 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wren rushed toward her toy box one sunny morning. She reached for a favorite toy, then stopped. She remembered something she wanted to do first. Wren knelt beside her bed and said her morning prayer. When she finished, she smiled and hurried back to play. The toys were still there. Wren had found a happy way to put Heavenly Father first, just as the people in Haggai's day learned to do.

Week 2 · Night 1 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Pretend to carry wood from the mountain. Stack it, one, two, three. Can you show me how you build the Lord’s house?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Haggai asked the people to stop and think, and they felt ready to put the Lord first.
NAME That quiet, thinking-it-over feeling is called thoughtfulness. Your mind might think, ‘What should come first?’ Your body might feel still, with a calm, slow breath.
FIND When have you stopped to think about what to do first?
FORK The people could keep fixing their own houses, or build the Lord’s house. They obeyed and built the Lord’s house.
CALM Fold your hands and be very still. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me put You first.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
When I put the Lord first, He is with me!
