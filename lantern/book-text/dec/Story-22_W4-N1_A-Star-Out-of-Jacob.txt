Week 4 · Night 1
A Star Out of Jacob
Long before Jesus came to earth, a man named Balaam spoke of a star that would rise from Jacob's family. His words pointed toward a coming King.
Then, when Jesus was born, wise men far to the east saw a special star. They knew a King had come. So they set out on a long journey, watching the sky and looking for Him.
At last, the star led them to the place where young Jesus was. Their hearts filled with joy. They bowed before Him and offered precious gifts: gold, frankincense, and myrrh.
The wise men had traveled a long way because finding Jesus mattered more than anything they carried. You can seek Him too. Each time you pray, read His words, or choose kindness, you take another step toward Him. Tonight, imagine that shining star lighting your way.

Week 4 · Night 1 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
At the Christmas party, Scout found a little paper star on the wall. Then he spotted another, and another! He followed the stars down the hallway until he reached the last one. 'I found it!' he cheered. Scout remembered the wise men who kept following their star until they found Jesus. Seeking the Savior can be an adventure too, one faithful little step at a time.

Week 4 · Night 1 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Hold one hand up high like a shining star. Now walk your other fingers across your blanket, following it. Can you show me how the wise men followed the star?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT The wise men followed the star and found Jesus, and they felt great joy.
NAME That bright, bouncy, I-found-it feeling is called joy. Your mind might think, ‘We found Him!’ Your body might feel light and sparkly, with a big smile.
FIND When have you felt joy after finding something special?
FORK The wise men could stay home, or follow the star a long way. They followed it and found Jesus.
CALM Hold your hands over your heart like a gift. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for Jesus.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus is the bright Star, and wise people still seek Him!
