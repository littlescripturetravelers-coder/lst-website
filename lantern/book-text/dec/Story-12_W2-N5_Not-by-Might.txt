Not by Might
Zechariah saw a golden lampstand shining with seven little lights. Two olive trees stood beside it, sending oil through golden pipes to keep the lamps burning. What an amazing sight!
Zechariah wondered what it meant. The Lord had a message for Zerubbabel, the man helping to rebuild the temple. The work was big, and the people needed help.
The Lord's message was simple and strong: 'Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit.' The temple would be finished through the Lord's help, not by strength alone.
You may be small, and some days your job may seem very big. But Heavenly Father has not asked you to do everything alone. You can pray, listen for the Holy Ghost, and take one good step at a time. His Spirit can help you do big things.

Week 2 · Night 5 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Beau's first day in a new class made his tummy feel wobbly. He stood by the door and watched the other children playing. What if no one wanted to be his friend? Beau whispered a little prayer. As he breathed slowly, he felt calmer. Then he smiled and waved hello to another child. He did not need to be the biggest or bravest. The Lord's Spirit could help him.

Week 2 · Night 5 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Make big muscle arms. Now let them go soft, and hold your hands open like a little lamp. Can you show me your quiet, glowing light?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Zerubbabel had a big job, but the Lord’s Spirit would help, and he could feel sure.
NAME That calm, safe-inside feeling is called trust. Your mind might think, ‘The Lord will help me.’ Your body might feel calm, with soft shoulders and a steady heart.
FIND When have you asked Heavenly Father for help with something big?
FORK Zerubbabel could try in his own strength alone, or trust the Lord’s Spirit. With the Lord’s help, he finished the Lord’s house.
CALM Open your hands softly on your blanket. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, please help me by Your Spirit.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
The Lord’s Spirit helps me do big things!
