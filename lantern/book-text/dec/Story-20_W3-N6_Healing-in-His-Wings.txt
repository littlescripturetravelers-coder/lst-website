Healing in His Wings
Have you ever watched the morning sun rise? First a thin line of gold appears. Then warm light spreads across the sky and wakes the world.
Malachi promised that the 'Sun of righteousness' would rise with healing in His wings. He was speaking about Jesus Christ. Like sunlight reaching across a chilly field, the Savior brings hope, light, and healing to His children.
Malachi pictured little calves being let out of their stalls. Imagine them springing into the sunshine, kicking up their heels and racing through the grass. What joy!
Jesus can bring that kind of hope to our hearts. Sometimes healing takes time, and sometimes hard things stay with us. Still, He loves us and can give us strength and peace. Tonight, wiggle your feet like a happy little calf, then rest in the Savior's love.

Week 3 · Night 6 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Scout had been sick with a cold, and he was tired of staying inside. One morning he finally felt better. He opened the door and stepped into the sunny yard. The warm light touched his face. Then he ran across the grass, laughing and jumping like a little calf! Scout was thankful for feeling better. The sunshine helped him remember that Jesus brings light, hope, and healing.

Week 3 · Night 6 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Spread your arms wide like warm sunshine. Now kick your feet under the blanket like a happy calf running out to play. Can you show me your sunny, happy feet?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Malachi said Jesus would bring healing, and people would feel glad, like calves let out to play.
NAME That bright, bouncy, free feeling is called joy. Your mind might think, ‘I feel so much better!’ Your body might feel light and springy, like you could jump.
FIND When have you felt bouncy and glad after feeling better?
FORK The people could turn away from the Lord, or love and honor Him. Malachi said those who honor Him would feel His healing light.
CALM Feel your warm blanket like sunshine on you. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for Jesus, my healing light.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus is like the warm sun, and He helps me feel better!
