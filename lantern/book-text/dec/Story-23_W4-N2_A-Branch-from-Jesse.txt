Week 4 · Night 2
A Branch from Jesse
Imagine an old tree stump standing quietly in a field. Its tall branches are gone. It looks as though nothing new could ever grow there. But one morning, a tiny green shoot pushes up beside it. New life!
Long before Jesus was born, the prophet Isaiah used a picture like this. He promised that a special Branch would grow from the family of Jesse, the father of King David.
Isaiah said the Spirit of the Lord would rest upon that promised King. He would have wisdom and understanding.
Many years later, an angel came to Mary with amazing news. She would have a son named Jesus, and He would be the promised King from David's family. The little Branch Isaiah spoke about pointed to our Savior. Heavenly Father had not forgotten His promise. He sent Jesus because He loves us.

Week 4 · Night 2 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wren stopped beside an old stump on a walk through the park. 'Look!' she said. Right next to the rough wood, a tiny green shoot was growing. She crouched down to see its small leaves. Something new had begun where she expected nothing at all. Wren smiled and remembered Isaiah's picture of a Branch. Heavenly Father kept His promise by sending Jesus Christ.

Week 4 · Night 2 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Curl up small, like a quiet root under the ground. Now slowly stretch one arm up, like a little green branch growing. Can you show me your growing branch?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Isaiah told of a new branch from an old stump, and the people felt hope.
NAME That warm, looking-forward feeling is called hope. Your mind might think, ‘Something good is coming.’ Your body might feel light, with a soft, happy chest.
FIND When have you felt hope that something good was coming?
FORK The people could think Jesse’s family was all done, or trust the Lord’s promise. Isaiah trusted, and Jesus came.
CALM Stretch your fingers up like a tiny branch. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for sending Jesus.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus is the little Branch from David’s family, sent with love!
