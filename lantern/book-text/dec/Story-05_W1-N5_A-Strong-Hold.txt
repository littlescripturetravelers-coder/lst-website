Week 1 · Night 5
A Strong Hold
Long ago, when danger came near a town, families could hurry into a strong stone tower. Inside its thick walls, they had a safe place to wait until the trouble passed.
The prophet Nahum used that picture to teach a wonderful truth. 'The Lord is good,' he said. The Lord is like a strong hold in a hard time. He is someone we can trust when things feel frightening.
Nahum also promised that the Lord knows those who trust Him. He knows each one by name. He knows you too.
Hard days may still come, but you never need to face them without Heavenly Father's love. You can whisper a prayer when you are worried, scared, or sad. Tonight, tuck your blanket around your shoulders like the walls of a strong tower. Think of the Lord's goodness and rest.

Week 1 · Night 5 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
BOOM! Thunder shook the windows. Wren jumped and hurried across the room to Mom. Mom knelt and opened her arms. Wren climbed into the hug and listened as the rain pattered against the glass. The thunder was still outside, but she felt calmer. The Lord can be a safe place for her heart too. Wren could talk to Him whenever she felt afraid.

Week 1 · Night 5 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Press your palms together and stretch them up tall, like a strong tower. Hold very still and steady. Can you show me your strong, safe tower?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Nahum said the Lord is a strong, safe place, and the people could feel safe and trusting.
NAME That steady, I-can-lean-on-Him feeling is called trust. Your mind might think, ‘The Lord knows me and will help.’ Your body might feel slow breaths and a relaxed tummy.
FIND When have you run to someone who kept you safe?
FORK In trouble, the people could feel all alone, or run to the Lord. Nahum taught them to trust the Lord.
CALM Wrap your blanket snugly around you. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, You are my safe place.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
The Lord is good, and He keeps me safe like a strong tower!
