Week 3 · Night 2
I Will Send My Messenger
Long before Jesus was born, the Lord gave Malachi an exciting promise. He would send a messenger to help the people get ready for Him.
Imagine tidying a path before a very special guest arrives. The messenger's work would be a little like that. He would call people to turn their hearts toward God, so they would be ready to welcome the Savior.
Many years later, John the Baptist stood beside the Jordan River, teaching people to repent and follow the Lord. He baptized those who were ready to make a covenant with God. John helped prepare the way for Jesus, just as Malachi had promised.
You can get ready to follow Jesus too. You can pray, choose what is right, and show love to others. Tonight, think of one kind thing you will do when morning comes.

Week 3 · Night 2 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Grandma was coming to visit, and Beau could hardly wait! He scooped up his toys, swept the front step with his little broom, and peeked through the window. At last he saw her coming. Beau opened the door wide. 'Grandma!' he cheered. Getting ready had shown how much he loved her. Beau remembered that John helped people get ready for someone even more wonderful: Jesus.

Week 3 · Night 2 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Pretend to sweep a path with a little broom, swish, swish. Now open your arms wide to welcome a special guest. Can you show me your ready, happy face?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Malachi promised a messenger, and John helped people get ready for Jesus, full of hope.
NAME That eager, can’t-wait, getting-ready feeling is called excitement. Your mind might think, ‘Someone special is coming!’ Your body might feel bouncy, with a big smile.
FIND When have you gotten ready for someone special to come?
FORK John could stay quiet, or tell everyone to get ready. John bravely took the road of helping people get ready for Jesus.
CALM Fold your arms and take a slow breath. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help my heart be ready for Jesus.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
John helped people get ready for Jesus, and I can get ready too!
