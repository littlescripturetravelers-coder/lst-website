Week 1 · Night 6
Feet Like a Deer’s
Habakkuk looked ahead and knew hard days might come. What if the fig trees had no fruit? What if no grapes grew? What if the sheep were gone and the fields were empty?
Even then, Habakkuk made a brave choice. He would rejoice in the Lord. His joy did not have to disappear when life was difficult, because the Lord would still be his strength.
Habakkuk sang about a deer climbing a steep, rocky hill. Watch how its strong feet find a place to stand, one careful step at a time. Habakkuk trusted that the Lord would help him stand strong in hard places too.
Some things are difficult, even for brave people. You can ask Heavenly Father for strength and keep trying. Tonight, wiggle your toes like a deer finding its footing. The Lord can help you take the next step.
=== page 3 ===
Week 1 · Night 6 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Beau stood at the bottom of the big park hill. It looked so tall! He began to climb, but soon his legs felt tired. He stopped and looked at the top. 'Help me, Heavenly Father,' he whispered. Then he took another step, and another. At last he reached the top and lifted his arms with a happy shout. The Lord had helped Beau keep going, even when the climb felt hard.

Week 1 · Night 6 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lie back and make your feet climb the air: step, step, step, like a deer up a rocky hill. Can you show me your strong, sure feet?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Habakkuk faced hard times, but he felt glad and strong in the Lord.
NAME That brave, glad-anyway feeling is called joy. Your mind might think, ‘Even when it’s hard, the Lord helps me.’ Your body might feel a strong back and a warm smile.
FIND When have you kept going when something was hard?
FORK Habakkuk could give up and stay sad, or rejoice in the Lord. Habakkuk chose to rejoice.
CALM Wiggle your toes, then let them rest. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for making me strong.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Even in hard times I can be glad, because the Lord makes me strong!
