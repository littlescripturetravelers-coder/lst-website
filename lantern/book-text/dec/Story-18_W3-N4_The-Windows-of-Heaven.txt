The Windows of Heaven
In Malachi's day, families grew much of their own food. They gathered grain and other good things from their fields. The Lord asked them to give Him one part out of every ten. That gift was called tithing.
The food was brought to the Lord's storehouse, where it could be used for His work. Giving a part back was one way the people could show their trust in Him.
Then the Lord made a beautiful promise. He would open the windows of heaven and pour out blessings. Imagine rain falling on dry ground, filling the earth with life. The Lord's blessings can come in many ways, not just as money or things.
Children can give tithing too. If you have ten coins, you can give one back to the Lord. Tonight, count three blessings you are thankful for. Heavenly Father sees your loving choices.

Week 3 · Night 4 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Beau lined up ten shiny birthday coins on the table. He counted them once, then again. Beside him, Dad held a tithing envelope. Beau picked up one coin and slipped it inside. Giving it felt like a big choice, but he was glad he had done it. He wanted to show Heavenly Father that he trusted Him. The Lord had promised blessings to those who give faithfully.

Week 3 · Night 4 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Hold your hands up high like open windows. Now wiggle your fingers down slowly, like gentle rain falling on you. Can you show me how blessings pour down?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT The Lord promised to pour down blessings, and His people could feel trust and thanks.
NAME That full, warm, thank-you feeling is called gratitude. Your mind might think, ‘I have so many blessings!’ Your body might feel full and happy in your chest.
FIND When have you felt thankful for a blessing?
FORK The people could keep it all, or give back one part. Malachi asked them to give, and see the Lord’s blessings pour down.
CALM Rest your open hands on your blanket. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for pouring down blessings.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
When I pay my tithing, the Lord pours down blessings!
