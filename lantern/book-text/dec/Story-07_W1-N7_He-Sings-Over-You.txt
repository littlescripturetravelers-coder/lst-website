Week 1 · Night 7
He Sings Over You
The prophet Zephaniah shared a promise of a joyful day when the Lord would be with His people. He told them to sing with all their hearts. They would not need to be afraid, because the Lord would be near.
Imagine a whole city singing together! Children laugh, families smile, and the air fills with happy voices. Zephaniah wanted the people to look forward to the Lord's love and goodness.
Then he shared the sweetest part. The Lord would rejoice over His people with singing! It is a little like a mother singing a soft song to the baby she loves.
That is how tenderly the Lord loves His children. He is not far away or too busy to care. He knows you, and you matter to Him. As you close your eyes tonight, imagine a loving song surrounding you like a warm blanket.

Week 1 · Night 7 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow held her baby cousin close while the room grew quiet. She rocked gently and sang a soft little lullaby. The baby's eyes blinked slowly, then closed. Willow smiled down at the sleepy face in her arms. Her heart felt full of love. She thought about Zephaniah's promise that the Lord rejoices over His children. What a happy thought: He loves us so much!

Week 1 · Night 7 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Hug your arms around yourself like a soft, warm blanket. Now rock gently side to side, like a song. Can you show me your happy, loved smile?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Zephaniah said the Lord would rejoice and sing over His people, and they felt deeply loved.
NAME That warm, wrapped-in-love feeling is called feeling loved. Your mind might think, ‘The Lord is happy with me.’ Your body might feel warm all over, like a hug.
FIND When has someone sung you a sweet song?
FORK The people could stay afraid, or hear Zephaniah’s words, “Fear thou not.” Zephaniah taught them to be glad.
CALM Hum one soft note. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for loving me so much.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
The Lord is close to me, and He is so happy to love me!
