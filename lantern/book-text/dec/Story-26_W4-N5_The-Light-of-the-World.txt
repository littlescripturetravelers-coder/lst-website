The Light of the World
Long before Jesus was born, Isaiah promised that people walking in darkness would see a great light. He was looking forward to the Savior.
Then, on the night Jesus was born, shepherds watched their sheep beneath the dark sky. Suddenly, the glory of the Lord shone around them. Heaven's bright message had come: the promised Savior was here!
When Jesus grew up, He told the people, 'I am the light of the world.' Those who follow Him do not need to walk without hope. His love can guide us like a lamp on a dark path.
At Christmas, tiny lights glow in windows and twinkle on trees. Each one can remind us of Jesus. Tonight, look at one little light and remember that the Savior can help your heart shine too.

Week 4 · Night 5 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
On a Christmas drive, Scout pressed his face close to the car window. 'Look at that one!' he called. Red and gold lights twinkled along a roof. Farther down the street, a bright star shone above a tree. Mom smiled. 'They can remind us of Jesus.' Scout watched the lights glow against the dark sky. He wanted to remember the Savior whenever he saw Christmas lights.

Week 4 · Night 5 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Open and close your fingers like twinkly Christmas lights. Blink, blink, blink! Now hold them still and bright. Can you show me your shining lights?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT The people waited in the dark, then Jesus came like a great light, and they felt wonder.
NAME That wide-eyed, sparkly, wow feeling is called wonder. Your mind might think, ‘How beautiful!’ Your body might feel tingly, with big shining eyes.
FIND When have you felt wonder looking at beautiful lights?
FORK We can stay in the dark, or follow Jesus’s light. Jesus said those who follow Him will have light.
CALM Close your eyes and picture one soft, warm light. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for Jesus, my light.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Every Christmas light can remind me of Jesus, the Light of the World!
