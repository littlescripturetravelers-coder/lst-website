Week 1 · Night 4
Into the Deep Sea
Sometimes people make choices that hurt themselves or others. Micah knew that. He also knew something wonderful about the Lord: He loves to forgive.
Micah told the people that the Lord delights in mercy. When someone turns back to Him with a sorry heart, He welcomes them with love. Micah wanted everyone to remember that the Lord has compassion for His children, even when they have made mistakes.
Then Micah gave them a beautiful picture. He said the Lord would cast their sins into the depths of the sea. Imagine a little stone dropping into deep blue water. Down, down, down it goes, far beneath the waves.
Through Jesus Christ, we can repent, make things right, and be forgiven. We do not have to carry every wrong choice forever. Tonight, open your hands and let them rest. Jesus can help you begin again.

Week 1 · Night 4 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Beau wanted his friend's toy truck so badly that he grabbed it. His friend began to cry. Suddenly, the truck did not feel fun at all. Beau's tummy felt heavy. He walked back, handed over the truck, and said, 'I'm sorry.' His friend took it, and they played together again. Beau felt lighter. He knew that turning around, making things right, and asking Jesus for help was the brave thing to do.

Week 1 · Night 4 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Pretend to hold a heavy rock in your hands. Now throw it far, far away into the deep sea. Splash! Can you show me how light your hands feel now?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Micah told how the Lord forgives sins, and the people could feel light and happy again.
NAME That light, clean, fresh-start feeling is called relief. Your mind might think, ‘I am forgiven!’ Your body might feel loose shoulders and a big, happy breath.
FIND When have you felt better after saying sorry?
FORK The people could hide their wrong choices, or turn to the Lord. Micah turned to the Lord, who delights in mercy.
CALM Take a deep breath and let it out slowly. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for forgiving me.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
The Lord loves to forgive, and He throws my sins into the deep sea!
