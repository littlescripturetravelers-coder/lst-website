We Have Waited for Him
Long before the first Christmas, Isaiah looked toward the coming Savior. He pictured the happy words people would say: 'We have waited for him, and he will save us.'
Then, one wonderful night, angels told shepherds that Jesus had been born. When the angels left, the shepherds hurried toward Bethlehem. They could hardly wait to see the baby the Lord had promised.
They found Mary and Joseph, and baby Jesus lying in a manger. The shepherds were so happy that they told others what they had heard. Everyone marveled at their news. Mary held these precious moments close in her heart.
Heavenly Father loved the world so much that He gave us His Son. Jesus came to save us. Tonight, snuggle under your blanket and remember the best Christmas gift of all: our Savior, Jesus Christ.

Week 4 · Night 7 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Beau stood behind the curtain at the Christmas program. His tummy fluttered as he waited for his turn. Then he stepped forward, stood tall, and said his line: 'Jesus is born!' His family smiled. Beau smiled too. Like the shepherds in Bethlehem, he had shared joyful news about the Savior. The good news of Jesus is a wonderful gift to give.

Week 4 · Night 7 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Wiggle your feet fast, like shepherds hurrying to Bethlehem. Now stop and hold your arms out wide. Can you show me your happy, hurrying feet?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT The shepherds hurried to find baby Jesus, and they were so excited to tell everyone.
NAME That bubbly, can’t-wait, jumpy feeling is called excitement. Your mind might think, ‘Let’s go see!’ Your body might feel bouncy, with quick feet and a bright smile.
FIND When have you been so excited you could hardly wait?
FORK The shepherds could stay in the field, or hurry to see Jesus. They hurried, then shared the good news.
CALM Hold your hands together like a gift. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for giving us Jesus.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father gave us Jesus, the best Christmas gift of all!
