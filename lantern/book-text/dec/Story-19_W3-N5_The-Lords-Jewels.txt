The Lord’s Jewels
In Malachi's day, some people loved the Lord and talked about Him together. They remembered His goodness, even when others had forgotten. The Lord listened to their words.
A special book of remembrance was written so their faith would not be forgotten. Long before that, families such as Adam's had kept records too. Their stories helped children and grandchildren remember what mattered most.
Then the Lord gave those faithful people a beautiful name. He called them His jewels. Think of a little shining stone kept carefully in a treasure box. The Lord was telling His people how precious they were to Him.
You are one of His precious children too. He knows your name, your hopes, and the good in your heart. Tonight, think of one thing you love about Jesus. Your love for Him is a treasure worth remembering.

Week 3 · Night 5 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow held a tiny glass ornament as Dad hung Christmas decorations. She cupped it in both hands so it would not fall. 'Careful,' Dad said softly. 'That one is precious.' Willow nodded. Then Dad smiled. 'And you are even more precious to me, and to the Lord.' Willow looked at the ornament glowing among the lights. She remembered Malachi's beautiful words: Heavenly Father's children are His jewels.

Week 3 · Night 5 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Cup your hands together like you are holding a tiny jewel. Hold it close to your heart, very gently. Can you show me how you keep something precious safe?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT The people talked about the Lord, and He called them His jewels, so they felt precious.
NAME That treasured, cared-for, special feeling is called feeling precious. Your mind might think, ‘I matter to Heavenly Father.’ Your body might feel warm and glowing, like a hug.
FIND When have you felt special and treasured?
FORK The people could forget the Lord, or talk together about Him. Those good people took the road of remembering Him.
CALM Hold your cupped hands close to your heart. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You that I am precious to You.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
I am one of the Lord’s precious jewels!
