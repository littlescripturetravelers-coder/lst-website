Week 3 · Night 1
I Have Loved You
The people in Malachi's day had forgotten how much the Lord loved them. Some had stopped thinking about Him at all. Then the Lord sent Malachi with a gentle, powerful message: 'I have loved you.'
The people wondered how the Lord had shown His love. Malachi reminded them that the Lord's name would be great across the earth, from the first light of morning to the last glow of evening.
Picture the sun rising over the hills. It shines on homes, fields, and roads. Later, that same sun slips below the horizon far away. The Lord's love reaches across the whole world too.
Jesus Christ loves His children in every place, through every morning and every night. That includes you. Tonight, wrap your arms around yourself like a warm hug and remember: you are always precious to Him.

Week 3 · Night 1 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow found her little brother sitting alone with a frown. He did not want to play, and his eyes looked sad. Willow sat beside him. 'I love you,' she whispered. 'Every single day.' He leaned against her shoulder, and soon a tiny smile appeared. Willow smiled too. She had shared the kind of love the Lord spoke about through Malachi, a love that does not disappear on grumpy days.

Week 3 · Night 1 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Stretch your arms up high like the morning sun. Slowly bring them down like the sun setting. Then wrap them around you. Can you show me a big, loved hug?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT The Lord told His people, “I have loved you,” and He wanted them to feel loved.
NAME That warm, safe, cared-for feeling is called feeling loved. Your mind might think, ‘Someone loves me.’ Your body might feel warm and soft, right in your heart.
FIND When have you felt really, really loved?
FORK The people could forget the Lord’s love, or remember it. Malachi took the road of sharing the Lord’s loving message.
CALM Put your hand on your heart. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for loving me.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
The Lord loves me, from sunrise to sunset!
