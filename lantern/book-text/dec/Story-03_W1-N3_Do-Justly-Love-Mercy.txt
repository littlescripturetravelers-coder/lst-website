Week 1 · Night 3
Do Justly, Love Mercy
What would make the Lord happy? Long ago, Micah asked the people to think about that question. Some people brought animals as gifts to the Lord. Would a young calf be enough? Would thousands of sheep be enough?
Micah wanted them to understand something even more important. The Lord was looking for goodness in their hearts, not just something they could carry in their hands.
Micah taught the people to do what is right, to be fair, to show mercy, and to walk humbly with God. That means choosing kindness, forgiving others, and listening when Heavenly Father guides us.
Think of a friend who needs a turn, or someone who needs a gentle word. One small, kind choice can show love for the Lord. Tonight, choose one kind thing you can do tomorrow. Heavenly Father will help you.

Week 1 · Night 3 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow had built a tall tower of blocks. Then her little brother bumped it, and crash! Blocks scattered across the floor. Willow's cheeks felt hot. She wanted to shout. Instead, she took a slow breath and looked at his worried face. 'It's okay,' she said. 'Let's build it together.' Soon they were stacking blocks side by side. Willow had chosen the kind, forgiving way Micah taught.

Week 1 · Night 3 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Make two fingers walk slowly across your blanket. Walk softly, like you are walking right beside Heavenly Father. Can you show me your gentle, humble walk?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Micah taught that the Lord wants fair, kind hearts, and the people could feel glad.
NAME That soft, kind-to-others feeling is called mercy. Your mind might think, ‘I will be kind, even when it’s hard.’ Your body might feel a warm heart and gentle hands.
FIND When have you been kind to someone who needed it?
FORK Micah could think gifts were enough, or listen to the Lord. Micah listened and taught fair, kind hearts.
CALM Fold your gentle hands. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me be fair, kind, and humble.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Be fair, love being kind, and walk with Heavenly Father!
