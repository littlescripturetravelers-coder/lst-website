Week 1 · Night 2
Little Bethlehem
Bethlehem was a little town nestled among the hills. Sheep wandered in the fields, and families lived in simple homes. It did not look like the place where a great King would be born.
But long before the first Christmas, the prophet Micah told the people to watch that little town. One day, a wonderful ruler would come from Bethlehem. Micah was telling them about Jesus.
Years and years passed. Then a bright star appeared, and wise men traveled to find the newborn King. When they asked where He would be born, the teachers remembered Micah's words: Bethlehem!
And that is where baby Jesus had been born. The little town was part of Heavenly Father's great plan. Tonight, hold up one little finger and smile. You may be little, but you matter very much to Heavenly Father too.

Week 1 · Night 2 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Scout held out his dark mitten as snow began to fall. Plink! One tiny snowflake landed right on his hand. He leaned close and saw six delicate points, sparkling like a little star. It was so small, but so beautiful! Scout thought of tiny Bethlehem and the wonderful Savior born there. Little things can be important to Heavenly Father. That includes Scout, and that includes you.

Week 1 · Night 2 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Curl up small like a little town on a hill. Now slowly stretch up tall, like a bright star over Bethlehem. Can you show me how big you can grow?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Micah said a great King would come from little Bethlehem, and the people felt hope.
NAME That glad, something-good-is-coming feeling is called hope. Your mind might think, ‘Jesus is coming!’ Your body might feel a warm smile and a fluttery tummy.
FIND When have you waited for something wonderful?
FORK The teachers could forget Micah’s words, or remember them. They remembered and said, “In Bethlehem!”
CALM Hold up one little finger. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for sending baby Jesus.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus was born in little Bethlehem, just as Micah said!
