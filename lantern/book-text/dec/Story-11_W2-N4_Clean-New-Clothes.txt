Clean New Clothes
The Lord showed Zechariah a remarkable picture. Joshua, the high priest, stood before an angel wearing dirty clothes. They looked so stained and worn.
Then the angel gave a command. The dirty clothes were taken away, and Joshua was dressed in clean ones. What a change! The Lord was showing that He could take away sin and help His people become clean again.
The Lord also promised that someone special would come, like a fresh green branch growing from an old tree. That promised Branch pointed to Jesus Christ.
Because of Jesus, we can turn away from wrong choices and begin again. We can say sorry, try to make things right, and ask for His help. Imagine slipping into soft, clean clothes after a muddy day. Jesus can help your heart feel clean and new too.

Week 2 · Night 4 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow had played outside until mud covered her hands, knees, and shoes. Mom helped her wash up and laid out clean pajamas. Willow slipped them on and gave a little twirl. 'I feel brand new!' she laughed. Then she remembered Joshua's clean clothes. Jesus can do something even more wonderful: when we turn to Him, He can help us become clean on the inside too.

Week 2 · Night 4 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Pretend to take off a dirty, heavy coat and drop it away. Now slip on a soft, clean shirt. Can you show me how clean and new you feel?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT The angel took away Joshua’s dirty clothes and gave him clean ones, so Joshua could feel clean.
NAME That light, clean-and-new feeling is called peace. Your mind might think, ‘I can start fresh.’ Your body might feel light, like a heavy bag was put down.
FIND When have you said sorry and felt better inside?
FORK We could hide our wrong choices, or bring them to the Lord. Joshua stood before the Lord’s angel, and he was made clean.
CALM Rub your hands softly, like washing. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, please help me feel clean and new.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus can make us clean and new inside!
