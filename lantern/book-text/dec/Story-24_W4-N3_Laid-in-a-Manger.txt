Laid in a Manger
Long before the first Christmas, Micah promised that a great ruler would come from little Bethlehem. Many years later, Mary and Joseph traveled to that very town. They had come because the ruler of Rome wanted the people to be counted.
Mary was going to have a baby soon. The roads had been long, and Bethlehem was crowded with travelers. It was time for her child to be born.
That night, baby Jesus was born. Mary wrapped Him snugly in cloths and laid Him in a manger, a little feeding place for animals. There was no room for them in the place where travelers stayed.
The promised Savior had arrived, not in a grand palace, but in a humble resting place. He came because Heavenly Father loves His children. Tonight, snuggle your pillow close and remember: there is always room to welcome Jesus in your heart.

Week 4 · Night 3 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Grandma's house was full of Christmas visitors. Every bed seemed taken, and Willow's little cousin looked sleepy. Willow scooted over on the sofa and lifted the edge of her blanket. 'There's room here,' she said. Her cousin curled up beside her. They felt warm and cozy together. Willow remembered Mary, Joseph, and baby Jesus in Bethlehem. Love can make room, even when a house feels full.

Week 4 · Night 3 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Hold your arms like you are cradling a tiny baby. Sway slowly, soft and gentle. Can you show me how softly you would hold baby Jesus?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Baby Jesus was born in little Bethlehem, and Mary wrapped Him with gentle love.
NAME That soft, warm, snuggly feeling is called love. Your mind might think, ‘I want to keep you safe.’ Your body might feel warm all over, with gentle arms.
FIND When have you felt love for someone small?
FORK Mary and Joseph could give up when the inn was full, or make a cozy place. They laid Jesus in a manger.
CALM Wrap your blanket snug, like soft baby cloths. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for baby Jesus.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus was born in little Bethlehem, and there is room for Him in my heart!
