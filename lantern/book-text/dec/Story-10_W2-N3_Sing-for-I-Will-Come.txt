Sing, for I Will Come
One day, the prophet Zechariah saw a man carrying a long measuring line. 'Where are you going?' Zechariah asked. The man was going to measure Jerusalem.
In those days, people built walls around their cities to keep danger away. Zechariah might have expected another high stone wall. But the Lord had something more wonderful to promise His people.
The Lord said He would be like a wall of fire around Jerusalem, protecting His people with His own power. He promised to be with them and invited them to sing for joy.
We look forward to the day Jesus Christ comes again, just as the prophets teach. While we wait, we can draw close to Him by following His ways. Tonight, think of His bright, protecting love and let your heart be glad.

Week 2 · Night 3 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Scout spotted Dad holding a measuring tape. 'What are you measuring?' he asked. Dad stretched the tape across the wall. 'Your room,' he said. 'I'm making a new shelf for your books.' Scout held one end while Dad checked the number. The little project made Scout curious, just like Zechariah's measuring line. Asking good questions can help us learn about the Lord's wonderful promises.

Week 2 · Night 3 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Stretch your arms way out wide, like a measuring line. Now make a big circle around you, like the Lord keeping you safe. Can you show me your big safe circle?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT Zechariah learned the Lord would come and live with His people, and they felt happy enough to sing.
NAME That bright, singing feeling is called joy. Your mind might think, ‘Jesus wants to be with me!’ Your body might feel bouncy, with a big smile.
FIND When have you felt so happy that you wanted to sing?
FORK Zechariah could stay quiet, or ask about what he saw. He asked, and he learned wonderful news.
CALM Hug yourself softly and smile. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You that Jesus will come again.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Sing and be happy, for Jesus will come and live with His people!
