Week 2 · Night 6
Streets Full of Children
Once, Jerusalem had been broken, and many families had gone far away. But the Lord gave Zechariah a picture of happier days ahead.
Imagine the streets filled with neighbors again. Grandmothers and grandfathers sit outside, resting with their walking sticks. The city is peaceful enough for them to stay and enjoy the sunshine. The Lord promises to be with His people.
And listen! There are children laughing in the streets. Little feet patter over the stones as boys and girls play together. Young and old have a place to belong. What a lovely city!
Zechariah's picture shows a place full of kindness and peace. We can help make our own homes feel that way. Maybe tomorrow you can hold a grandparent's hand, play kindly with a friend, or help someone who needs you.

Week 2 · Night 6 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow walked beside Grandpa on the path near the pond. He moved slowly with his cane, so Willow slowed down too. She held his hand while they watched ducks paddle across the water. One duck gave a funny little quack, and they both laughed. Willow loved being with Grandpa. Zechariah's happy city had room for grandparents and children together, and Willow's heart did too.

Week 2 · Night 6 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Wiggle your toes like boys and girls skipping and playing in the street. Now lie still like a grandpa resting. Can you show me your happy playing face?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT The Lord told of a city with old people and children playing, and they felt happy and safe.
NAME That warm, all-together feeling is called belonging. Your mind might think, ‘I am safe here.’ Your body might feel warm and snuggly, with a happy smile.
FIND When have you felt happy and safe with your family?
FORK The people could be unkind to each other, or show love and kindness. The Lord asked them to be kind to everyone.
CALM Hug your pillow like a family hug. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me make my home happy and kind.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
The Lord’s holy city is happy for grandparents and children too!
