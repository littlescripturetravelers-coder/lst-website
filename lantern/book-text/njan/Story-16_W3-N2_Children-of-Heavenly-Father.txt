Week 3 · Night 2
Children of Heavenly Father
Baby John grew up to be John the Baptist. Heavenly Father sent him with a job to do.
John’s job was to tell everyone about the Light, and the Light is Jesus. John was not the Light. He pointed to Jesus, like a friend pointing to the sunrise.
Jesus came into the world He had made. But many people did not know who He was. Some would not let Him in.
But some people believed in Jesus and let Him into their hearts. To all of them, Jesus gave a gift: power to become Heavenly Father’s children.
When we believe in Jesus and follow Him, we belong in Heavenly Father’s family. Tonight, say softly, “I am Heavenly Father’s child. I belong to Him.”

=== page 3 ===
Week 3 · Night 2 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow the Warm knows that the best feeling is belonging to a family that loves you. Tonight’s story says that everyone who believes in Jesus belongs to Heavenly Father’s family.
In Willow’s world today: Willow is new at church and does not know anyone. A girl walks over, takes her hand, and says, “Come sit with us.” Willow sits down in the row, and her heart feels warm. She belongs here.
Just like John taught, Willow learned that we all belong in Heavenly Father’s family.

Week 3 · Night 2 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Wrap your arms around yourself and squeeze, like a big family hug. Now hold the hand of the person next to you. Can you show me your belonging squeeze?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   John taught that everyone who believes in Jesus becomes Heavenly Father’s child. The people who believed felt like they belonged.
NAME   That you-are-part-of-us, I-have-a-place feeling is called belonging. Your mind might think, “This is my family.” Your body might feel warm and settled.
FIND   When did someone say, “Come sit with us,” and you felt like you belonged?
FORK   People could turn away from Jesus, or receive Him. Those who received Him became Heavenly Father’s children.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and put your hand on your heart. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You that I am Your child.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
When we believe in Jesus, we belong in Heavenly Father’s family!
