Jesus Heals Peter's Family
Jesus and His friends came to Capernaum, a town by the lake. On the Sabbath, the day of rest, Jesus taught in the meeting house.
The people were amazed. Jesus did not just repeat what others said. He taught like someone who truly knew Heavenly Father.
Then Jesus went to Simon Peter’s house. Peter’s wife’s mother was in bed, very sick with a fever. They told Jesus about her.
Jesus went to her, took her hand, and lifted her up. Right away the fever left her! She got up and helped serve them all.
That evening, people brought sick friends to the door. Jesus healed many, because He cares when people hurt. Tonight, pray for someone who feels sick or sad.

=== page 3 ===
Week 4 · Night 6 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow the Warm has a heart full of compassion. Jesus took a sick woman’s hand and lifted her up, and that is exactly the kind of caring Willow loves.
In Willow’s world today: Willow’s mom has a bad headache and lies down on the couch. Willow tiptoes over, brings her a cup of water, and gently pats her hand. “I’ll be quiet so you can rest, Mom.” Mom smiles with her eyes closed.
Just like Jesus, Willow learned that compassion means noticing someone who hurts and helping them.

Week 4 · Night 6 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Hold out your hand like you are gently helping someone sit up. Pull slowly, soft and careful. Can you show me your helping hand?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Peter’s wife’s mother was sick. Jesus held her hand and healed her. Jesus felt compassion for her.
NAME   That I-see-you’re-hurting, I-want-to-help feeling is called compassion. Your mind might think, “How can I help?” Your body might feel soft hands and a caring face.
FIND   When did you see someone who was sick or sad, and you wanted to help?
FORK   Jesus could rest after teaching, or help the sick woman. Jesus went right to her and lifted her up.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and make your hands soft. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me care for people who are hurting.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus cares when people are hurting, and we can care, too!

Testimony
1. Share a time Jesus lifted you up when you were sick, sad, or weak.
2. Who needs your compassion this week, and what could you do for them?
3. Was there a grandparent or ancestor who cared for the sick? Tell what they did.
