Week 2 · Night 1
No Room at the Inn
Caesar, a great ruler, made a rule. Everyone had to go to their family’s home town to be counted.
Joseph’s family came from Bethlehem, King David’s town. So Joseph and Mary went from Nazareth to Bethlehem. Mary’s baby was almost ready to come.
Bethlehem was very full. There was no room for them in the inn, where travelers stayed.
In Bethlehem, baby Jesus was born. Mary wrapped Him in soft cloths. She laid Him in a manger, the hay box animals eat from. It was a humble bed for the King of the world.
Jesus came in a simple, quiet way, close to everyone. Tonight, snuggle up and thank Heavenly Father for your cozy place.

=== page 3 ===
Week 2 · Night 1 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow the Warm knows that a warm heart makes any place cozy. The stable was not fancy, but with Mary, Joseph, and baby Jesus, it was full of peace and love.
In Willow’s world today: The power goes out at Willow’s house, and the rooms get dark. Willow helps Mom light a candle, and the family snuggles under one big blanket. Willow smiles and says, “It’s still cozy, because we’re all together.”
Just like Mary and Joseph, Willow learned that peace comes from love, not from fancy things.

=== page 4 ===
Week 2 · Night 1 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Curl up small under your blanket like baby Jesus wrapped in soft cloths. Breathe slow and easy. Can you show me how still and peaceful you can be?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   There was no room for Mary and Joseph. But in the quiet stable, with baby Jesus, they felt peaceful.
NAME   That calm, cozy, everything-is-okay feeling is called peaceful. Your mind might think, “I am safe.” Your body might feel soft and slow.
FIND   When did you feel peaceful, like snuggling in bed after a long day?
FORK   Mary and Joseph could grumble that there was no room, or be thankful for a quiet place. They made it a place of peace.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and let your whole body sink into bed. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for peace.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus came to a simple stable so He could be close to everyone, even you!

Parent Prompt
Question for the Child

Parent Experience
Testimony
1. Share a time your family felt peace in a simple or hard place.
2. What does it mean to you that Jesus was born in a stable, not a palace?
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor live simply but with great faith? Tell your child about their home.
