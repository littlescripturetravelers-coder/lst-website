Week 1 · Night 4
Mary's Thank-You Song
Mary stayed with Elisabeth. Her heart was so happy that her words came out like a song.
Mary said, “My heart tells how great the Lord is! I am so glad in Him who saves me. He looked kindly on plain, humble me.”
She said that He is strong and holy. He fills hungry people with good things. He lifts up the small and humble.
He keeps the promises He made long ago to Abraham. Now He was keeping them through Mary’s baby, Jesus. After three months, Mary went home.
You can sing a thank-you too. Tonight, name three gifts from Heavenly Father, like your bed and your family. Then hum, “Thank You!”

Week 1 · Night 4 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wren the Wonderer likes to pause and ponder. Mary stopped to think about all that Heavenly Father had done, and her thinking turned into a thank-you song.
In Wren’s world today: At bedtime, Wren looks around her room. She sees her soft blanket, her drawing on the wall, and Dad reading beside her. She whispers, “Heavenly Father gave me all of this.” Then she hums a little thank-you tune.
Just like Mary, Wren learned that when we count our blessings, our hearts fill up with thanks.

=== page 4 ===
Week 1 · Night 4 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Hold up one hand. For each finger, name something you are thankful for, and fold that finger down. Can you show me all five fingers folded into a thank-you fist?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Mary thought about everything Heavenly Father had done for her. She felt so thankful that she sang.
NAME   That warm, full, glowing feeling is called thankful. Your mind might think, “I have so many good things.” Your body might feel warm in your chest.
FIND   When did someone give you something that made you feel full of thanks?
FORK   Mary could keep her blessings to herself, or praise Heavenly Father out loud. Mary sang her thanks.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and smile. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for everything You give me.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
When we count our blessings, our hearts sing thank-you to Heavenly Father!
