Jesus Prays in the Morning
Jesus had helped people all day, until after the sun went down. Very early the next morning, while it was still dark, Jesus got up.
He went out to a quiet place, all by Himself. There He prayed to Heavenly Father.
Even Jesus, the Son of Heavenly Father, wanted to pray. He wanted to be close to His Father.
Soon Simon Peter and the others came looking for Him. “Everyone is looking for You!” they said. Jesus said, “Let us go to the other towns. I will teach there too. That is why I came.”
So Jesus went all through Galilee, teaching. You can pray like Jesus, in a quiet place. Tonight, kneel and talk to Heavenly Father.

Week 4 · Night 7 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Beau the Brave knows that even brave hearts need quiet time with Heavenly Father. Jesus got up early to pray before His big day, and that is where Beau’s bravery comes from, too.
In Beau’s world today: Beau wakes up before everyone else. The house is quiet and the sky is just turning pink. He kneels by his bed and whispers a prayer, just him and Heavenly Father. When he stands up, he feels calm and ready for the day.
Just like Jesus, Beau learned that quiet time with Heavenly Father fills us with calm and helps us know what to do.

Week 4 · Night 7 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lie still and listen. Can you hear the quiet? Breathe in slowly and out slowly, three times, like a still morning. Can you show me your quietest breathing?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Jesus got up early and prayed alone in a quiet place. He felt calm and ready for the day.
NAME   That still, quiet, settled feeling is called calm. Your mind might think, “All is well.” Your body might feel slow breathing and loose, heavy arms.
FIND   When have you felt calm and still, like early in the morning or right before sleep?
FORK   Jesus could sleep in after a long day, or get up early to pray. Jesus chose to pray.
CALM   Take three slow breaths. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, I want to be close to You, like Jesus.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Quiet time with Heavenly Father fills us with calm, just like Jesus!
