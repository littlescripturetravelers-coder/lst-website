Launch Out into the Deep
A big crowd pressed close to Jesus by the lake. Nearby, fishermen were washing their nets.
Jesus sat in Simon Peter’s boat and taught the people from the water. Then He said, “Go out to the deep water. Let down your nets.”
Peter said, “Master, we fished all night and caught nothing. But because You say so, I will.”
The net filled with so many fish that it began to break! Friends came to help, and both boats began to sink. Peter fell at Jesus’s knees, amazed. Jesus said, “Do not be afraid. From now on you will catch people.” They left everything and followed Him.
When something feels hard, try again like Peter. Tonight, say his words: “Because You say so, I will.”

=== page 3 ===
Week 5 · Night 4 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Scout the Seeker loves to try one more time and see what happens. Peter had fished all night and caught nothing, but he tried once more because Jesus said so. What he found amazed him.
In Scout’s world today: Scout has been trying to ride his bike without training wheels all afternoon. He keeps falling. Dad says, “One more try. I believe in you.” Scout pushes off, and this time he rides all the way down the sidewalk! He yells, “I did it!”
Just like Peter, Scout learned that when we try again because Jesus says so, amazing things can happen.

Week 5 · Night 4 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Pretend to pull a heavy net full of fish into the boat. Pull, pull, pull! Now let your arms flop down, amazed. Can you show me your amazed face?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Peter let down the net because Jesus said so, and it filled with fish. Peter felt amazed.
NAME   That whoa, I-can’t-believe-it feeling is called amazed. Your mind might think, “How did that happen?” Your body might feel your mouth drop open and your eyes go big.
FIND   When did something turn out so much better than you expected?
FORK   Peter could say, “We already tried,” or try again because Jesus said so. Peter let down the net.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and let your arms rest. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me trust Jesus and try again.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
When Jesus asks us to try, we can say yes, because He says so!
