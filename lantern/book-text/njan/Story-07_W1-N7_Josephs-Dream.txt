Week 1 · Night 7
Joseph's Dream
Joseph was a carpenter who made things from wood. He was going to marry Mary. Then he learned she was going to have a baby, and he was worried.
Joseph was a good, kind man. He did not want to hurt Mary. He thought about quietly ending the wedding plans.
Then an angel came to him in a dream. “Joseph, do not be afraid to marry Mary. Her baby is from the Holy Ghost.”
“Name Him Jesus, for He will save His people from their sins.” When Joseph woke up, he did just what the angel said.
When you feel worried, tell Heavenly Father. He can help you know what to do, just like He helped Joseph.

=== page 3 ===
Week 1 · Night 7 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Beau the Brave has a big heart and a brave start. Joseph was worried, but he was brave enough to obey the angel right away, and that is Beau’s kind of courage.
In Beau’s world today: Beau is worried about his first night sleeping at Grandpa’s house without Mom. His tummy feels tight. He says a prayer, then remembers Grandpa’s warm smile. He takes a big breath and says, “I can do this.”
Just like Joseph, Beau learned that when we give our worries to Heavenly Father, He helps us be brave.

=== page 4 ===
Week 1 · Night 7 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Scrunch your shoulders up to your ears like you are worried. Hold it. Now let them drop down, soft and easy. Can you show me your shoulders letting go?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Joseph did not know what to do about Mary. He felt worried, and then the angel helped him know.
NAME   That tight, twisty, what-if feeling is called worried. Your mind might think, “What if it goes wrong?” Your body might feel tight in your tummy.
FIND   When did you feel a what-if worry in your tummy?
FORK   Joseph could quietly walk away, or trust the angel and stay with Mary. Joseph obeyed Heavenly Father.
CALM   Breathe out slowly and let your tummy go soft. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, I give You my worry.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
When we give our worries to Heavenly Father, He shows us what to do!
