Week 5 · Night 1
Jesus Says No
After His baptism, the Spirit led Jesus into the desert. For forty days He ate nothing and grew hungry.
Satan came. “If You are the Son of God, turn these stones into bread.” Jesus said no: people need Heavenly Father’s words more than bread.
Satan took Him to the top of the temple. “Jump off! Angels will catch You.” Jesus said no. The scriptures say not to test the Lord.
Satan showed Him all the kingdoms of the world. “Bow down to me, and they are Yours.” Jesus said, “Go away, Satan! Worship only the Lord.” Then angels came to help Him.
Each time, Jesus remembered Heavenly Father’s words and said no. Tonight, practice saying, “No, I choose the right,” and feel strong.

=== page 3 ===
Week 5 · Night 1 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Beau the Brave says, “Big heart, brave start!” Jesus was hungry and tired, but He bravely said no to Satan three times. Saying no to something wrong is one of the bravest things you can do.
In Beau’s world today: Beau sees a cookie on the counter. Mom said no treats before dinner. Nobody is looking. His hand reaches out, then stops. He remembers Mom’s words. “No,” he says to himself, and he walks away. He feels strong.
Just like Jesus, Beau learned that remembering good words helps us say no to wrong things.

Week 5 · Night 1 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Reach your hand out slowly like you want to grab something. Now pull it back, hold up your hand, and say, “No!” Can you show me your strong no hand?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Satan tried to get Jesus to do wrong three times. Jesus felt tempted, but He said no every time.
NAME   That I-want-to-but-I-shouldn’t feeling is called tempted. Your mind might think, “Nobody will know.” Your body might feel your hand reaching and your tummy twisting.
FIND   When did you want to do something you knew was wrong, and you stopped?
FORK   Jesus could do what Satan said, or remember Heavenly Father’s words and say no. Jesus said no.
CALM   Pull your hands in close and breathe slowly. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me choose the right.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Remembering Heavenly Father’s words helps us say no to wrong things, just like Jesus!
