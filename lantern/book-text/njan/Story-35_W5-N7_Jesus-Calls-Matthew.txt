Week 5 · Night 7
Jesus Calls Matthew
Matthew, also called Levi, was a tax collector. He took money from people for the rulers. Some tax collectors took extra, so people did not like them.
Jesus walked by and said, “Follow Me.” Matthew left everything and followed Him. Then he made a big feast, and many tax collectors came.
Some leaders grumbled, “Why does Jesus eat with those people?”
Jesus said, “Healthy people do not need a doctor. Sick people do.” Jesus is like a kind doctor. He came to help hearts that need to change.
Jesus did not walk past Matthew. He welcomes everyone, including you. Tomorrow, invite someone who is left out to sit with you.

=== page 3 ===
Week 5 · Night 7 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wren the Wonderer pauses to ponder people others walk past. Jesus stopped for Matthew when everyone else looked away, and Wren thinks that is a wonder worth remembering.
In Wren’s world today: At the lunch table, a girl in a wrinkled shirt sits by herself. Some kids whisper. Wren picks up her tray, walks over, and sits down across from her. “Hi, I’m Wren. Want to eat together?” The girl’s whole face changes.
Just like Jesus, Wren learned that welcoming someone everyone else leaves out is what love looks like.

Week 5 · Night 7 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Pat the bed beside you like you are saving a seat for a friend. Now wave your hand and say, “Come sit with me!” Can you show me your welcome wave?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Matthew was a tax collector nobody liked. Jesus called him and ate with him. Matthew felt welcome.
NAME   That come-sit-with-us, you-belong-here feeling is called welcome. Your mind might think, “They want me!” Your body might feel a big smile and relaxed shoulders.
FIND   When did someone save you a seat or invite you in, and you felt welcome?
FORK   Jesus could walk past Matthew like everyone else, or call him to follow. Jesus called him.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and smile. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for welcoming me, and help me welcome others.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus welcomes everyone to His table, and we can, too!
