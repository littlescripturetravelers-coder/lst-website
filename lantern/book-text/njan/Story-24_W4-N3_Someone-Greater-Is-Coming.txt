Someone Greater Is Coming
The people loved to listen to John. They wondered, “Could John be the Christ, the Savior we are waiting for?”
John knew what they were thinking. He said, “I baptize you with water. But Someone much greater than I am is coming.”
John said, “I am not good enough even to untie His shoes.” Back then, untying dusty shoes was the very smallest job a helper did.
So Jesus is far, far greater than John. John said Jesus would baptize with the Holy Ghost and with fire. The Holy Ghost fills hearts with light and makes them clean.
John kept teaching, always pointing to Jesus. Tonight, fold your arms and sit still. Feel how great Jesus is.

Week 4 · Night 3 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wren the Wonderer says, “Pause and ponder, what a wonder!” John stopped to think about how great Jesus is, and his respect for Jesus filled every word he said.
In Wren’s world today: At church, Wren watches her grandpa stand up and speak about Jesus in a soft, strong voice. Everyone is quiet. Wren folds her hands and sits very still. She thinks, “Grandpa loves Jesus so much.”
Just like John, Wren learned that respect means knowing how great Jesus is and treating Him that way.

Week 4 · Night 3 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Pretend to kneel down and untie a sandal, slow and careful. Now sit up tall and bow your head. Can you show me your respect bow?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The people wondered if John was the Christ. John said Jesus was far greater. John felt deep respect for Jesus.
NAME   That He-is-so-great, I-am-honored feeling is called respect. Your mind might think, “He is greater than me.” Your body might feel a bowed head and still hands.
FIND   When have you felt respect for someone great, like a grandparent or a teacher?
FORK   John could let people think he was the Savior, or tell them about Jesus. John pointed to Jesus.
CALM   Breathe in slowly with your head bowed. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me honor Jesus.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus is greater than anyone, and we can honor Him with respect!
