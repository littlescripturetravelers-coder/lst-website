Week 5 · Night 5
I Will. Be Clean.
A man in one town had leprosy, a sickness that made skin sores. People with leprosy had to live away from everyone. Nobody would touch them. That is a lonely way to live.
The man saw Jesus and fell on his face. “Lord, if You want to, You can make me clean.”
Jesus could have healed him from far away. Instead, He reached out and touched him. “I will. Be clean.”
Right away the sickness was gone! Jesus sent him to show the priest he was well. Soon big crowds came to Jesus.
Jesus touched the man nobody else would touch. He loves every person. Tomorrow, find someone sitting alone and ask, “Want to play with me?”

=== page 3 ===
Week 5 · Night 5 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow the Warm says, “Warm hugs, warm hearts!” The man with leprosy had not been touched by anyone for a long time. Jesus reached out and touched him, and that is Willow’s kind of love.
In Willow’s world today: At recess, a new boy sits alone on the bench. Nobody is playing with him. Willow walks over, sits down next to him, and says, “Want to play with us?” His face lights up. They run to the swings together.
Just like Jesus, Willow learned that reaching out to someone lonely is one of the kindest things we can do.

Week 5 · Night 5 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Wrap your arms around yourself and feel how it feels to be all alone. Now reach one arm out, like Jesus reaching to touch the man. Can you show me your reaching-out arm?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The man with leprosy lived away from everyone. He felt lonely, and then Jesus touched him and healed him.
NAME   That nobody’s-near-me, left-out feeling is called lonely. Your mind might think, “Does anyone see me?” Your body might feel an empty ache in your chest.
FIND   When did you feel lonely, like no one was playing with you?
FORK   Jesus could heal from far away, or reach out and touch the man. Jesus touched him.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and put your hand on your own arm, gently. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You that Jesus sees me.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus reaches out to the lonely, and we can reach out, too!
