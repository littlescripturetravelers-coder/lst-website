Week 2 · Night 3
Simeon and Anna Wait for Jesus
Mary and Joseph took baby Jesus to the temple in Jerusalem. There they gave thanks for Him.
A good old man named Simeon lived in Jerusalem. The Holy Ghost had promised he would see the Savior before he died. He waited and waited.
That day the Holy Ghost led Simeon to the temple. He took baby Jesus in his arms. “Now I can go in peace,” he said. “I have seen Your Savior!”
An old woman named Anna prayed in the temple day and night. When she saw Jesus, she gave thanks. She told others about Him.
Simeon and Anna waited a long time, and Heavenly Father kept His promise. When you wait, keep trusting Him.

=== page 3 ===
Week 2 · Night 3 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wren the Wonderer says, “Pause and ponder.” Simeon and Anna waited quietly for years, trusting Heavenly Father’s promise, and Wren understands that kind of patient wondering.
In Wren’s world today: Wren plants a bean seed in a cup. Every morning she looks. Nothing. Still nothing. She waters it anyway and waits. On the ninth day, a tiny green sprout pokes up! Wren whispers, “It was growing the whole time.”
Just like Simeon and Anna, Wren learned that Heavenly Father keeps His promises, and waiting is worth it.

Week 2 · Night 3 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lie still and count slowly to ten in your head, like Simeon waiting for Jesus. Breathe softly while you count. Can you show me how patiently you can wait?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Simeon and Anna waited many years to see the Savior. They felt patient, and then full of joy.
NAME   That quiet, keep-trusting, not-yet feeling is called patient. Your mind might think, “It will come.” Your body might feel still and calm.
FIND   When did you have to wait a long time for something good?
FORK   Simeon could give up waiting, or keep trusting Heavenly Father’s promise. He kept trusting, and he saw Jesus.
CALM   Take one slow breath in and one slow breath out. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, I will wait with You.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father keeps His promises, so we can wait with a happy heart!
