Week 2 · Night 6
Jesus Grows Up
After their visit to the temple, Mary and Joseph took Jesus home to Nazareth. There Jesus grew up.
Joseph made things from wood, and Jesus learned to be a carpenter too. He helped His family, the way you help yours.
Jesus grew strong. He grew wise, learning more and more of what is true and good. And Heavenly Father’s love was with Him.
Jesus obeyed Mary and Joseph. He grew taller, and He grew wiser. Heavenly Father loved Him, and the people who knew Him loved Him too.
You are growing too, a little more every day. Tonight, think of one way you helped your family today. Heavenly Father’s love is with you while you grow.

Week 2 · Night 6 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow the Warm says, “Warm hugs, warm hearts!” Jesus grew up in a home full of love, and Willow knows that love is what helps us grow.
In Willow’s world today: Willow helps Dad set the table. She puts a fork by every plate, one by one. Dad says, “You’re growing up so well, Willow.” She feels warm all over and stands a little taller.
Just like Jesus, Willow learned that we grow best when we are loved and when we help.

Week 2 · Night 6 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lie flat and stretch your arms up over your head and your toes down, as long as you can. You are growing! Can you show me how tall you can stretch?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Jesus grew up in Nazareth, helping and obeying. Heavenly Father’s love was with Him, and He felt loved.
NAME   That warm, wrapped-up, someone-cares-for-me feeling is called loved. Your mind might think, “I belong here.” Your body might feel warm and soft.
FIND   When did someone help you learn something new and you felt loved?
FORK   Jesus could do only what He wanted, or help and obey His family. Jesus chose to help and obey.
CALM   Breathe in and feel your blanket hug you. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for loving me.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father’s love helps us grow strong and kind, just like Jesus!
