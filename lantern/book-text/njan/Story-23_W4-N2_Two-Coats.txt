Week 4 · Night 2
Two Coats
Many people came to the river to be baptized by John. He said, “Show that you have really changed.” A good tree shows it is good by its fruit.
The people asked John, “Then what should we do?” He said, “Two coats? Give one to someone who has none. Share your food, too.”
Tax collectors came too. They gathered money for the rulers, and some took extra for themselves. John said, “Take only what is fair.”
Soldiers asked, “And what should we do?” John said, “Do not hurt anyone or tell lies about them. Be happy with your pay.”
A changed heart shows in kind hands. Tonight, think of one thing you can share tomorrow.

=== page 3 ===
Week 4 · Night 2 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow the Warm says, “Warm hugs, warm hearts!” John told the people to share their coats and their food, and sharing is one of Willow’s favorite ways to show love.
In Willow’s world today: It is snack time at preschool, and a boy at Willow’s table forgot his snack. His face looks sad. Willow breaks her granola bar in half and slides one piece over. “Here, we can share.” The boy smiles, and Willow’s heart feels full.
Just like John taught, Willow learned that a loving heart shows up in sharing hands.

Week 4 · Night 2 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Pretend to hold a cozy coat in your arms. Now reach out and hand it to someone who is cold. Can you show me your giving hands?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   John told the people to share coats and food and be fair. The ones who listened felt generous.
NAME   That open-hands, I-want-to-give feeling is called generous. Your mind might think, “You can have some of mine.” Your body might feel warm and light.
FIND   When did you share something you really liked, and it made you feel good?
FORK   The people could keep both coats, or give one away. John taught them to give.
CALM   Open your hands on your blanket, palms up. Breathe slowly. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me share.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
A loving heart shows up in sharing hands!
