Jesus Is Baptized
Jesus came from Nazareth to the Jordan River. He asked John to baptize Him. John was surprised. “I need You to baptize me!” he said.
Jesus said, “Let it be so. It is right to do all that is good.” Jesus had never done anything wrong. But He wanted to obey Heavenly Father in everything.
So John baptized Jesus, under the water and up again.
When Jesus came up, the heavens opened. The Holy Ghost came down like a dove and rested on Him. A voice said, “This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.”
It was Heavenly Father, happy with His dearly loved Son. He is happy when you obey too. Tonight, tell Him one way you will obey tomorrow.

=== page 3 ===
Week 4 · Night 4 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Beau the Brave says, “Big heart, brave start!” Jesus bravely went first and showed us the way to be baptized, and Heavenly Father said He was pleased. That is the best well done ever.
In Beau’s world today: Beau is at the pool, and the water is cold. The swim teacher says, “Who will go first?” Beau’s heart pounds, but he climbs in and puts his face in the water. Dad calls, “Well done, Beau!” Beau feels tall and warm inside.
Just like Jesus, Beau learned that when we obey Heavenly Father, He is pleased with us.

Week 4 · Night 4 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Take a big breath and slowly sink down under your blanket. Now rise up and stretch your arms to the sky, like coming up out of the water. Can you show me your rising up?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Jesus was baptized, and Heavenly Father said He was well pleased. Jesus felt loved and proud to obey.
NAME   That warm, standing-tall, well-done feeling is called proud. Your mind might think, “I did the right thing.” Your body might feel tall, with a big breath.
FIND   When did someone say well done to you, and you felt tall inside?
FORK   Jesus could skip baptism, since He had done nothing wrong, or obey Heavenly Father in everything. Jesus obeyed.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and stand tall in your heart. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, I want to make You happy.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
When we obey Heavenly Father, He is pleased and says well done!

Testimony
1. Share what your own baptism meant to you, or what you hope it will mean to your child.
2. When have you felt Heavenly Father was pleased with a choice you made?
3. Tell about a grandparent’s or ancestor’s baptism, or how they followed Jesus’s example.
