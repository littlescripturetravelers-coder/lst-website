Week 1 · Night 3
The Baby Who Jumped for Joy
After the angel left, Mary hurried into the hills to see her cousin Elisabeth. Elisabeth was going to have a baby too, baby John.
Mary came in and called hello. The moment Elisabeth heard Mary’s voice, her baby jumped for joy!
The Holy Ghost filled Elisabeth. She knew Mary’s baby would be the Lord.
Elisabeth’s voice was loud and happy. “You are blessed, Mary, and so is your baby! You believed Heavenly Father’s words, and they will come true.”
Even a tiny baby can feel joy when Jesus is near. Tonight, think of someone who makes you want to jump for joy. Thank Heavenly Father for them.

=== page 3 ===
Week 1 · Night 3 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow the Warm loves warm hugs and warm hearts. Tonight’s story is about two cousins whose joy spilled out in hugs and happy words, just like Willow.
In Willow’s world today: Willow hears the doorbell and runs to the window. It is Grandma! Willow’s feet do a happy dance before the door even opens. She flings her arms around Grandma’s neck and squeals, “You came!”
Just like Elisabeth, Willow learned that joy is Heavenly Father’s gift, and it grows when we share it.

Week 1 · Night 3 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lie in bed and give yourself a big, squeezy hug. Now let your toes wiggle like a baby kicking for joy. Can you show me your happiest wiggle?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Mary came to visit, and the baby inside Elisabeth jumped. Elisabeth and Mary felt happy and full of joy.
NAME   That bubbly, bouncy, can’t-sit-still feeling is called joy. Your mind might think, “This is the best!” Your body might feel light and jumpy.
FIND   When did someone come to see you and your whole body felt happy?
FORK   Elisabeth could keep her joy quiet, or shout it out and bless Mary. She shared it with a loud, happy voice.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and let your smile stay. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for joy.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Joy is a gift from Heavenly Father, and it grows bigger when we share it!
