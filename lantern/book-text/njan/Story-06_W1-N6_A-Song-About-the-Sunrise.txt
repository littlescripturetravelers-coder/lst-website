Week 1 · Night 6
A Song About the Sunrise
When Zacharias could talk again, the Holy Ghost filled him. He praised Heavenly Father with words like a happy song.
“Blessed be the Lord!” he said. “He is sending the Savior, just as the prophets promised long ago.”
Then he looked at his tiny baby. “And you, little child, will get people ready for the Lord. You will teach that sins can be forgiven.”
All this comes from Heavenly Father’s tender mercy, His soft and gentle love. The sun comes up each morning and chases the dark away. Zacharias said Jesus would come like that sunrise, bringing light.
Jesus is gentle with us, like the morning sun. Tonight, use soft words with someone who feels sad.

=== page 3 ===
Week 1 · Night 6 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow the Warm has a soft, caring heart. Tonight’s song is about Heavenly Father’s tender mercy, and tender is exactly the kind of warm that Willow is.
In Willow’s world today: Willow’s little brother spills his milk and starts to cry. Willow does not scold him. She gets a towel, helps him wipe it up, and says softly, “It’s okay. Everybody spills sometimes.” Her brother stops crying and smiles.
Just like Zacharias sang, Willow learned that Heavenly Father’s tender mercy is soft and kind, and we can share it.

=== page 4 ===
Week 1 · Night 6 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Make your hands very soft, like you are holding a baby bird. Now gently pat your own cheek, slow and kind. Can you show me the gentlest touch you have?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Zacharias sang about Heavenly Father’s tender mercy, like a sunrise. He felt soft, gentle, and full of love.
NAME   That soft, gentle, careful-with-you feeling is called tender. Your mind might think, “I want to be kind.” Your body might feel calm, with gentle hands.
FIND   When did someone treat you gently after you made a mistake?
FORK   Willow could scold her brother, or be gentle. She chose to be tender, like Heavenly Father is with us.
CALM   Breathe in slowly, like the sun rising. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for Your tender mercy.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus is like the sunrise, bringing gentle light when it feels dark!
