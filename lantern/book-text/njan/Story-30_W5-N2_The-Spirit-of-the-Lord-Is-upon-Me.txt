Week 5 · Night 2
The Spirit of the Lord Is upon Me
Jesus came home to Nazareth, where He grew up. On the Sabbath, He went to the meeting house, like always.
There they handed Him a long rolled-up paper called a scroll. It held the words of Isaiah, a prophet from long ago. Jesus stood up and read.
He read, “The Spirit of the Lord is upon Me. He sent Me to heal the brokenhearted, hearts that hurt with sadness.”
Jesus rolled up the scroll and sat down. Everyone stared. He said, “Today these words came true, right in front of you.” Isaiah was talking about Jesus!
When your heart feels sad, Jesus knows. He came to heal hearts, yours too. Tonight, tell Him what makes you sad, and let His love hold you.

Week 5 · Night 2 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wren the Wonderer loves to pause and ponder old words that come true. Isaiah wrote about Jesus long ago, and Jesus said, “Today it has come true.” What a wonder!
In Wren’s world today: Wren’s best friend is moving away, and Wren cries on the porch. Grandma sits down beside her, wraps her in a blanket, and says, “Jesus knows how a sad heart feels.” Wren leans in. The sad feeling is still there, but it feels held.
Just like Isaiah promised, Wren learned that Jesus came to comfort brokenhearted people.

Week 5 · Night 2 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Pretend to unroll a long, long scroll with both hands, slowly. Now put your hand over your heart, where Jesus heals. Can you show me your hand on your heart?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Jesus read that He came to heal the brokenhearted. The people who were hurting felt comfort.
NAME   That someone-is-here-for-me, held-close feeling is called comfort. Your mind might think, “I am not alone.” Your body might feel your shoulders relax and your breath slow down.
FIND   When were you sad, and someone sat close and made you feel comforted?
FORK   The people could ignore Jesus’s words, or let Him heal their hearts. Those who believed found comfort.
CALM   Put both hands over your heart and breathe slowly. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for sending Jesus to comfort me.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus came to heal hurting hearts, and He is close to you!
