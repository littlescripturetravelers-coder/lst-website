Week 3 · Night 1
The Light of the World
John was one of Jesus’s best friends. He wrote a book about Jesus. It begins, “In the beginning was the Word.”
The Word is a name for Jesus. Our words show what is in our hearts. Jesus shows us Heavenly Father’s heart.
Before the world was made, Jesus was with Heavenly Father. Jesus helped Him make everything: the stars, the sea, and the trees.
John wrote that Jesus is like a light. When it is dark, a light shows the way. Jesus’s light shines in the dark.
Then Jesus came to earth with a real body, just like yours. He lived with people, full of kindness and truth. Tonight, when your light goes off, whisper, “Jesus is my light.”

=== page 3 ===
Week 3 · Night 1 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wren the Wonderer says, “Pause and ponder, what a wonder!” Tonight’s story is the biggest wonder of all: Jesus helped make the world, and then He came to live in it.
In Wren’s world today: Wren lies on the grass at night with Grandpa and looks up at the stars. There are so many! Grandpa says, “Jesus helped make every one.” Wren is quiet for a long time. Then she whispers, “Wow.”
Just like John, Wren learned that Jesus is the light who made the world and came to be with us.

=== page 4 ===
Week 3 · Night 1 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Cup your hands together in the dark, then slowly open them like a light turning on. Open them wider and wider. Can you show me your light shining?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   John told about Jesus making the world and coming to earth. The people who knew Jesus felt wonder.
NAME   That big, hushed, eyes-wide feeling is called wonder. Your mind might think, “Wow, how can it be?” Your body might feel still, with a quiet gasp.
FIND   When did you see something so big or beautiful that you said, “Wow”?
FORK   People could walk in the dark, or follow the light of Jesus. John chose to follow the light.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and picture a soft light glowing. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for sending Jesus, my light.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus is the light of the world, and the dark can never put Him out!
