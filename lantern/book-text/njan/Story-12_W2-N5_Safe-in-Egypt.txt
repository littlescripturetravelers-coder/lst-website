Week 2 · Night 5
Safe in Egypt
After the wise men left, an angel came to Joseph in a dream. “Get up! Take the child and His mother to Egypt. King Herod wants to hurt Him.”
Egypt was a land far away. Joseph did not wait until morning. He left that very night with Mary and Jesus.
They stayed in Egypt, safe and sound, until King Herod died.
Then the angel came again. “It is safe now. Go home.” So Joseph brought his family back to Nazareth, where Jesus would grow up.
Joseph obeyed right away, and Heavenly Father kept Jesus safe. Tonight, when Mom or Dad asks you to do something, obey right away.

=== page 3 ===
Week 2 · Night 5 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Beau the Brave has a big heart and a brave start. Joseph got up in the middle of the night and did a hard thing right away to keep his family safe. That is Beau’s kind of brave.
In Beau’s world today: A storm rolls in while Beau is playing outside. Dad calls, “Come inside, now!” Beau does not wait or argue. He runs inside right away. Then he watches the rain from the window, warm and safe.
Just like Joseph, Beau learned that obeying quickly keeps us safe in Heavenly Father’s care.

Week 2 · Night 5 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Pull your blanket up to your chin and wrap it tight around you, like a safe little tent. Take a slow breath inside. Can you show me your safe and cozy face?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Joseph heard the angel’s warning and left right away. Mary and Jesus felt safe because Joseph obeyed.
NAME   That warm, protected, nothing-can-get-me feeling is called safe. Your mind might think, “Someone is watching over me.” Your body might feel relaxed and cozy.
FIND   When did you feel safe, like being held or tucked in tight?
FORK   Joseph could wait until morning, or go right away. He obeyed that very night.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and feel your bed holding you. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for keeping me safe.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
When we obey right away, Heavenly Father keeps us safe!
