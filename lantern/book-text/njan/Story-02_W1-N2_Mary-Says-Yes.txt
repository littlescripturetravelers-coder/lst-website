Week 1 · Night 2
Mary Says Yes
In the little town of Nazareth lived a young woman named Mary. She was going to marry Joseph.
One day the angel Gabriel came to her. “Hello, Mary! The Lord is with you.” Mary was afraid.
Gabriel said, “Fear not. You will have a baby boy. Name Him Jesus. He will be the Son of the Highest, a King forever.”
Mary asked, “How can this be?” Gabriel said, “With Heavenly Father, nothing is impossible.” Mary trusted Him. She said, “I am the Lord’s servant. Let it be as you said.” That was a brave yes!
Heavenly Father sometimes asks us to do hard things. Tonight, think of one brave thing you can try. You can say yes, like Mary.

=== page 3 ===
Week 1 · Night 2 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Beau the Brave has a big heart and a brave start. Mary said yes to something very hard before she knew how it would all work out. That is the bravest kind of yes.
In Beau’s world today: Beau’s teacher asks who will be first to stand up and share. Beau’s tummy flutters. He does not know if he can do it. But he takes a big breath, raises his hand, and says, “I will try.”
Just like Mary, Beau learned that being brave means saying yes to Heavenly Father even when we feel small.

=== page 4 ===
Week 1 · Night 2 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lie on your back and let your arms go soft, like you are resting. Now take a big breath and make your hand into a strong fist. Can you show me your brave fist?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Mary saw an angel and heard big news. She felt afraid, and then she felt brave enough to say yes.
NAME   That shaky-but-strong, I-can-do-it feeling is called brave. Your mind might think, “I will try.” Your body might feel your chest get tall.
FIND   When did you do something that felt hard, like a first day somewhere new?
FORK   Mary could say, “No, this is too hard,” or trust Heavenly Father and say yes. Mary said yes.
CALM   Take one slow breath and let your shoulders drop. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me be brave like Mary.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
With Heavenly Father, nothing is impossible, so we can say a brave yes!
