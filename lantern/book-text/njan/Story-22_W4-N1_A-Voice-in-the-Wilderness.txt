Week 4 · Night 1
A Voice in the Wilderness
John the Baptist lived in the wild, empty land by the Jordan River. He wore rough camel’s hair and a leather belt. He ate wild honey and big grasshoppers called locusts.
Heavenly Father gave John a job: get people ready for Jesus. John called out, “Repent!” To repent is to turn from wrong and come back to Heavenly Father.
Long before, the prophet Isaiah said a voice would call, “Make the way ready!” Isaiah was talking about John.
Back then, people fixed bumpy roads before a king came. John helped people smooth the bumpy places in their hearts for Jesus.
People came from all around. They said sorry, and John baptized them in the river. Tonight, you can get your heart ready for Jesus too.

=== page 3 ===
Week 4 · Night 1 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Scout the Seeker loves an adventure in wild places. John lived in the wilderness, wore camel hair, ate honey, and did his job with all his might. Scout thinks that is the best kind of determined.
In Scout’s world today: Scout is picking up every toy in the playroom before Grandma comes. It is a big job. His sister wants to quit, but Scout says, “We’re getting ready for Grandma! Let’s keep going.” They finish just as the doorbell rings.
Just like John, Scout learned that getting ready for someone we love takes a determined heart.

Week 4 · Night 1 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
March your feet under the covers like John walking through the desert. Keep going, keep going. Now stop and take a breath. Can you show me your keep-going march?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   John had a big job: get people ready for Jesus. He felt determined, and he did not quit.
NAME   That I-will-do-this, don’t-quit feeling is called determined. Your mind might think, “Keep going!” Your body might feel strong and steady.
FIND   When did you keep working on something hard until it was done?
FORK   John could live an easy life, or do the job Heavenly Father gave him. John chose to prepare the way.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and let your feet rest. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me get my heart ready for Jesus.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
We can make a straight path in our hearts for Jesus to come in!
