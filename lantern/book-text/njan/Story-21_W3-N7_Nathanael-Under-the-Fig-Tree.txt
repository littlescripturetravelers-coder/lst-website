Nathanael Under the Fig Tree
Jesus said to a man named Philip, “Follow Me.” Philip did, and he hurried to find his friend Nathanael.
“We have found Him!” said Philip. “He is the One the prophets wrote about. He is Jesus from Nazareth.”
Nathanael was not sure. “Can anything good come out of Nazareth?” he asked. Philip did not argue. He just said, “Come and see.”
When Jesus saw Nathanael coming, He said, “Here is an honest man.” Nathanael was surprised. “How do You know me?” Jesus said, “I saw you under the fig tree, before Philip called you.”
Nathanael’s doubt turned to belief. He said, “Teacher, You are the Son of God!” Jesus knows you too, even when you are alone. Tonight, tell Him what is in your heart.

Week 3 · Night 7 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wren the Wonderer pauses to think before she decides. Nathanael was not sure at first, but he was honest and came to see, and then he believed. Wren likes honest thinking like that.
In Wren’s world today: Wren’s cousin says the new library has a reading tree house. Wren frowns. “I don’t think that’s real.” Her cousin says, “Come and see!” At the library, there it is, a real tree house full of books! Wren laughs. “I’m glad I came.”
Just like Nathanael, Wren learned that it is okay to wonder, as long as we come and see for ourselves.

Week 3 · Night 7 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Scrunch up your nose and tilt your head like you are not so sure. Now nod your head slowly, yes, yes, yes. Can you show me your I-believe nod?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Nathanael was not sure anything good could come from Nazareth. He felt doubt, and then he came and believed.
NAME   That hmm, I’m-not-so-sure feeling is called doubt. Your mind might think, “Is that really true?” Your body might feel a frown and crossed arms.
FIND   When were you not sure about something, and then you found out it was true?
FORK   Nathanael could stay home with his doubt, or come and see Jesus. He came, and his doubt turned to faith.
CALM   Take a slow breath and let your face soften. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me come and see, even when I’m not sure.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus sees you and knows you, even when no one else is there!
