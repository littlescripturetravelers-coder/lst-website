Week 1 · Night 5
His Name Is John
At last Elisabeth had her baby, a little boy! Family and neighbors heard how kind Heavenly Father had been, and they were glad.
On his eighth day, it was time to name him. Everyone said, “Call him Zacharias, like his father.” But Elisabeth said, “No. His name is John.”
The people were puzzled. “Nobody in your family has that name!” They made signs to ask Zacharias.
He still could not talk. So he wrote on a little board, “His name is John.” Right then, he could talk again! He praised Heavenly Father. Everyone wondered what this child would grow up to be.
Zacharias obeyed the angel, and his voice came back. Tonight, use your voice to say something kind to your family.

=== page 3 ===
Week 1 · Night 5 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Scout the Seeker looks and sees, and asks, “What could it be?” The neighbors looked at baby John and wondered what he would grow up to be. Scout loves a good wondering.
In Scout’s world today: Scout’s family brings home a new baby brother. Everyone guesses what his name will be. Then Dad says, “His name is Eli.” Scout grins at the tiny baby and says, “Hi, Eli! I wonder what you’ll be when you grow up.”
Just like Zacharias, Scout learned that when we do what Heavenly Father asks, happy things follow.

=== page 4 ===
Week 1 · Night 5 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Press your lips together tight, like Zacharias who could not talk. Now open your mouth and say, “His name is John!” Can you show me your happiest whisper?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Baby John was born, and Zacharias got his voice back. The whole family felt happy and amazed.
NAME   That smiling, light, everything-is-good feeling is called happy. Your mind might think, “Hooray!” Your body might feel like clapping and grinning.
FIND   When did something good happen in your family that made everyone smile?
FORK   Zacharias could name the baby after himself like everyone wanted, or obey the angel. He obeyed, and he could talk again.
CALM   Take a slow breath and let a smile come. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for happy days.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
When we obey Heavenly Father, happy things follow!
