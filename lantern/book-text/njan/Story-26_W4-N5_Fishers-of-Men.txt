Week 4 · Night 5
Fishers of Men
Jesus went to Galilee, by a big lake. He taught, “Turn back to Heavenly Father, and believe the good news!”
By the water, Jesus saw two brothers, Simon and Andrew. They were fishermen, throwing their net into the lake. Jesus said, “Come, follow Me. I will make you fishers of men.”
What did He mean? They gathered fish into nets. Now Jesus would teach them to gather people to Heavenly Father.
Right away, Simon and Andrew left their nets and followed Him. Then Jesus saw James and John fixing nets in a boat with their father.
Jesus called them too. Right away they left the boat and went with Him. Tonight, think of one way you can follow Jesus tomorrow.

=== page 3 ===
Week 4 · Night 5 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Scout the Seeker is always ready to go. When Jesus said, “Come, follow Me,” the fishermen said yes right away, and Scout loves a right-away yes.
In Scout’s world today: Scout is building a fort when Mom calls, “Who wants to help me take dinner to the Garcias?” Scout puts down his blocks and says, “I will!” On the way, he gets to carry the warm bread. It smells amazing.
Just like the fishermen, Scout learned that being willing means saying yes to Jesus right away.

Week 4 · Night 5 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Pretend to throw a big fishing net out over your blanket. Now pull it back in, hand over hand. Can you show me how you drop the net and follow?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Jesus called the fishermen, and they left their nets right away. They felt willing to follow Him.
NAME   That ready, yes-I’ll-come feeling is called willing. Your mind might think, “I’ll do it!” Your body might feel your feet ready to go.
FIND   When did someone ask you to help, and you said yes right away?
FORK   The fishermen could stay with their boats, or follow Jesus right away. They went with Jesus.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and let your feet relax. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, I am willing to follow Jesus.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
When Jesus calls, we can say yes right away!
