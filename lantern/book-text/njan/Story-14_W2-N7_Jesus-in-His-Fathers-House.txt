Jesus in His Father's House
When Jesus was twelve, His family went to Jerusalem for the Passover. It was a holiday to remember how Heavenly Father saved His people.
On the way home, they thought Jesus was in the big group. But at day’s end, He was not there!
They hurried back and looked for three days. At last they found Jesus in the temple. He was listening to the teachers and asking questions.
Everyone was amazed at what He understood. Mary said, “We were so worried!” Jesus said, “I must be doing My Father’s work.” He meant Heavenly Father. Then He went home and obeyed them.
Mary and Joseph kept looking until they found Jesus. Tonight, you can look for Him too, when you pray.

Week 2 · Night 7 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wren the Wonderer loves to ask questions and listen, just like twelve-year-old Jesus in the temple. And Wren knows the big whoosh of relief when something lost is found.
In Wren’s world today: Wren cannot find her favorite stuffed bunny anywhere. She looks under the bed and in the closet. Her chest feels tight. Then Mom finds Bunny behind the couch, and Wren lets out a big, happy breath. “There you are!”
Just like Mary and Joseph, Wren learned that the whoosh of relief is a gift, and Heavenly Father helps us find what we are looking for.

Week 2 · Night 7 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Take in a big, worried breath and hold it. Now let it all whoosh out, long and slow, like finding something you lost. Can you show me your biggest whoosh?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Mary and Joseph could not find Jesus for three days. When they found Him in the temple, they felt relieved.
NAME   That whoosh, all-better, I-can-breathe-again feeling is called relieved. Your mind might think, “Oh good!” Your body might feel loose and light.
FIND   When did you lose something and then feel a big whoosh when you found it?
FORK   Mary and Joseph could give up looking, or keep searching. They kept looking until they found Jesus.
CALM   Let out one long, slow breath. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for helping me find what I need.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus loved being in Heavenly Father’s house, and we can love it, too!
