Week 5 · Night 3
A Great Light
John the Baptist was put in prison. So Jesus went to Galilee. He moved to Capernaum, a town beside the lake.
Isaiah wrote long ago, “The people who sat in darkness saw a great light.” Isaiah was talking about Jesus coming there.
Why a light? Think of a dark room that feels scary. Someone turns on a lamp, and everything feels safe. Jesus is that light for the whole world.
In Capernaum, Jesus began to teach, “Repent!” To repent is to turn away from wrong and back to Heavenly Father. It is like stepping from the dark into the light.
When the dark feels scary, whisper, “Jesus is my light.” Then snuggle down, safe and warm.

=== page 3 ===
Week 5 · Night 3 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Scout the Seeker says, “Look and see!” When a great light comes on, Scout can see everything, and nothing looks scary anymore. Jesus is that light.
In Scout’s world today: Scout wakes up in the night, and his room is dark. The shadows look strange. He whispers, “Jesus is my light,” and clicks on his little lamp. Now he can see his bear, his books, and his blanket. He snuggles down, safe.
Just like Isaiah said, Scout learned that Jesus is a great light that makes us feel safe.

Week 5 · Night 3 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Close your eyes tight and make everything dark. Now open them wide and imagine a great light turning on. Can you show me your light-is-on smile?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   People were sitting in darkness, and Jesus came like a great light. They felt safe in His light.
NAME   That the-light-is-on, nothing-can-get-me feeling is called safe. Your mind might think, “I can see now.” Your body might feel your shoulders drop and your breath slow.
FIND   When did a light turning on make you feel safe in the dark?
FORK   People could stay in the dark, or come into Jesus’s light. Jesus invited everyone to come.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and picture a warm, bright light. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for Jesus, my light.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus is a great light, and in His light we are safe!
