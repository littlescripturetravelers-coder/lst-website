Week 2 · Night 2
Angels and Shepherds
That night, shepherds were in the fields near Bethlehem, keeping their sheep safe.
Suddenly an angel came, and bright light shone all around! The shepherds were afraid. The angel said, “Fear not! I bring good news of great joy.”
“Today a Savior is born, Christ the Lord. You will find Him wrapped in cloths, lying in a manger.”
Many angels praised, “Glory to Heavenly Father, and on earth peace!” When they went back to heaven, the shepherds said, “Let’s go see!” They hurried and found Mary, Joseph, and baby Jesus. Then they told everyone.
At first the shepherds were afraid. Then the good news filled them with joy. Tonight, tell someone the happy news: Jesus was born!

=== page 3 ===
Week 2 · Night 2 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Scout the Seeker says, “Look and see, what could it be?” The shepherds heard the angel and said, “Let’s go see!” That is Scout’s favorite thing to say.
In Scout’s world today: Scout hears a strange noise in the backyard at night. His heart thumps. Then Dad says, “Let’s go look together.” With the flashlight on, they find a little owl in the tree. Scout’s fear turns into a big smile.
Just like the shepherds, Scout learned that when we go and see what Heavenly Father shows us, fear turns into joy.

Week 2 · Night 2 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Cover your eyes with your hands like the shepherds hiding from the bright light. Now peek through your fingers and open them wide. Can you show me your go-and-see eyes?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The angel came with bright light, and the shepherds felt afraid. Then they heard good news and felt joy.
NAME   That jumpy, want-to-hide feeling is called afraid. Your mind might think, “What is that?” Your body might feel your heart pound and your legs want to run.
FIND   When did something sudden, like a loud noise, make you feel afraid?
FORK   The shepherds could hide in the field, or go and see the baby. They hurried to Bethlehem.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and feel your blanket around you. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me not be afraid.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Fear not! Jesus is good news of great joy for everyone, even you!
