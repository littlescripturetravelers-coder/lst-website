Andrew Finds His Brother
Andrew had spent the whole day with Jesus. His heart was too full to keep it inside. The first thing he did was find his brother, Simon.
Andrew said, “We have found the Messiah!” That was the Savior Heavenly Father had promised to send. Everyone had been waiting for Him.
Andrew did not just tell Simon. He brought Simon to Jesus, so Simon could meet Him too.
Jesus looked at Simon and said, “You are Simon. You will be called Cephas.” Cephas means a stone, like a big, strong rock. We know him as Peter.
Jesus could see that Simon would grow strong and steady, like a rock. When you find something wonderful, share it! Tonight, think of someone you can tell about Jesus.

=== page 3 ===
Week 3 · Night 6 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Willow the Warm
Willow the Warm says, “Warm hugs, warm hearts!” Andrew’s heart was so warm from being with Jesus that he ran to share it with his brother. Willow loves sharing good things with people she loves.
In Willow’s world today: Willow learns a new song at church that makes her heart happy. On the way home she can hardly wait. She runs to her little brother and says, “Come here, I have to teach you this song!” They sing it together until bedtime.
Just like Andrew, Willow learned that good news about Jesus is meant to be shared.

Week 3 · Night 6 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lie still, then let your legs run in place under the blanket, like Andrew running to find his brother. Now stop and smile. Can you show me your running-to-share legs?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Andrew found Jesus and ran to tell his brother. He felt eager to share the good news.
NAME   That can’t-wait, I-have-to-tell-you feeling is called eager. Your mind might think, “You have to hear this!” Your body might feel quick and bouncy.
FIND   When did you learn something so good that you ran to tell someone?
FORK   Andrew could keep Jesus to himself, or bring his brother to meet Him. Andrew brought Simon to Jesus.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and let your legs rest. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me share Jesus with the people I love.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
When we find Jesus, the best thing to do is bring someone else to Him!
