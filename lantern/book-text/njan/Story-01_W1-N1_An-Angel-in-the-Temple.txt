Week 1 · Night 1
An Angel in the Temple
Long ago lived a good old man named Zacharias. He and his wife, Elisabeth, had no children.
One day Zacharias went into the temple, Heavenly Father’s holy house. He burned sweet-smelling incense while the people prayed outside. An angel stood by the altar!
Zacharias shook with fear. The angel said, “Fear not. Your prayer is heard. Elisabeth will have a baby boy named John.”
“How can I know this? We are so old,” said Zacharias. The angel said, “I am Gabriel. You did not believe me, so you cannot talk until the baby comes.” Zacharias came out and could only wave his hands.
Heavenly Father hears every prayer, even ones we pray for a long time. Tonight, tell Him something you have been asking for.

=== page 3 ===
Week 1 · Night 1 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Wren the Wonderer
Wren the Wonderer pauses and ponders. Zacharias saw something so big he could hardly take it in, and Wren loves to wonder about big, holy things.
In Wren’s world today: Wren has asked Heavenly Father every night to help her baby cousin get better. Tonight Mom whispers that the baby came home from the hospital. Wren sits very still and feels her heart go thump, thump. “Heavenly Father really heard me!”
Just like Zacharias, Wren learned that Heavenly Father hears every prayer, even when the answer takes a long time.

Week 1 · Night 1 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lie very still and breathe in slowly, like Zacharias smelling the sweet incense. Now open your eyes wide, like he did when he saw the angel. Can you show me a surprised face?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Zacharias saw an angel in the temple. He was shaking and surprised, and then he heard happy news.
NAME   That big, jumpy, wide-eyed feeling is called surprised. Your mind might think, “Is this real?” Your body might feel your heart beat fast.
FIND   When did something surprise you so much your eyes went wide?
FORK   Zacharias could believe the angel’s words, or doubt them. He doubted at first. Later he learned to trust Heavenly Father.
CALM   Put one hand on your heart. Breathe slowly until it is calm. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for hearing me.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Heavenly Father hears every prayer, even when the answer takes a long time!
