Friends on the Roof
Jesus was teaching in a packed house. Nobody else could get in the door.
Four men carried their friend, who could not walk, on a mat. They believed Jesus could heal him. But the crowd was in the way.
So the friends climbed up onto the flat roof. They opened it and lowered their friend right down to Jesus!
Jesus saw how much they believed. He said the man’s sins, the wrong things he had done, were forgiven. Some leaders grumbled, “Only Heavenly Father can forgive sins!” So Jesus showed His power. He said, “Get up, take your mat, and go home.” The man walked home, praising Heavenly Father!
Those friends did not give up. Tonight, plan one kind thing to help a friend.

=== page 3 ===
Week 5 · Night 6 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Beau the Brave has a big heart and a brave start. The four friends did not give up, even when the door was blocked. They climbed up on the roof! Beau loves brave, hopeful friends like that.
In Beau’s world today: Beau’s friend Max broke his leg and cannot come to the park. So Beau has an idea. He brings the park to Max, with a ball, a kite, and a picnic blanket in Max’s backyard. Max grins. “You didn’t forget me.”
Just like the four friends, Beau learned that hopeful friends find a way to bring people to Jesus.

Week 5 · Night 6 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Pretend you are carrying one corner of a heavy mat with three friends. Lift it up, up, up to the roof. Now lower it down slowly. Can you show me your gentle lowering?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Four friends could not get their friend to Jesus. They felt hopeful, found a way, and he was healed.
NAME   That it-can-still-happen, don’t-give-up feeling is called hopeful. Your mind might think, “There must be a way.” Your body might feel your eyes looking up.
FIND   When did you keep hoping for something good, even when it looked hard?
FORK   The friends could give up at the crowded door, or find another way. They climbed up to the roof.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and look up. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me never give up on my friends.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Hopeful friends find a way to bring each other to Jesus!
