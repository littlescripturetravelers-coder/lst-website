The Lamb and the Dove
One day John the Baptist saw Jesus coming toward him. John called out, “Look! The Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world!”
Why a Lamb? Long ago, people gave Heavenly Father a perfect little lamb. It showed they were sorry for the wrong things they did.
Jesus is Heavenly Father’s perfect gift. He takes away our wrong things when we are sorry.
John told everyone what he had seen. The Holy Ghost came down from heaven like a dove and stayed on Jesus. That was the sign Heavenly Father had promised John.
So John spoke up: “This is the Son of God!” Tonight, tell someone you love Jesus, just like John did.

=== page 3 ===
Week 3 · Night 4 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Beau the Brave
Beau the Brave has a big heart and a brave start. John called out the truth about Jesus in front of everyone with a bold, strong voice, and that takes a brave heart.
In Beau’s world today: At the park, a kid says, “Praying is silly.” Beau’s cheeks get hot. He takes a breath and says kindly but strongly, “I pray every night, and Heavenly Father listens.” Then he goes back to playing, feeling quiet and strong inside.
Just like John, Beau learned that we can bravely and reverently say who Jesus is.

Week 3 · Night 4 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Fold your arms gently and bow your head, like you are in a quiet, holy place. Breathe softly. Can you show me your most reverent, still body?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   John saw Jesus and called Him the Lamb of Heavenly Father. The people felt reverence, quiet and holy.
NAME   That quiet, holy, hush-now feeling is called reverence. Your mind might think, “This is special and true.” Your body might feel still and soft.
FIND   When have you felt quiet and holy inside, like in a prayer or at church?
FORK   John could keep quiet, or boldly tell everyone who Jesus was. John spoke up.
CALM   Take one slow, quiet breath. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for sending Jesus, Your Son.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus is the Lamb Heavenly Father sent to take away our sins!
