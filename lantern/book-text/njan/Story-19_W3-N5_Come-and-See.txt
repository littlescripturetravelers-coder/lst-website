Week 3 · Night 5
Come and See
The next day John the Baptist stood with two friends who learned from him. One of the two was named Andrew.
Jesus walked by. John looked at Him and said, “Look! The Lamb of God!” The two friends heard him, and they followed Jesus down the road.
Jesus turned around and saw them following. He asked, “What are you looking for?”
They said, “Teacher, where do You live?” They wanted to know Him better. Jesus said, “Come and see.”
So they went with Him and saw where He lived. They stayed with Jesus the rest of that day. Jesus still says, “Come and see.” Tonight, tell Him one thing you want to learn about Him.

=== page 3 ===
Week 3 · Night 5 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Scout the Seeker says, “Look and see, what could it be?” When Jesus said, “Come and see,” Andrew followed right away. That is exactly what Scout would do.
In Scout’s world today: Scout’s teacher says, “I have something amazing in this box. Who wants to come and see?” Scout is the first one up. Inside is a butterfly just coming out of its cocoon. Scout stares and whispers, “I want to know everything about it.”
Just like Andrew, Scout learned that when Jesus says, “Come and see,” the best thing to do is follow.

Week 3 · Night 5 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Put your hand over your eyes like you are looking far away down a road. Now lean forward like you are following someone. Can you show me your come-and-see lean?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   Andrew and his friend followed Jesus and asked where He lived. They felt curious and eager to know Him.
NAME   That I-want-to-know-more, leaning-in feeling is called curious. Your mind might think, “What will I find?” Your body might feel your eyes get wide and your feet want to go.
FIND   When did you want to find out about something so much that you hurried to see?
FORK   Andrew could stay where he was, or follow Jesus down the road. He followed, and Jesus welcomed him.
CALM   Take a slow breath and let your body settle. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me come and see Jesus every day.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Jesus says, “Come and see,” and He is waiting for you to follow!

Parent Experience
Testimony
1. Share a time you accepted Jesus’s invitation to come and see, and what you found.
2. What have you learned about Jesus by spending time with Him in prayer and scripture?
3. Did a grandparent or ancestor ever invite someone to come and see the gospel? Tell what happened.
