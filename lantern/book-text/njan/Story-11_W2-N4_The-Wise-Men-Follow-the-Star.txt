Week 2 · Night 4
The Wise Men Follow the Star
Wise men in the east saw a new star. It meant a new King was born. They came a long way to worship Him.
King Herod heard about them and was upset. He wanted to be the only king. He said, “Find the child, then come tell me.” But Herod did not mean well.
The star went ahead and stopped over the house where Jesus was. The wise men were so happy! They bowed down and worshipped Him.
They gave Jesus treasures: gold, and sweet-smelling frankincense and myrrh. Then Heavenly Father warned them in a dream, so they went home another way.
The wise men gave Jesus their best. Tonight, give Jesus a gift too, like a kind word or a helping hand.

Week 2 · Night 4 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Scout the Seeker loves to look up and ask, “What could it be?” The wise men looked up, saw a new star, and followed it all the way to Jesus.
In Scout’s world today: Scout’s family goes on a treasure hunt in the park. He follows the clues, one by one. His heart races as he gets closer. Under the big tree he finds the prize, and he shouts, “I found it!”
Just like the wise men, Scout learned that when we keep seeking Jesus, we find Him.

Week 2 · Night 4 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Lie on your back and point up at the ceiling like you see a bright star. Follow it slowly with your finger across the sky. Can you show me where your star stops?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The wise men saw the star stop over the house. They felt excited and full of joy to find Jesus.
NAME   That bubbly, can’t-wait, almost-there feeling is called excited. Your mind might think, “We’re so close!” Your body might feel wiggly, with fast breath.
FIND   When were you so excited that you could not wait another minute?
FORK   The wise men could go back and tell Herod, or obey Heavenly Father’s dream. They obeyed and went home another way.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and let your wiggles settle. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, thank You for leading me.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
When we keep looking for Jesus, we find Him!
