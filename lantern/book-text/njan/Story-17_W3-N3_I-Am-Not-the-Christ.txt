I Am Not the Christ
John the Baptist taught by the Jordan River. He told people to repent. To repent is to stop doing wrong and come back to Heavenly Father.
Leaders in Jerusalem sent priests to ask him, “Who are you?” John told the truth. “I am not the Christ,” he said. He was not the promised Savior.
Isaiah had said a voice would call out in the wild, empty land. John said, “I am that voice.”
“Make the way ready for the Lord,” John said. “I baptize with water. But Someone much greater is coming.”
John said he was not good enough even to untie His shoes. He wanted people to look for Jesus. Tonight, be honest like John, and tell someone about Jesus.

=== page 3 ===
Week 3 · Night 3 · Traveler
Scripture Traveler of the Night
Scout the Seeker
Scout the Seeker loves to find out who people really are. The priests came seeking answers, and John told them the honest truth: “I am not the Christ. I point to Him.”
In Scout’s world today: Scout helps his big sister build a huge block tower. When Grandma says, “Scout, what a tower!” Scout says, “My sister did most of it. She’s really good at building.” His sister beams, and Scout feels glad inside.
Just like John, Scout learned that being humble means pointing to someone greater instead of to yourself.

Week 3 · Night 3 · Feeling · Remember
A Feeling Moment
An activity, then the Five Stones
1. MOVE & IMAGINE
Point your finger at yourself. Now slowly turn it and point up toward heaven, like John pointing to Jesus. Can you show me your pointing-to-Jesus finger?
2. THE FIVE STONES
SPOT   The priests asked John if he was the Christ. John said no. He felt humble, happy to point to Jesus.
NAME   That small-but-happy, let-someone-else-shine feeling is called humble. Your mind might think, “This isn’t about me.” Your body might feel quiet and calm.
FIND   When did you let someone else get the praise, and it felt good?
FORK   John could say he was important, or tell the truth and point to Jesus. John told the truth.
CALM   Breathe in slowly and let your shoulders rest. Whisper, “Heavenly Father, help me be humble like John.”
Remember This,
Sweet Boys & Girls
Being humble means pointing to Jesus instead of to ourselves!
