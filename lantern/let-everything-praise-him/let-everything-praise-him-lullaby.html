Tonight's Lullaby  ★  Week 1 · Night 7

The Whole World Sings

from tonight's story Let Everything Praise Him! · with Scout the Seeker
Common meter (8-6-8-6) — it also sings to any hymn tune you already know.
Verse 1

The sun and moon, the shining stars,
Sing praises up on high;
The mountains and the tallest trees
Reach up to touch the sky.

Chorus

Let everything that breathes sing praise,
Let every heart rejoice;
The whole world is a joyful song,
And I can add my voice!

Verse 2

The whales that swim, the birds that fly,
The bugs and lions too,
All say a thank-You to the One
Who made them, me, and you.

Chorus

(repeat chorus)

Verse 3

So clap your hands and stomp your feet,
Sing out with all your might;
Then whisper one soft thank-You note
To Him who made the night.

Chorus

(repeat chorus)

Verse 4

Now close your eyes, my little one,
The stars still sing above;
And every breath you breathe tonight
Is praise to Him, with love.

Chorus

(repeat chorus)
