## The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints edition

**Grace and agency work together.** The Savior's Atonement is the power that saves and changes us; we cannot save or
change ourselves. And we receive that power as we use our agency: we desire it, exercise faith, pray, repent, make and
keep covenants, and keep turning to Him. Never write that He does it all and nothing is asked of us, and never write
that we earn it or must be perfect first.
- A soft heart: we desire it, humble ourselves, pray and ask; as we turn to Him, He softens and changes the heart.
  1 Nephi 2:16 ("having great desires ... I did cry unto the Lord; and behold he did visit me, and did soften my
  heart"); Mosiah 5:2 (the mighty change, by the Spirit, after they believed and covenanted); Alma 5:14; Ether 12:27
  ("if men come unto me ... and they humble themselves ... then will I make weak things become strong");
  Moroni 10:32–33 ("deny yourselves of all ungodliness ... then is his grace sufficient for you"); 2 Nephi 25:23
  (grace "after all we can do": His grace is with us all along the way, not only at the end).
- Forgiveness of sin is offered to all and received through faith and repentance (Alma 34:15–17; D&C 58:42–43;
  Helaman 5:10–11: redeemed from their sins, not in them).
- Some gifts are unconditional: every person will be resurrected (Alma 11:42–44; 2 Nephi 9:22), and the Savior
  took upon Him our pains, sicknesses and infirmities so He knows how to help us (Alma 7:11–13). It is right to say
  He carries our pain and is with us in it; forgiveness and the changed heart still come as we come to Him.
- Enabling power: His grace also strengthens us to do what we cannot do alone (Bible Dictionary, "Grace").
- Heavenly Father's and the Savior's love is perfect and infinite; do not call it "unconditional" (President Nelson,
  "Divine Love", Feb 2003 Ensign: many of the blessings that flow from it are conditioned on obedience).
- The Godhead is three separate beings, one in purpose: Heavenly Father, His Son Jesus Christ, the Holy Ghost. No
  creedal Trinity wording ("one God in three persons", "one substance").
- Covenants and ordinances (baptism, the gift of the Holy Ghost, the sacrament, the temple) are how we bind ourselves
  to Him; the sacrament renews our covenants each week.
- Little children are alive in Christ and need no repentance (Moroni 8:8–12); accountability begins at eight
  (D&C 68:25–27). Teaching a child to say sorry and try again is learning to repent, not paying for sin.
- Words: Heavenly Father, the Savior, Jesus Christ, the Holy Ghost, covenants, repentance, agency, grace. Not "saved
  by grace alone", "faith alone", "accept Jesus as your personal Lord and Savior" as the whole of it.

## The Christian edition (for Christian families across churches)

**Saved by grace through faith, and a changed life follows.** Salvation and forgiveness are God's free gift through
Jesus' death and resurrection, received by faith, never earned (Ephesians 2:8–9; Romans 3:23–24; Titus 3:5). Good works
are the fruit of a changed heart, not the price of it (Ephesians 2:10; James 2:17). Never write that we earn God's love,
forgiveness or salvation, or that we must be good enough first.
- A soft heart: God gives the new heart (Ezekiel 36:26) by His Spirit; we are called to turn to Him, repent, pray and
  not harden our hearts (Hebrews 3:15; Psalm 51:10 "Create in me a clean heart, O God"). Say both: we turn and ask,
  and God does the changing. Philippians 2:12–13 ("work out your salvation ... for it is God which worketh in you").
- Forgiveness is complete for those who trust Christ (1 John 1:9; Romans 8:1); growth in holiness is a life-long walk
  with the Holy Spirit (2 Corinthians 3:18; Galatians 5:22–23).
- God is one God in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit. Jesus is fully God and fully man.
- Keep it to what Christians across churches share. Leave out what churches differ on: whether baptism saves, infant
  or believer's baptism, once saved always saved, the end times. Leave out what only Latter-day Saints teach: a life
  before birth, eternal marriage and temples, exaltation, ordinances as saving, Heavenly Parents, the Book of Mormon,
  modern prophets, "covenant path", "Holy Ghost" (say the Holy Spirit).
- Words: God, Jesus, the Holy Spirit, grace, faith, the gospel, the cross, repentance, God's Word.

## Both editions

- Never invent a scripture, a quotation, or a teaching; quote the verse when the point is fine.
- Jesus Christ is the Son of God, our Savior and Redeemer, who died for us and rose again.
- When a sentence could be read either way, write the true one plainly. A tired parent should never come away
  thinking "I don't have to do anything" or "I have to earn it."
