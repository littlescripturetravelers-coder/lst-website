# The brand kit

The pictures the marketing media maker (lantern/marketing_media.py) sends as
reference images whenever it has to generate a picture, so the characters stay
the same from one day to the next.

- travelers-cast.jpg - the four Travelers together (the app's own cast art)
- beau.jpg - Beau the Brave: boy, dark curly hair, brown tunic with rope belt
- scout.jpg - Scout the Seeker: boy, blond spiky hair, green tunic
- willow.jpg - Willow the Warm: girl, long red curly hair, purple headband, purple dress
- wren.jpg - Wren the Wonderer: girl, black curly hair, gold headband, coral-red dress
- presenter.png - the one real-looking presenter for parents-audience pieces;
  made once by the media maker and committed by the Action, then reused.
  Delete it to have a new presenter made (every later piece follows the new one).

THE PICTURE RULE: kids pieces are built from the night's own book illustrations
first (images/book/). Generation with these references is the fallback only.
To change how the Travelers are drawn, replace these files - nothing else.
