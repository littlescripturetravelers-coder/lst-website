# The environment and character locks

The owner's permanent locks for the rooms and people of the Little Scripture
Travelers world, kept word for word as the owner wrote them (one file each).
The marketing media maker (lantern/marketing_media.py) sends the SHORT version
of every lock a scene calls for whenever it has to generate a picture, on top
of the brand kit's reference images, so a room or a person never drifts.

- front-room.md      Grandma Lewis's Front Room (the family living room)
- grandma-room.md    Little Scripture Travelers Grandma's Room (the bedroom)
- beau-room.md       Beau's room
- scout-room.md      Scout's room (the faith-focused sports bedroom)
- willow-room.md     Willow's room (the lavender explorer loft)
- wren.md            Wren in pajamas, Wren's bedroom, and Wren's family (Mom and Dad)

How a lock is chosen: the scene text (the drafter's image_prompt) names the
place or the person - "Beau's room", "Grandma's room", "the front room",
"Wren in her pajamas", "Wren's mom" - and every matching lock is added. A
Traveler named together with bedroom / bedtime words gets that Traveler's room.
The style lock (warm premium cinematic 2D storybook, no realism, no 3D, no
anime, no style drift) is always added.
