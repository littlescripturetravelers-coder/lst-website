✦ STORY CATALOG ✦

The Virtue Library

Your child is standing in front of you with a real problem. Look up what they need tonight — and find the scripture story that teaches it.

✦

Knowledge is power.

When a hard moment arrives — the lie, the shoving match over a toy, the tears at the bedroom door — most of us reach for the same three or four stories we happen to remember. This library exists so you never have to teach from memory alone.

Every story in the Little Scripture Travelers series is tagged with the virtues and principles it teaches. Look up what your child is facing today, and you'll get every story you can use to teach it — straight from the scriptures, in a loving way, at a level a small child can actually hold.

But knowing is only the beginning. No catalog can tell you what your child's heart needs tonight. This is a tool, not a substitute — it has got to be a prayerful endeavor, worked by the parent, for each individual child. You are the one who knows them. Ask Heavenly Father, and then use what you find here to teach with.

Three complete stories are free. Browsing is open to everyone — and when you find one you want to read tonight, sign in with your email and we’ll send you a code. Three stories open right up, no card, no subscription.

✦ YOUR HALF OF THE LIBRARY ✦

Family Stories

The library above holds the scripture stories. This holds yours — and they are tagged with the same virtues, so they sit side by side. Look up courage and you find Daniel in the lions’ den and the great-grandfather who walked away from everything he owned.

Open your Family Stories

They are looked up by the same five doors as the stories above — by virtue, by life situation, by feeling, by traveler, by scripture — and everything you write there is in the app as well.

Built to Grow With Your Child

The library starts where the stories start — ages one through five. As the series grows through each stage of child and adolescent development, the same virtues will carry forward with stories written for who your child is becoming.
