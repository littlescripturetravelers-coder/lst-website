Your family app, to tap through

This is a working model of the app, built from the real app’s screens, colors and words. It uses your family as sample data. Nothing here touches the real app or your database.

  • Tap the tabs at the bottom, open ideas, check off reminders, and add your own.
  • Try More, then Settings, to change colors, fonts, dark mode and text size.
  • Take the love-languages quiz from the Family tab and watch the ideas change.

Want something changed? Tap Leave a note, then tap the part of the app you mean, and type what you want. Screenshots in our chat work too.

What you change here is kept on this device only. The real app gets your changes when Claude builds them in.
