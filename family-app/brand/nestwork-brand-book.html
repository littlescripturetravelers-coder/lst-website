What the logo means, the colors and fonts, how NestWork sounds, and how it sits under LST. NestWork wears the LST Parent Brand colors and fonts.

The idea

A home is a nest you keep weaving, and the light you keep inside it.

NestWork helps parents keep the whole family well: the marriage, the kids, the calendar, meals, money, health and faith. That work happens a strand at a time, every day. The logo shows it in two pictures.

The woven nest

Three woven bands for the marriage, the children and the home, each made of small daily strands. The rim holds them all together.

The eternal round

A light rises out of the nest inside an unbroken ring. A ring has no beginning and no end. It stands for eternity, the covenant of marriage, and a family that goes on together.

Family, well kept

“Well kept” means looked after, like a cared-for home. Read it again and it also means the family is kept well. Upkeep and wellness in three words.

Name

NestWork

Three meanings in one word. A nest is the home you build together. A network is the people woven into it. And nest work is the everyday upkeep of family life: meals, calendars, chores, date nights and faith. The capital W keeps both halves easy to read.

A search in October 2026 found no family app called NestWork. A Westfield State University campus tool uses the name, and Google sells smart-home products under “Nest”. Before launch, have a trademark search done (USPTO, class 9 for apps and class 44 for wellness services) and claim the App Store name when the listing is created.

App icon

Use it like this

  • Linen nest and gold light on navy is the main version. On linen or white, the nest is navy and the light is Frame Amber.
  • Keep the amber rule between the name and “FAMILY, WELL KEPT.”
  • Clear space equal to the height of the capital N on all four sides.
  • Smallest size: 24 px for the mark alone, 160 px wide for the lockup. Below 160 px, drop the tagline line rather than shrink it.
  • Always use the saved files instead of retyping the name.

Never do this

  • Don't add stars, sparkles or arcs.
  • Don't break or open the ring around the light.
  • Don't recolor, stretch, rotate or outline the logo.
  • Don't set the tagline in anything but Anton capitals.
  • Don't put the logo over a photo without a solid navy or linen plate.

How it sounds

Plain-spoken, warm and practical, like a trusted friend across the table who keeps the calendar straight and never makes you feel behind.

Dinner’s planned. The groceries are on the list.
You haven’t planned dinner yet!
Three ideas for Friday, all under $10.
Don’t forget to prioritize your marriage.
  • No guilt, no exclamation points, no emoji.
  • Short sentences that say what happens next.
  • Faith words follow each family’s own faith (for Latter-day Saint families, “Heavenly Father”).
  • Italics only for scripture.
Under LST

Part of the LST family of companies

NestWork wears the LST Parent Brand colors and fonts, so it sits naturally next to the parent line. The four Travelers and the children's badge never appear in NestWork: it speaks to parents.

Shows “An LST company”

The App Store seller line, the website footer, the About screen in Settings, press and printed pieces.

Leaves it off

The app icon, the everyday screens, and anything kids see. The full name “Little Scripture Travelers” stays out of the app.

Looks in the app

NestWork, with Coral one tap away

  1. NestWork is the default look: these colors and fonts, with the navy app icon and opening screen.
  2. Coral is the coral and white look, kept in Settings → Appearance → Theme for anyone who likes it.
  3. The logo inside the app follows whichever look is on.

Logo files are in the project at family-app/brand/lst/: the mark on navy and on linen, the lockup with the tagline, the name-only logo (one line and stacked), all as SVG, plus the 1024 × 1024 app icon. The coral versions are in family-app/brand/coral/.
