Family, well kept.

The brand book for the family app: what the logo means, the two colors, the fonts, how it sounds, and how it sits under LST.

The idea

A home is a nest you keep weaving, and the light you keep inside it.

NestWork helps parents keep the whole family well: the marriage, the kids, the calendar, meals, money, health and faith. That work happens a strand at a time, every day. The logo shows it in two pictures.

The woven nest

Three woven bands for the marriage, the children and the home, each made of small daily strands. The rim holds them all together.

The eternal round

A light rises out of the nest inside an unbroken ring. A ring has no beginning and no end. It stands for eternity, the covenant of marriage, and a family that goes on together.

Family, well kept

"Well kept" means looked after, like a cared-for home. Read it again and it also means the family is kept well. Upkeep and wellness in three words.

Name

NestWork

Three meanings in one word. A nest is the home you build together. A network is the people woven into it. And nest work is the everyday upkeep of family life: meals, calendars, chores, date nights and faith. The capital W keeps both halves easy to read.

A search in October 2026 found no family app called NestWork. A Westfield State University campus tool uses the name, and Google sells smart-home products under “Nest”. Before launch, have a trademark search done (USPTO, class 9 for apps and class 44 for wellness services) and claim the App Store name when the listing is created.

App icon

Use it like this

  • White on coral is the main version. On white, use the coral version.
  • Leave clear space around the logo at least as tall as the nest's rim on every side.
  • Smallest size: 24 px for the mark alone, 140 px wide for the mark with the name.
  • The name is Fraunces SemiBold with the soft setting, saved as outlines. Always use the saved files instead of retyping it.

Never do this

  • Don't use any color but coral and white in the logo.
  • Don't break or open the ring around the light.
  • Don't stretch, tilt, outline, or add shadows or glows.
  • Don't put the logo on photos or busy patterns.
  • Don't use Little Scripture Travelers colors, characters or fonts with it.

Fonts

An heirloom voice and an everyday hand

Fraunces · titles and the logo
Keep your family well

A warm serif with soft, rounded edges. It feels like a family Bible, a wedding invitation or a recipe card handed down, which suits a brand about family and forever. Use SemiBold 600 with the "soft" setting turned all the way up.

Figtree · everything else
Dinner at 6:00

A clean, friendly sans with open letters that stay easy to read on a phone, even at small sizes. It does the daily work: lists, menus, reminders and buttons. Regular 400 in charcoal for reading, Bold 700 for headlines, ExtraBold 800 for small capital labels.

Where it shows

The sign-in screen under the name, Settings → About, the App Store and website, the social covers, and printed pieces. Not on the icon or the everyday screens.

Under LST

Part of the LST family of companies

NestWork has its own look. LST appears as the line “An LST company” in Figtree ExtraBold capitals between two thin lines, in coral or white, never in LST colors or fonts.

Shows the LST line

The App Store seller line, the website footer, the About screen in Settings, press and printed pieces.

Leaves it off

The app icon, the everyday screens, and anything kids see. The full name “Little Scripture Travelers” stays out of the app.

Looks in the app

Coral, one tap away

  1. NestWork in the LST colors (navy, gold and linen) is the app’s default look.
  2. Coral is this coral and white look, in Settings → Appearance → Theme → Coral.
  3. The logo inside the app follows whichever look is on.

Coral logo files are in the project at family-app/brand/coral/: the mark on coral and on white, the name-only logo (one line and stacked, on coral and on white), all as SVG, plus the 1024 × 1024 app icon (PNG and SVG).
