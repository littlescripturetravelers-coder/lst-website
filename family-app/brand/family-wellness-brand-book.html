The brand book for the family app: what the logo means, the two colors, the fonts, how it sounds, and how it sits under LST.

The idea

A home is a nest you keep weaving, and the light you keep inside it.

Family Wellness helps parents keep the whole family well: the marriage, the kids, the calendar, meals, money, health and faith. That work happens a strand at a time, every day. The logo shows it in two pictures.

The woven nest

Three woven bands for the marriage, the children and the home, each made of small daily strands. The rim holds them all together.

The eternal round

A light rises out of the nest inside an unbroken ring. A ring has no beginning and no end. It stands for eternity, the covenant of marriage, and a family that goes on together.

Family, well kept

"Well kept" means looked after, like a cared-for home. Read it again and it also means the family is kept well. Upkeep and wellness in three words.

Name

Family Wellness

Plain and warm. Anyone can tell what it does from the name alone. A web search in October 2026 found no App Store app with this exact name. Before launch, run a trademark search (USPTO, class 9 for apps and class 44 for wellness services) and claim the App Store name when the listing is created.

App icon

Use it like this

  • White on coral is the main version. On white, use the coral version.
  • Leave clear space around the logo at least as tall as the nest's rim on every side.
  • Smallest size: 24 px for the mark alone, 140 px wide for the mark with the name.
  • The name is Fraunces SemiBold with the soft setting, saved as outlines. Always use the saved files instead of retyping it.

Never do this

  • Don't use any color but coral and white in the logo.
  • Don't break or open the ring around the light.
  • Don't stretch, tilt, outline, or add shadows or glows.
  • Don't put the logo on photos or busy patterns.
  • Don't use Little Scripture Travelers colors, characters or fonts with it.

A warm serif with soft, rounded edges. It feels like a family Bible, a wedding invitation or a recipe card handed down, which suits a brand about family and forever. Use SemiBold 600 with the "soft" setting turned all the way up.

A clean, friendly sans with open letters that stay easy to read on a phone, even at small sizes. It does the daily work: lists, menus, reminders and buttons. Medium 500 for reading, Bold 700 for headlines, ExtraBold 800 for small capital labels.

  • No guilt, no exclamation points, no emoji.

Under LST

Part of the LST family of companies

Family Wellness has its own look. LST appears as the line “An LST company” in Figtree ExtraBold capitals between two thin lines, in coral or white, never in LST colors or fonts.

Shows the LST line

The App Store seller line, the website footer, the About screen in Settings, press and printed pieces.

Leaves it off

The app icon, the everyday screens, and anything kids see. The full name “Little Scripture Travelers” stays out of the app.

Next steps

Bringing it into the app

  1. Add a “Family Wellness” theme in coral and white with these fonts, and make it the default for new families.
  2. Keep today’s look as a theme called “Classic” in Settings → Appearance, so you can switch back with one tap. It is also saved in the code history.
  3. Change the app’s name, icon and opening screen to Family Wellness.
  4. Run the trademark search, and claim the App Store name when the listing is created.

Logo files are in the project at family-app/brand/: the mark on coral and on white, the name-only logo (one line and stacked, on coral and on white), all as SVG, plus the 1024 × 1024 app icon (PNG and SVG).
