## Product rules
- **Privacy model (0012/0013):** family things are seen by the whole family; "Just for me" things are seen only by the person who made them, and nobody else, parents included.
  - `events`, `reminders`, `goals`, `think_sessions` have `owner_id` (defaults to `auth.uid()`) and `private`. RLS: family rows as before (goals/think are parents-only); private rows owner-only; inserting a private row needs `can_private()`.
  - `can_private()` is true for every parent, and for a child only when a parent turns on `family_members.private_ok` (Settings, the kid's profile). The `guard_member_switches` trigger stops anyone but a parent changing `private_ok`, `can_login`, `kid_sections` or `role`. Turning it off stops new private items; existing ones stay private.
  - Server functions that read these tables with the service role must filter `private.is.false` or `owner_id = caller` (see `todaySummary` in `_shared/voice-actions.ts` and `get_goals` in `advisor`).
- **Work and personal calendars** (`app/connections.tsx`, `src/lib/connections.ts`, `src/lib/ics.ts`): read on the phone only. Device calendars come through `expo-calendar` (work accounts are added in iPhone Settings > Apps > Calendar > Calendar Accounts). Task tools (Asana, Todoist, Trello, Jira, ClickUp, Monday…) come in as https/webcal feed links parsed with `ical.js`. Choices live in AsyncStorage (`outside-calendars`). Each source is "Only me", "Family sees Busy" or "Family sees details"; only the last two write start/end (plus the title for details) to `busy_blocks` for the next 14 days. The Calendar tab has "Everyone / Just me".
- **Calendar** (`app/tabs/calendar.tsx`, `src/components/calendar-views.tsx`, `src/hooks/use-events.ts`, `src/lib/calendar.ts`, `app/event.tsx`, `app/event-edit.tsx`): works like Apple Calendar. Day (week strip + hour grid), Week (7 columns), Month (grid + day list; tap the selected day again for Day view), Year; Today, search, swipe to move, touch-and-hold an empty time to add. Events have location, URL, notes, who, area, alert, repeat (daily, weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, yearly) with End Repeat; `exdates` and `repeat_until` (0014) give Apple's "Delete This Event Only / All Future Events". All-day events are stored at noon UTC of their date(s). Editing a repeat opens on the tapped day and shifts the whole series. `syncEventsToPhone` schedules alerts (ids start `ev-`) for the next 14 days and copies family events into the app's own Apple Calendar (map in AsyncStorage `apple-event-map`).
- **Marriage tab** (`app/tabs/marriage.tsx`): everything about the parents' relationship lives here (Morning Lantern couple study, quote and joke of the day, Coming up for us, love-languages ideas and quiz, date ideas, marriage goals). Levi wants it to look exactly like the rest of the app; new couple features go here in the same design system. Parents only:
  - Children's accounts never receive couple data (0015): events on the Marriage calendar (`events.couple`, generated from `area = 'marriage'`), the Date night reminder list (`reminder_lists.couple`, from `color = 'marriage'`) and its reminders (`list_is_couple()`), and `daily_ideas` of kind `couple`. Couple studies, goals and think sessions were already parents-only. The tab is hidden for kids (`href: null`), and `lantern.tsx` and `love-languages.tsx` redirect kids to `/kid`.
  - No Face ID lock on parents' phones: Levi asked only that it be left out of the kids' profiles.
- **Our list** (`app/our-list.tsx`, `src/hooks/use-wishes.ts`, `src/lib/wish-catalog.ts`, migration 0016): date nights, trips, home projects and intimacy. Each parent answers each idea Yes / Maybe / No thanks privately (`wish_votes`, own rows only). An idea shows under "We both want" only when both said yes, via `wish_matches()`, the only thing that reveals anything about the other person's answers. Anyone can add their own idea (`couple_wishes`; counts as their yes; only the adder can remove it, which clears its answers). Catalog keys are permanent.
