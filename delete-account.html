Delete your account

Little Scripture Travelers · for the iPhone app, the Android app and the website

You can delete your Little Scripture Travelers account at any time. Deleting it removes your account and everything saved with it, for good. It cannot be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Little Scripture Travelers app and sign in.
  2. Tap the round person button at the top right, then Account.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete My Account.
  4. Tap OK to confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email littlescripturetravelers@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We delete the account and everything saved with it within 7 days, and write back to tell you it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your name, email address and password.
  • Everything you saved: family stories, journal entries (with any places and voice memos), handwriting samples and fonts, photos, family books and printed-book orders.
  • Your notification settings and your phone’s notification token.

Nothing is kept afterwards, except where the law requires us to keep a record of a payment.

If you have a subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it there first, so you are not charged again:

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → The Lantern → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play Store → your picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Little Scripture Travelers → Cancel subscription.
  • Website: email us, or cancel from the link in your Payhip receipt.
