Bible stories for little children, not a lecture

Children learn far more from a story than from being told what to do. So every night tells one Bible moment, like Esther finding her courage, Daniel praying anyway, or Ruth choosing to stay, and then shows one of our four Travelers living the same lesson the next day at home or at school.

Every story points back to Jesus Christ and to a Heavenly Father who loves His children. Each night ends with something small to do together, so the story turns into a conversation.

For Christian families of every church

There is a Christian edition for families of any denomination, and a Latter-day Saint edition that follows Come, Follow Me. Same stories, same Travelers, with scripture references and study notes that fit your family.

Questions parents ask

What ages are these Bible stories for?

The stories are written for children ages three to eight, in the smallest words that still carry the meaning.

How long is each Bible story?

About ten quiet minutes. There is nothing to prepare and nothing to look up.

Is there a version for non-LDS Christian families?

Yes. The Christian edition is written for Christian families of any church.

How much does it cost?

The first 7 days are free. After that it is $6.99 a month or $49 a year, and you can cancel anytime.
