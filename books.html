✦ Inside Each Story ✦

Every nightly story is a four-page bedtime liturgy

Most children's bibles tell the story and stop there. Ours invite your child to feel the story, remember the lesson, and discuss it with you — across four carefully designed pages that make scripture a complete bedtime moment.

Week 2 · Night 1
Page 1 · The Anchor

The Travel Chest Opens

Each story begins with the four Travelers gathered around the glowing Travel Chest. Tonight's story title, scripture reference, and week night are introduced — a quiet, magical signal that bedtime scripture time has begun.

"Story 1 — Israel Wants a King" (1 Samuel 8)
Week 2 · Night 1 · Tonight's Story
Page 2 · The Story

Tonight's Scripture, Retold

The scripture story is told in gentle, child-friendly language across alternating illustrations and text. The Travelers appear within the scenes — walking beside Samuel, Esther, Nephi, or whoever the story belongs to that night.

"The prophet Samuel was getting old. The people came and said, 'We want a king like all the other nations have!'"
Week 2 · Night 1 · Feeling · Remember
Page 3 · Feel It · Remember It

A Feeling Moment + Remember This

A short "Move · Imagine · Connect" activity lets little hands and hearts experience the story. Then "Remember This, Sweet Boys & Girls" gently lands the lesson — turning scripture into a feeling your child can carry into sleep.

"Stand up tall like you're a king with a crown. Now bow down low. Close your eyes and think of three blessings you already have."
Week 2 · Night 2 · Prompt · Experience
Page 4 · Parent Connection

Parent Prompt + Parent Experience

A simple discussion question for the child, followed by an optional space for the parent to share a real-life testimony. This is where bedtime becomes generational — where your testimony enters your child's heart.

"Why did Israel want a king? Was this what Heavenly Father wanted?"

This four-page rhythm repeats 28 to 35 times per month, depending on how many nights are in the month. By the end of one year, your child will have journeyed through an entire volume of scripture — story by story, feeling by feeling, prayer by prayer.
